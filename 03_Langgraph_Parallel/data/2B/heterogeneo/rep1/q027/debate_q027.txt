1. **Power Efficiency:**
   - **Quasi-TEM:** Higher power efficiency as the wider beamwidth allows more efficient use of available frequency resources.
   - **Quasi-TE1:** Lower power efficiency due to narrower beamwidth, requiring higher transmit powers but potentially better spectral efficiency.

2. **Interference Management:**
   - **Quasi-TEM:** Easier interference management with reduced co-channel interference as the wider beam allows more isolation between beams.
   - **Quasi-TE1:** More complex interference management required due to narrower beamwidths, which can lead to increased co-channel interference.

3. **Spectral Utilization:**
   - **Quasi-TEM:** Higher spectral utilization efficiency as it maximizes bandwidth usage within the same transmit power constraints.
   - **Quasi-TE1:** Lower spectral utilization efficiency due to lower bandwidth efficiency in quasi-TE1 mode, leading to potential higher interference and lower throughput.
