  - Lower SNR reduces achievable data rates as modulation schemes require higher bandwidths.

**Filter Type Impact:**

- **Low-Pass Filters:** These are less effective at suppressing high-order harmonics like 19th, leading to more ACI and ISI.
- **Band-Pass Filters:** More effective in reducing overall spectral inefficiency but may not fully mitigate the 19th harmonic issue.

**Conclusion:**

While 19th harmonic suppression can improve power efficiency by reducing channel interference, it comes at a cost of lower SNR and reduced achievable data rates. The choice between low-pass and band-pass filters depends on specific channel characteristics and modulation schemes used in dense payload configurations. Careful consideration must be given to the trade-offs between these factors for optimal performance.

- **Selective Filtering:** Use combination of low-pass and band-pass filters to address both ACI from nonlinear effects and overall spectral efficiency.
