[DLB] In MPLS-based VPNs, bandwidth allocation is managed using label switching paths (LSPs), which provide a fixed path for traffic without requiring detailed route calculations. In contrast, IPsec-based VPNs require detailed routing and can be more expensive due to the need for complex route calculations and the overhead of IPsec security protocols. This can lead to higher latency and slower performance in terms of congestion management. However, IPsec provides strong security guarantees and is well-suited for applications requiring high QoS, such as critical data transmission in electric utility automation.

The key budget implications are in the overhead of the label switching mechanism in MPLS and the complexity of routing in IPsec.
