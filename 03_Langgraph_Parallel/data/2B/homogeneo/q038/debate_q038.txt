[DLB] Optimizing spectrum efficiency demands a nuanced approach considering beam allocation. The inherent challenge lies in the **inter-beam interference** caused by adjacent beams' overlapping signal footprints. 

To mitigate this, **optimal beamforming** is crucial.  
- **Wide beams** offer wider coverage but increased interference. 
- **Narrow beams** improve spectral efficiency but limit coverage. 
- **Dynamic beamforming** adjusts beamwidth based on traffic density.

Budgetary implications are clear: **narrow beams** require **higher transmit power** for the same coverage, increasing **system complexity and power consumption**.
