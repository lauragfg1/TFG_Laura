TOPIC: How does L-DACS integration impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
L-DACS integration can improve spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links by leveraging the L-Band spectrum.  L-DACS operates in the L-Band, offering a dedicated spectrum for aeronautical communication.  The use of L-DACS can reduce interference with other terrestrial communication systems, potentially leading to more efficient spectrum sharing in congested airspace.
