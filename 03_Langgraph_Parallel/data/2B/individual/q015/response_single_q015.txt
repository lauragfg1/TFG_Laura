TOPIC: How do distributed satellite constellations impact orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Distributed constellations increase the complexity of orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and interference mitigation.  The increased number of satellites necessitates more sophisticated inter-satellite interference mitigation techniques, such as orthogonal resource allocation and beam-forming.  This trade-off needs to be carefully considered to achieve optimal performance in terms of coverage and signal quality.
