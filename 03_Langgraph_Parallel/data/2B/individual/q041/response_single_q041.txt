TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between array size and radiation efficiency for SIW arrays in high-gain inter-satellite antennas?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Increasing array size (from 16 to 64 sub-arrays) leads to a decline in radiation efficiency, dropping from 70% to 27.5%. This decrease is attributed to the increased length of the SIW structures, resulting in higher insertion loss.
