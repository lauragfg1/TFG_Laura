TOPIC: What are the trade-offs in using additive manufacturing for CTS array fabrication versus traditional methods in terms of deployment cost and scalability?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
{
    "response": "Additive manufacturing (AM) offers advantages in cost reduction and scalability compared to traditional methods for CTS array fabrication. AM can achieve compact designs, reducing material consumption and manufacturing time, while also enabling complex shapes and internal geometries not feasible with conventional techniques. However, AM may face higher tooling costs for intricate designs and can be limited by material availability and resolution. The overall cost-benefit depends on the complexity of the design and production volume."
