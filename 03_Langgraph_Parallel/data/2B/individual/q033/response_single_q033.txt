TOPIC: How do GEO vs LEO satellite orbits affect latency and coverage in hybrid networks for rural substation monitoring?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
GEO satellites exhibit significantly higher latency (round trip delay) due to their geostationary orbit, exceeding the latency of terrestrial networks. LEO satellites, with lower orbital altitudes, offer drastically reduced latency, comparable to terrestrial networks. This reduction in latency directly translates to improved performance in rural substation monitoring with faster data transmission and reduced delays in communication.
