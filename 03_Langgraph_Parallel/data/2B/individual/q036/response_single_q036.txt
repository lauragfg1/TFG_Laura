TOPIC: What link budget trade-offs exist when balancing data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Satellite link budget trade-offs involve balancing data rates (e.g., bitrates) and reliability (e.g., signal-to-noise ratio) under adverse weather conditions. This is crucial for ensuring reliable communication between satellites.  Factors impacting reliability include antenna gain, modulation scheme (e.g., QPSK, 8PSK), coding techniques (e.g., Turbo codes), and link budget calculations. These factors directly affect the achievable data rate and link reliability under challenging environments.
