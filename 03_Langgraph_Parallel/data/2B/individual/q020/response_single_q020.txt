TOPIC: What beam switching strategies minimize setup times while maintaining spectrum efficiency for multi-satellite ground station operations?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Beam switching strategies should prioritize techniques like adaptive beamforming and dynamic beam allocation. These methods allow for real-time adjustments based on traffic demands and interference levels. Standards like the ITU-R M.1570-2 for multibeam satellite antenna systems and the IEEE 802.11ax standard for OFDMA can be applied for efficient spectrum sharing and power allocation.
