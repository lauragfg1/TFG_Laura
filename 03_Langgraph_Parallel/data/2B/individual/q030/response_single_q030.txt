TOPIC: How does defocused feed array placement optimize coverage versus scan loss in DBF architectures?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Defocused feed arrays in SATCOM architectures minimize scan loss by strategically placing elements to reduce signal scattering. This reduces the need for extensive beam scans, improving coverage and reducing unnecessary energy expenditure. This concept is particularly beneficial for challenging environments like those encountered with satellite communications.
