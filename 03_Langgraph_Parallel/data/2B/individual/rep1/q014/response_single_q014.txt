TOPIC: What antenna aperture size trade-offs exist between LEO and GEO satellites when optimizing gain and portability for CMOS-based ground terminals?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
LEO satellites have smaller aperture sizes compared to GEO satellites, leading to lower overall antenna gain. This tradeoff is driven by the proximity of LEO satellites to Earth and their higher orbital speeds, which influence signal propagation time delay and free space path loss (FSPL).  The context provides a User Antenna Gain (GR) value of 39.8 dBi for LEO, implying a smaller aperture size than GEO, typically in the range of 1-2 meters.
