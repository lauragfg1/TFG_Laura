TOPIC: How does the hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology balance efficiency and compactness in V-band satellite antenna designs?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
The hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology optimizes antenna performance by combining the advantages of both structures. It utilizes microstrip patches, substrate integrated waveguide (SIW), and waveguide power dividers to achieve a wideband characteristic. This approach minimizes insertion loss while maintaining a compact form factor, contributing to high efficiency and low cost.
