TOPIC: How do MPLS and IPsec VPNs differ in mitigating congestion for real-time QoS in multi-telectric utility backbones?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
MPLS and IPsec VPNs both use label switching (LSP) for path selection, but MPLS offers more precise QoS control by prioritizing packets based on DSCP fields. IPsec VPNs primarily focus on securing traffic with encryption, not guaranteeing specific latency or jitter values. IPsec relies on the network's best-effort service paradigm, which may not meet real-time QoS requirements like those found in electric utility backbones.
