ID: 31
TOPIC: Can MVDR beamforming sufficiently compensate for pattern degradation in array-fed reflectors during element failures?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
MVDR beamforming can partially compensate for pattern degradation in array-fed reflectors during element failures. The provided data indicates that MVDR beamforming reduces gain loss, but it doesn't entirely eliminate it.  The performance of the defocused reflector is comparable to the conventional reflector under failure conditions.
