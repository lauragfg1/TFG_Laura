TOPIC: How does the 11.6% bandwidth of the CTS antenna influence frequency planning and spectral efficiency for broadband satellite links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
The CTS antenna's 11.6% bandwidth directly impacts frequency planning and spectral efficiency by limiting the available bandwidth for each communication channel.  This reduced bandwidth can affect both uplink and downlink capacity, potentially necessitating more efficient modulation techniques or increased channel spacing to maintain desired throughput.
