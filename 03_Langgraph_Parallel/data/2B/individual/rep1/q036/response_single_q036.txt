TOPIC: What link budget trade-offs exist when balancing data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Satellite backhaul link budgets for high data rates face trade-offs between power and reliability in adverse weather. Atmospheric attenuation limits transmission, requiring higher power levels to support high modulation orders (e.g., 50W) to achieve desired throughput.  This necessitates careful design considerations to balance these factors for reliable communication in challenging environments.
