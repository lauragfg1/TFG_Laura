TOPIC: What antenna design considerations minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
To minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments, use directional antennas with low impedance and minimized physical size. This helps to reduce reflection and scattering, which are major contributors to signal loss.  Additionally, employing shielded cables for the transmission system can further mitigate interference.
