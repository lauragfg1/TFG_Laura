TOPIC: How does L-DACS integration impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
L-DACS integration aims to mitigate spectrum congestion in airspaces by providing dedicated, high-capacity data links for A2G communication. L-DACS operates within the L-band and uses techniques like FDMA (frequency division multiple access) and TDD (time division duplexing), enabling efficient sharing of frequencies for both terrestrial and satellite networks.
