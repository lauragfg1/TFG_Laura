TOPIC: What antenna design considerations optimize ground station visibility during short service windows in LEO constellations?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Antenna design considerations for ground station visibility in LEO constellations must optimize beamwidth and elevation angle to maximize line-of-sight. The Walker Star constellation layout, with an inclination of 87.5 degrees, is proposed for global coverage. This configuration ensures a wide field-of-view and minimizes the shadowing effect on satellite communication signals.
