TOPIC: How does transponder allocation balance network efficiency against thermal/power constraints during peak service demand periods?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Transponder allocation aims to balance network efficiency and power constraints during peak demand.  Dynamic bandwidth allocation, often based on pricing models, seeks to optimize traffic accommodation while adhering to thermal limits. This is typically achieved through techniques like channel capacity management and link-level prioritization, balancing data throughput and power usage. The exact methods employed depend on the specific satellite communication system and its network architecture.
