TOPIC: How does defocused feed array placement optimize coverage versus scan loss in DBF architectures?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Defocused feed array placement in satellite communication architectures improves coverage by reducing scan loss. The defocusment minimizes signal leakage and maximizes effective antenna gain, leading to better signal reception across a wider area. This reduces the need for extensive ground-based infrastructure compared to conventional reflectors.
