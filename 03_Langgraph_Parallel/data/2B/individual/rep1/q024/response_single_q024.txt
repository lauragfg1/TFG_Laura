TOPIC: What are the trade-offs in using additive manufacturing for CTS array fabrication versus traditional methods in terms of deployment cost and scalability?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Additive manufacturing (AM) offers significant cost and scalability advantages over traditional methods for CTS array fabrication. AM reduces size, weight, and production time, enabling complex designs previously unattainable with conventional techniques. While AM may require specialized tooling, it allows for the creation of monolithic structures that directly address the limitations of split block approaches. This results in reduced manufacturing costs and improved efficiency compared to traditional methods.
