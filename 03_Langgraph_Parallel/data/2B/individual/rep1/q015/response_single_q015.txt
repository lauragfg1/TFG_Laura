TOPIC: How do distributed satellite constellations impact orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Distributed satellite constellations, by leveraging multiple satellites in each orbital plane, can improve coverage. However, this increased density also introduces more inter-satellite interference. This tradeoff is primarily determined by the specific constellation topology and the chosen modulation scheme.  The higher the number of satellites, the greater the potential for interference.  However, proper selection of modulation schemes, antenna aperture size, and interference mitigation techniques can optimize performance.
