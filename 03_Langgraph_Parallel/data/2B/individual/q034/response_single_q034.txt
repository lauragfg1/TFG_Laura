TOPIC: What antenna design considerations minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
To minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power lines, use high-gain directional antennas with low-noise amplifiers.  Consider frequency-selective impedance matching and employing shielded cables to reduce electromagnetic interference.  Adherence to IEEE standards (e.g., 802.15.4) can optimize performance.
