Satellite link budgets can be optimized for high throughput and low latency in global aeronautical coverage by employing techniques such as dynamic resource allocation and adaptive modulation and coding. These methods consider the varying link conditions and user traffic demands to dynamically adjust transmit power, modulation schemes, and coding rates, ensuring optimal channel utilization and minimizing latency.  Furthermore, employing low-latency satellite communication technologies such as LEO and MEO satellites can further enhance throughput and latency performance.
