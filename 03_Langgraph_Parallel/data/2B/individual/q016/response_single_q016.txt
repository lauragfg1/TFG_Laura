TOPIC: What antenna design considerations optimize ground station visibility during short service windows in LEO constellations?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Optimizing ground station visibility in LEO requires a carefully chosen antenna pattern with a wide beamwidth (HBW) to maximize the coverage area.  The Walker star constellation layout, with an inclination of 87.5°, and a specific antenna pattern defined in TR 38.821 (for both S-band and Ka-band)  ensures global coverage.
