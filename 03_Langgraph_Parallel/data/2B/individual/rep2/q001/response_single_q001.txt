TOPIC: How does interleaved array element spacing affect coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression in Ka-band satellite links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Interleaved array element spacing in Ka-band satellite links impacts coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression through frequency reuse.  Adjacent beams experience interference, leading to signal degradation as per SINR. To mitigate this, operators employ sub-bands with disjoint frequencies (as per Fig. 2) or orthogonal polarizations for beam separation.
