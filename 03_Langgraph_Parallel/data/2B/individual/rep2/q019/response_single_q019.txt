TOPIC: How do on-board storage constraints influence payload power allocation between immediate and time-tagged service prioritization?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Immediate services require on-board storage capacity to store data packets before operation. Time-tagged services, which must be started after upload completion, may not exceed available on-board memory, thus influencing payload power allocation. This constraint limits the amount of power allocated to each service based on its priority.
