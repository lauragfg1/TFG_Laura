TOPIC: How can frequency planning address interference between satellite uplinks and broadband power line communication systems?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Frequency planning for satellite uplinks and broadband power line communication systems must employ techniques like channel separation, time division multiplexing (TDM), and frequency reuse to mitigate interference.  The required frequency bands for each system are defined by standards such as the ITU-R Recommendation F.104 for satellite communications.
