TOPIC: What beamforming strategies ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Consistent Line-of-Sight (LOS) connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations requires adaptive beamforming strategies. These strategies must consider factors like aircraft velocity, position, and direction of flight to optimize signal transmission paths. Techniques such as Distributed Beamforming can be used to achieve this.
