TOPIC: What are the trade-offs in using additive manufacturing for CTS array fabrication versus traditional methods in terms of deployment cost and scalability?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Additive Manufacturing (AM) offers potential cost and size advantages over traditional methods for CTS array fabrication. AM's monolithic design eliminates the need for complex assembly, potentially reducing manufacturing time and costs. However, AM's scalability is dependent on the complexity of the antenna design. Traditional methods may offer greater production volume capabilities but face limitations in achieving intricate designs like those found in high-performance antennas.
