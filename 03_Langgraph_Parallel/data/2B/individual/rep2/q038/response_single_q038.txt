TOPIC: What beam allocation strategies optimize spectrum efficiency in hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Optimizing spectrum efficiency in hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand requires dynamic beam allocation strategies.  These strategies must consider factors like intercell interference, user priority and signal diversity to allocate resources efficiently. This can be achieved through techniques like dynamic spectrum sharing and market-based mechanisms.
