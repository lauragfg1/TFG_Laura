TOPIC: How does adaptive modulation impact power margins in satellite transponders for fluctuating rural substation traffic?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Adaptive modulation techniques, like OFDM, reduce power margins by dynamically adjusting modulation and coding schemes based on varying signal levels. This minimizes power consumption while maintaining data rate performance for fluctuating traffic loads. The precise impact on power margins depends on the specific modulation scheme and its implementation within a satellite transponder.
