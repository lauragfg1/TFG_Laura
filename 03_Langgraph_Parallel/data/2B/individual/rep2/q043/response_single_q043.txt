TOPIC: What orbital configurations justify prioritizing V-band (57-66 GHz) over optical systems for inter-satellite communication?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Prioritizing V-band for inter-satellite communication requires overcoming limitations imposed by atmospheric attenuation and receiver sensitivity. The higher power efficiency, lower noise floor, and wider bandwidth offered by V-band compared to optical systems make them well-suited for long-distance communication at high data rates.
