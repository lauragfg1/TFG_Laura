TOPIC: How do GEO vs LEO satellite orbits affect latency and coverage in hybrid networks for rural substation monitoring?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
GEO satellites offer limited coverage and high latency due to their large orbital footprint. LEO satellites, with lower latency and broader coverage, are ideal for rural substation monitoring.  This is because the distance between a GEO satellite and a remote location can be significant, leading to substantial delays in communication.
