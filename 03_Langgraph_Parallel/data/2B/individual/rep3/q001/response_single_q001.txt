TOPIC: How does interleaved array element spacing affect coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression in Ka-band satellite links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Interleaved array element spacing in Ka-band satellite links can affect coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression.  The use of interleaved arrays typically leads to a reduction in beam steering accuracy, increasing the likelihood of interbeam interference and grating lobes. To mitigate this, frequency band separation and orthogonal polarization techniques are commonly employed.
