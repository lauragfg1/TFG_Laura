TOPIC: What antenna designs balance aerodynamic constraints with MIMO requirements for high-speed aircraft communication?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Achieving high-speed communication in aircraft with aerodynamic constraints necessitates antenna designs that balance size and weight while supporting multiple input/output (MIMO) capabilities.  This requires careful consideration of antenna array configurations, power handling, beamforming, and interference mitigation techniques.  The context suggests MIMO systems are a promising solution for aeronautical communications.
