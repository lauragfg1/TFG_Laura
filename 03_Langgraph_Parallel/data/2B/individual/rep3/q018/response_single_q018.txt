TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Overlapping satellite visibility windows create interference challenges as multiple ground stations attempt to communicate.  This can result in 'in line' interference between NGSO and GSO satellites, or even between satellites of different constellations operating on the same frequency.  Efficient spectrum management techniques are needed for effective coordination.
