TOPIC: How does adaptive modulation impact power margins in satellite transponders for fluctuating rural substation traffic?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Adaptive modulation techniques, like OFDM and QAM, directly impact power margins in satellite transponders.  By dynamically adjusting modulation and coding schemes based on signal strength and channel conditions, these techniques can optimize data rates while minimizing interference and power consumption.  This improves efficiency and reduces the overall power requirements of the transponder.
