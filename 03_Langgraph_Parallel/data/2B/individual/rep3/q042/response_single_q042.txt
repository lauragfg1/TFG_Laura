TOPIC: How does the 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna impact link budget calculations for GEO-LEO satellite links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Antenna efficiency directly impacts link budget calculations by influencing transmitted power.  The 41% efficiency reduces the output power available for transmission, resulting in lower signal strength and potential degradation of link quality. This reduction is a direct consequence of energy conversion losses.
