    "response": "Reflector antenna design tradeoffs exist between aperture size and feed element redundancy.  A larger aperture provides greater gain but necessitates more robust elements to ensure consistent performance under failure scenarios. The defocused reflector concept, with its increased robustness against individual or multiple feed element failures, offers significant advantage in this regard."
