TOPIC: What beam management strategies are needed to mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
To mitigate efficiency fluctuations in the 57-66 GHz band, implementing beamforming techniques with appropriate beamwidth and steering strategies is crucial. Further investigation into adaptive beamforming algorithms based on channel state information (CSI) can enhance performance.
