TOPIC: What are the trade-offs in using additive manufacturing for CTS array fabrication versus traditional methods in terms of deployment cost and scalability?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Additive manufacturing (SLA) offers design freedom and monolithic fabrication, potentially reducing size, weight, and cost compared to traditional methods. However, SLA's resolution and tolerances may introduce surface roughness impacting performance. Scalability for large-scale deployments is a concern, requiring further investigation.
