TOPIC: How do MPLS and IPsec VPNs differ in mitigating congestion for real-time QoS in multi-telectric utility backbones?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
MPLS provides QoS guarantees by managing available capacity, while IPsec VPNs rely on best-effort service. MPLS is preferred for large networks with strict QoS requirements due to its ability to prioritize traffic and reduce communication costs.
