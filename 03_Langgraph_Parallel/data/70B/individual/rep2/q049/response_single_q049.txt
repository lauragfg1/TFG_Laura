TOPIC: How does L-DACS integration impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
L-DACS integration mitigates saturation in A2G communication systems, potentially reducing interference with satellite links. L-DACS1 and L-DACS2 operate differently, with L-DACS1 offering full-duplex transmission and L-DACS2 using half-duplex.
