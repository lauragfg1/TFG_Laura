TOPIC: What power allocation trade-offs arise from differing scan losses between quasi-TEM and quasi-TE1 modes for multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Power allocation trade-offs arise due to differing scan losses between quasi-TEM and quasi-TE1 modes. Quasi-TE1 mode typically has lower scan loss, allowing for more efficient power allocation across beams.
