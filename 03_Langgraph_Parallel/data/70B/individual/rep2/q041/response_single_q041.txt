TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between array size and radiation efficiency for SIW arrays in high-gain inter-satellite antennas?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
As array size increases, radiation efficiency decreases due to higher insertion loss from longer SIW lengths. Efficiency drops drastically from 70% to 27.5% when array size increases from 16 to 32.
