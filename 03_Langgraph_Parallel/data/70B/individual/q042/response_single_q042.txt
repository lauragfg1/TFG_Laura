TOPIC: How does the 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna impact link budget calculations for GEO-LEO satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
The 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna reduces the effective gain, increasing the required transmit power to maintain the desired carrier-to-noise ratio (C/N). This impacts link budget calculations by decreasing the link margin, potentially affecting the overall system performance and reliability.
