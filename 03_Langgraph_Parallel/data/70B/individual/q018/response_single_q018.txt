TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Frequency planning challenges include interference, beam collision, and spectrum congestion. Coordinating ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility requires precise beam steering, frequency allocation, and power control to minimize interference and ensure efficient spectrum use, adhering to standards like FDMA, TDMA, or CDMA.
