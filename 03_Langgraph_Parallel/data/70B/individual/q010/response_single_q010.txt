TOPIC: How do interference mitigation techniques adapt when handling tunneled versus non-tunneled traffic in multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Interference mitigation techniques adapt by optimizing beamforming and power allocation for tunneled traffic, prioritizing signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR) and throughput. For non-tunneled traffic, they focus on minimizing co-channel interference and maximizing spectral efficiency, adhering to standards like ITU-R S.1431 and S.1325.
