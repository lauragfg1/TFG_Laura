TOPIC: How does the hierarchical classification framework impact satellite link budget margins when prioritizing QoS-sensitive traffic?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
The hierarchical classification framework impacts satellite link budget margins by prioritizing QoS-sensitive traffic, allocating sufficient bandwidth and minimizing latency. This is achieved through QoS-OSPF with RSVP, ensuring calculation of shortest-path with sufficient bandwidth and resources blocking along a calculated path.
