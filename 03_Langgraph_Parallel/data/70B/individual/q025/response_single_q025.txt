TOPIC: How does the 11.6% bandwidth of the CTS antenna influence frequency planning and spectral efficiency for broadband satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
The 11.6% bandwidth of the CTS antenna allows for flexible frequency planning, supporting various broadband satellite link configurations. It can accommodate a minimum bandwidth of 1 MHz for inter-satellite links, enhancing spectral efficiency.
