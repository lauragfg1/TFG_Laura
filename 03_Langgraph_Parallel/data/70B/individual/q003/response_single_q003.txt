TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between the filter's compact size and integration with phased array antennas for satellite coverage optimization?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Trade-offs exist between filter compactness and phased array antenna integration, affecting satellite coverage optimization. Compact filters (e.g., square root raised cosine with 0.25 roll-off) can be used, but may limit antenna directivity and beam steering, impacting link budget and achievable rate.
