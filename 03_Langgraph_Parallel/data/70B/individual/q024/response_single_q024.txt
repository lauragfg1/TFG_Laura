TOPIC: What are the trade-offs in using additive manufacturing for CTS array fabrication versus traditional methods in terms of deployment cost and scalability?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Additive manufacturing offers reduced deployment cost and increased scalability due to monolithic designs, but may introduce surface roughness affecting microwave performance. Traditional methods provide better control over thickness and impedance, but are limited by complexity and cost.
