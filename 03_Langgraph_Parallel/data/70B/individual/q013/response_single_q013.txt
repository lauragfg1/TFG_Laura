TOPIC: How does the transceiver's variable gain distribution between RF and baseband impact dynamic range and noise performance trade-offs in different satellite orbital configurations?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Variable gain distribution between RF and baseband affects dynamic range and noise performance. Increasing RF gain enhances sensitivity but may saturate the baseband, while increasing baseband gain reduces noise floor. Optimal distribution depends on satellite orbital configuration, noise figure, and signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) requirements.
