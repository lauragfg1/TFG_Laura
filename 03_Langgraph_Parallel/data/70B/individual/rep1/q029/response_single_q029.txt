TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between reflector aperture size and feed element redundancy in robust antenna design?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Reflector aperture size and feed element redundancy trade off between gain and robustness. Larger apertures increase gain, while redundancy enhances fault tolerance. Defocused reflectors maintain performance despite feed failures, but may have slightly reduced gain.
