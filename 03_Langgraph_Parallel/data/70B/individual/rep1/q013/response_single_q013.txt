TOPIC: How does the transceiver's variable gain distribution between RF and baseband impact dynamic range and noise performance trade-offs in different satellite orbital configurations?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Variable gain distribution between RF and baseband impacts dynamic range and noise performance trade-offs by adjusting signal levels, thus affecting SNR. In different satellite orbits, this adjustment is crucial due to varying path losses and interference.
