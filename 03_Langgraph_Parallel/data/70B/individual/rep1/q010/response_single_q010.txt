TOPIC: How do interference mitigation techniques adapt when handling tunneled versus non-tunneled traffic in multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Interference mitigation techniques adapt by optimizing beamforming and power allocation for tunneled traffic, focusing on capacity blocking and link status. For non-tunneled traffic, they prioritize signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR) improvement through frequency separation and orthogonal polarizations.
