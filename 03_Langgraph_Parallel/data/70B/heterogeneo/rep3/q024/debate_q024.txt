This impacts **deployment cost** as AM reduces part count and assembly labor but may require post-processing (e.g., copper plating) to compensate for surface roughness. Scalability is constrained by AM's resolution limits at higher frequencies, while traditional methods face bottlenecks in fabricating complex geometries.
