   - Higher frequency bands (e.g., Ka-band) offer more bandwidth but are more susceptible to atmospheric conditions, necessitating adaptive allocation strategies.

5. **Integration with Terrestrial Networks**:
   - Combining satellite and terrestrial networks allows for dynamic traffic routing based on ML insights, optimizing both spectral efficiency and QoS. However, this introduces challenges in managing latency and ensuring seamless handovers between networks.
