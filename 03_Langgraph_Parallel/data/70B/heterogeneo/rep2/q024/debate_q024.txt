Traditional methods offer superior surface finish and tighter tolerances, ensuring stable impedance matching and minimizing phase errors, which are critical for maintaining high gain and low sidelobes in satellite communications. The trade-off lies in AM's design freedom and scalability versus traditional methods' precision, impacting the system's overall performance budget.
