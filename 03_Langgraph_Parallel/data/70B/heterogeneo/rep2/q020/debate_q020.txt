[DLB] To optimize beam switching for multi-satellite ground stations while minimizing setup times and maintaining spectrum efficiency, consider the following strategies:

2. **Efficient Power Allocation**: Optimize power distribution across beams to minimize waste, ensuring power is directed where needed most, which supports both power minimization and spectrum efficiency.

3. **Advanced Beamforming Techniques**: Utilize precise beamforming to reduce interference and spill-over between beams, enhancing signal clarity and maintaining efficiency during switching.

4. **Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing (OFDM)**: Employ OFDM to maximize bandwidth utilization by efficiently managing frequencies, reducing interference, and optimizing data transmission rates.

These strategies collectively enhance resource allocation, allowing the system to handle increased traffic without compromising service quality or setup times.
