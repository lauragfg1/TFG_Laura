[DLB] Interleaving techniques can optimize satellite link budgets for high throughput and low latency in global aeronautical coverage by mitigating burst errors. This is achieved through several key mechanisms:

1. **Error Burst Mitigation**: By rearranging data, interleaving breaks up error bursts into smaller, manageable errors, enhancing the effectiveness of Forward Error Correction (FEC) codes.

2. **Complementary Functionality with FEC**: Interleaving reduces retransmissions by allowing FEC to handle errors more efficiently, thus lowering latency and improving throughput.

3. **Buffer Management Optimization**: Reduced retransmissions alleviate buffer congestion, ensuring smoother traffic flow and lower queuing delays.

4. **Synergy with Adaptive Modulation**: Maintains stable link quality under varying channel conditions, preventing drastic modulation changes that could affect throughput.

5. **Link Budget Efficiency**: Potentially reduces required power or SNR thresholds, optimizing overall link efficiency without significant processing overhead in high-latency environments.

6. **Implementation Considerations**: Selection of appropriate interleaver types (e.g., block vs. helical) and seamless integration with FEC mechanisms are crucial for effectiveness.

Overall, interleaving enhances error resilience, supports efficient traffic management, and ensures consistent performance across diverse regions, optimizing satellite link budgets effectively.
