MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The transceiver's variable gain distribution between RF and baseband impacts dynamic range and noise performance trade-offs in different satellite orbital configurations. As per the Shannon theorem, channel capacity can be increased by varying the signal-to-noise power ratio (SNR). Increasing antenna gain or decreasing system temperature can improve SNR, but these options are limited due to size constraints on small satellites. The Boltzmann constant and noise figure of the receiver influence the noise variance.
