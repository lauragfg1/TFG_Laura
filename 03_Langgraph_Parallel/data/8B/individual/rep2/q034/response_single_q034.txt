TOPIC: What antenna design considerations minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration, consider using directional antennas with a narrow beamwidth to reduce signal dispersion and interference. Antenna design should also incorporate noise-rejection techniques such as notch filtering or adaptive arrays to mitigate the effects of electrical motors, power supplies, fluorescent lights, and radio signal interferences.
