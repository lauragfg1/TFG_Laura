TOPIC: What beam management strategies are needed to mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band, adaptive beamforming techniques are necessary. This can be achieved through dynamic beam steering and shaping, utilizing phased arrays or smart antennas. The system should also employ real-time channel state information (CSI) feedback to adjust the beamforming weights and track changes in the propagation environment.
