TOPIC: How does the 45° scan range affect link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users compared to broadside beams?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 45° scan range affects link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users compared to broadside beams, as it introduces additional losses due to off-focus illumination of the beamformers. This results in a decrease in signal strength and an increase in noise temperature, impacting the overall signal-to-noise ratio (SNR). The quasi TE1 mode excitation leads to faster increase of side lobe levels with scan angle for v-polarized beams.
