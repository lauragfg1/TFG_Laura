TOPIC: How do interference mitigation techniques adapt when handling tunneled versus non-tunneled traffic in multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Interference mitigation techniques adapt by adjusting power allocation and beamforming to minimize interference between adjacent beams in multi-beam satellite systems. For tunneled traffic, techniques such as orthogonal polarizations or frequency band separation are employed. Non-tunneled traffic may require more aggressive interference mitigation due to its non-structured nature.
