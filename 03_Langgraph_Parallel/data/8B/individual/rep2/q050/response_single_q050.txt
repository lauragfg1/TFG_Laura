TOPIC: What beamforming strategies ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations, adaptive beamforming strategies that account for Rician distributed fading and spatial non-stationarity are required. Dynamic beamforming schemes, such as those using machine learning algorithms or optimization techniques, can adapt to the rapidly fluctuating frequency selective fading and channel conditions. These schemes should also consider the visibility regions where the channel energy is significant.
