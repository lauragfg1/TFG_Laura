TOPIC: What interference mitigation techniques are viable for overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations, viable interference mitigation techniques include adaptive frequency allocation, orthogonal frequency division multiple access (OFDMA), and rate splitting. These methods can be used to minimize mutual interference between satellite communications and terrestrial wireless communications.
