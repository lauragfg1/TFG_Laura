TOPIC: How do GEO vs LEO satellite orbits affect latency and coverage in hybrid networks for rural substation monitoring?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
GEO satellite orbits have larger delays due to their higher altitude, resulting in latency issues for real-time applications. In contrast, LEO satellites offer lower delay and pointing requirements, making them more suitable for rural substation monitoring. This is because LEO satellites are closer to the Earth's surface, reducing signal transmission time.
