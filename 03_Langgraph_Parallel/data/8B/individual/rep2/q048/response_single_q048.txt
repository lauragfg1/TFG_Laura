TOPIC: What antenna designs balance aerodynamic constraints with MIMO requirements for high-speed aircraft communication?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Large-scale MIMO systems with hundreds of antennas can provide high data rates for aeronautical communications. To balance aerodynamic constraints, aircraft typically have large airframes that can accommodate antenna arrays. A modular antenna design with an aperture size that can be configured by adding more modules is an interesting option to fulfill requirements as inter-satellite distance or transfer rate varies.
