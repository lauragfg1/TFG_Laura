TOPIC: How does the 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna impact link budget calculations for GEO-LEO satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna impacts link budget calculations by reducing the effective transmit power. Assuming a linear relationship, the actual transmit power would be 59% (1 - 0.41) of the calculated value. This reduction affects the carrier-to-noise ratio and overall system performance.
