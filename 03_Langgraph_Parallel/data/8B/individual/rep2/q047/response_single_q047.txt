TOPIC: How can satellite link budgets be optimized for high throughput and low latency in global aeronautical coverage?

==================================================
To optimize satellite link budgets for high throughput and low latency in global aeronautical coverage, consider using Multi-Packet Reception (MPR) techniques in Fixed Satellite Service (FSS). This can help increase data rates while minimizing latency. Additionally, utilize routing algorithms like Bandwidth Delay Satellite Routing [89] that take into account delay and bandwidth metrics. Regular link budget calculations should be performed to ensure optimal system performance.
