TOPIC: How does L-DACS integration impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
L-DACS integration may impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces due to its operation on L-band frequencies. The system's narrowband single-carrier design using TDD technique may lead to potential interference with other systems operating on the same frequency band, such as satellite communications. Proper coordination and management are required to avoid interference and ensure efficient spectrum sharing.
