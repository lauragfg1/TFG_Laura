TOPIC: What link budget trade-offs exist when balancing data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To balance data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links, trade-offs exist between transmission power, modulation order, and atmospheric attenuation. High modulation orders require higher transmission powers to compensate for increased errors due to atmospheric interference. However, high transmission powers are limited by the availability of power sources and potential damage from overheating. A balance must be struck between these competing factors.
