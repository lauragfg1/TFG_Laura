TOPIC: How does antenna design influence the accuracy of encrypted traffic classification systems in heterogeneous satellite networks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The accuracy of encrypted traffic classification systems in heterogeneous satellite networks is influenced by antenna design through the modulation and demodulation processes. The design should minimize signal distortion, ensure proper frequency stability, and optimize polarization matching to prevent interference. This requires consideration of factors such as beamwidth, gain, and radiation patterns.
