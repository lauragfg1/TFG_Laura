TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between reflector aperture size and feed element redundancy in robust antenna design?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In robust antenna design, increasing reflector aperture size can improve gain and directivity but may also increase spillover loss. Conversely, incorporating feed element redundancy can mitigate the impact of individual or multiple feed failures, as seen in the defocused reflector concept. However, this comes at the cost of increased complexity and potential taper losses.
