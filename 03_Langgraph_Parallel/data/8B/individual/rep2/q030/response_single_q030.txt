TOPIC: How does defocused feed array placement optimize coverage versus scan loss in DBF architectures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Defocused feed array placement optimizes coverage versus scan loss in DBF (Digital Beamforming) architectures by reducing gain drop in the center and minimizing significant gain loss in the direction of failed elements. This is achieved through a defocused reflector concept, which exhibits robustness when individual or multiple feed elements drop out.
