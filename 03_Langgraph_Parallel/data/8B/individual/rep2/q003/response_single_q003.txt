TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between the filter's compact size and integration with phased array antennas for satellite coverage optimization?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The filter's compact size and integration with phased array antennas for satellite coverage optimization involve trade-offs between directivity, beam steering, and angular coverage. A smaller filter may compromise directivity, while a larger filter may require more complex beam steering mechanisms. Planar antenna arrays can provide higher directivity but limit angular coverage to ±40 degrees.
