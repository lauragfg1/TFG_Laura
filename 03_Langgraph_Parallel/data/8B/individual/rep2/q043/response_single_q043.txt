TOPIC: What orbital configurations justify prioritizing V-band (57-66 GHz) over optical systems for inter-satellite communication?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Orbital configurations with large buffer sizes and high data rates justify prioritizing V-band (57-66 GHz) over optical systems for inter-satellite communication. This is because V-band offers a higher data rate capacity, up to hundreds of Gbps, without the limitations of spectrum availability.
