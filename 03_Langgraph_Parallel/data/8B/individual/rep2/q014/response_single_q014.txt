TOPIC: What antenna aperture size trade-offs exist between LEO and GEO satellites when optimizing gain and portability for CMOS-based ground terminals?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
LEO satellites have smaller antenna aperture sizes compared to GEO satellites due to their closer proximity to Earth, resulting in lower signal propagation time delay and reduced free space path loss. For CMOS-based ground terminals, a 74 cm size aperture antenna is used for LEO, while larger antennas are used for GEO. This trade-off optimizes gain and portability.
