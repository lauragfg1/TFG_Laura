TOPIC: How do on-board storage constraints influence payload power allocation between immediate and time-tagged service prioritization?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
On-board storage constraints influence payload power allocation by prioritizing immediate services over time-tagged services. Time-tagged services require data packets to be stored on the on-board memory between upload completion and operation starting times, which may lead to increased power consumption. To optimize power allocation, the system must balance the storage constraints with the power requirements of each service type, considering metrics such as packet size (lj), on-board memory buffer size (bs), and transmission power (pk).
