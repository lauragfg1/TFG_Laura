TOPIC: How does the 14.6% bandwidth affect frequency planning for multi-satellite networks with adjacent channel interference risks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 14.6% bandwidth affects frequency planning for multi-satellite networks by increasing the adjacent channel interference risks. This is because a smaller bandwidth increases the spectral efficiency, but also increases the susceptibility to adjacent channel interference. To mitigate this, a 10% guard band is recommended to ensure a safe margin between adjacent channels, as per the ITU-R Recommendation S.1424.
