TOPIC: How does the RA's 2-3 dB unit cell loss impact link budget margins for Ku-band satellite uplink and downlink?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The RA's 2-3 dB unit cell loss impacts link budget margins by reducing the available power for transmission. For Ku-band satellite uplink, this loss is significant due to the high atmospheric attenuation and limited transmitter power. The total loss of 8.46-8.9 dB (for 2-3 dB unit cell loss) and spillover loss of 4.95-5.655 dB will further reduce the link margin, making it challenging to achieve reliable transmission.
