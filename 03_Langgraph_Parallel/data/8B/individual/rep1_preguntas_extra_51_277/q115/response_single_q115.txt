TOPIC: What operational challenges arise in managing power distribution across hybrid satellite-aerial networks during peak congestion?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The operational challenges in managing power distribution across hybrid satellite-aerial networks during peak congestion arise from the need to balance energy efficiency and spectrum efficiency while adapting to time-variant traffic distributions. This requires dynamic power allocation, which can lead to increased complexity and latency. Additionally, high fixed latencies on links negatively impact QoS guarantees.
