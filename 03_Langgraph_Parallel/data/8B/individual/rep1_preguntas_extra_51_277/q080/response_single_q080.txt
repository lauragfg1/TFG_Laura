TOPIC: What challenges arise in maintaining link budget margins for massive beamforming with non-stationary spatial channels in XL-MIMO systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The challenges in maintaining link budget margins for massive beamforming with non-stationary spatial channels in XL-MIMO systems arise due to the difficulty in separating signals coming from widely different directions, leading to interference and the need for distributed or hierarchical interference-rejecting precoding/combining schemes. Additionally, beam squint effects occur with large arrays, causing frequency-selective beamforming.
