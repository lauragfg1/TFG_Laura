TOPIC: How can beam radius affect root sequence selection and cyclic shift offsets to minimize timing advance estimation errors?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Beam radius affects root sequence selection and cyclic shift offsets by influencing the delay spread (δtrt dmax) in the beam. To minimize timing advance estimation errors, the cyclic shift offset (ncs) must be carefully selected to accommodate all possible time uncertainties within the beam. A lower bound for ncs is expressed as ncs ≥ (δtrt dmax + τmax)nz/tseq, where nz is the sequence length and tseq is the sequence duration.
