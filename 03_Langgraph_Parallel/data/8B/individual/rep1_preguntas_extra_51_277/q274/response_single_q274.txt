TOPIC: What adaptive coding strategies compensate for non-Gaussian noise statistics in reconciliation phases of CV-QKD over turbulent channels?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Multi-dimensional reconciliation via multi-edge LDPC codes is considered suitable for CV-QKD over satellite channels with low SNRs. Non-deterministic noiseless linear amplification can also be applied to distill improved CV entanglement and enhance QKD performance.
