TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between increasing digital channelizer ports and maintaining feasible antenna design in hybrid transponders?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Increasing digital channelizer ports in hybrid transponders improves flexibility and traffic accommodation rate, but may compromise antenna design feasibility due to increased complexity and signal processing requirements. A larger number of ports requires more complex channel allocation algorithms, potentially leading to increased latency and reduced system robustness.
