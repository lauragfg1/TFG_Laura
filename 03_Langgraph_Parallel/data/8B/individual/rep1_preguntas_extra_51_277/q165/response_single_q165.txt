TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise when deploying ECRA with MRC in multi-beam satellite architectures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
When deploying ECRA with MRC in multi-beam satellite architectures, frequency planning challenges arise due to the need for precise synchronization and coordination between multiple beams. The use of multiple replicas (MRC) increases the complexity of frequency planning, as it requires careful management of interference and signal overlap between beams.
