TOPIC: What congestion control mechanisms prevent entanglement swapping failures in satellite relay architectures during multi-user CV-QKD operations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To prevent entanglement swapping failures in satellite relay architectures during multi-user CV-QKD operations, congestion control mechanisms such as Adaptive Modulation and Coding (AMC), Link Adaptation (LA), and Hybrid Automatic Repeat Request (HARQ) can be employed. These mechanisms help manage the trade-off between spectral efficiency and reliability to mitigate the effects of long transmission delays and packet losses in satellite links.
