TOPIC: How does temporal variability in satellite EIRP per beam impact radar detection consistency across different sea states?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Temporal variability in satellite EIRP per beam impacts radar detection consistency across different sea states by affecting signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and clutter levels. As EIRP varies, so does the power density on the target, leading to fluctuations in SNR. In high sea states, multipath effects exacerbate these variations, making consistent detection more challenging.
