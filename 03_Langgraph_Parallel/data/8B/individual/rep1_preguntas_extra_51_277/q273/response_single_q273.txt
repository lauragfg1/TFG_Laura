TOPIC: How do thermal noise characteristics of LEO satellite payload subsystems degrade squeezed state fidelity in continuous variable protocols?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Thermal noise characteristics of LEO satellite payload subsystems degrade squeezed state fidelity in continuous variable protocols by introducing Gaussian noise, which reduces the variance of one quadrature while increasing the variance of its conjugate.
