TOPIC: How does statistical CSI-based precoding affect margin requirements in LEO link budgets compared to terrestrial systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Statistical CSI-based precoding in LEO satellite communications reduces margin requirements by leveraging slowly varying channel statistics, which are less affected by small-scale fading and rain fading. This approach allows for more accurate estimation of channel direction vectors and average power, enabling optimized transmissions and minimizing the need for excessive margins.
