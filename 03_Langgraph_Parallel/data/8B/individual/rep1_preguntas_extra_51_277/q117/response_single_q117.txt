TOPIC: What aperture size trade-offs optimize optical terminals considering Fried parameter and power limitations in ground-to-GEO links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Optical terminals in ground-to-GEO links are optimized by balancing aperture size to minimize Fried parameter (r0) and power limitations. A larger aperture reduces r0, but increases power requirements. Considering the optical peak power of 0.5W, a compromise is reached with an aperture diameter of few centimeters, similar to that used in optical ground-to-space links.
