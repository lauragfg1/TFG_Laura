TOPIC: What trade-offs exist in designing multi-beam antennas to optimize coverage versus frequency reuse efficiency?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Designing multi-beam antennas to optimize coverage versus frequency reuse efficiency involves trade-offs between contiguous coverage, spectral efficiency, and interference mitigation. Increasing the number of beams can lead to higher gains and frequency reuse, but may result in increased complexity, lower aperture efficiency, and potential for inter-cell interference.
