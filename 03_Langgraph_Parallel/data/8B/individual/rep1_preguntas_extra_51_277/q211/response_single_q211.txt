TOPIC: How do Doppler effects in LEO satellite systems impact UAV link budget calculations for high-mobility operations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In LEO satellite systems, Doppler effects introduce significant frequency shifts due to the relative motion between the satellite and user terminals. This can impact UAV link budget calculations for high-mobility operations by causing variations in signal frequency, leading to potential errors in resource allocation and synchronization. The Doppler spread limits the UAV speed, affecting data link performance.
