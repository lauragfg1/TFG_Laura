TOPIC: How does non-optimal switching threshold selection impact end-to-end link KPIs like outage probability and latency?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Non-optimal switching threshold selection can lead to increased outage probability and latency in end-to-end links. When the threshold is overestimated, the system may switch too frequently, causing unnecessary overhead and potentially leading to packet loss or delay. This can result in decreased network reliability and overall performance.
