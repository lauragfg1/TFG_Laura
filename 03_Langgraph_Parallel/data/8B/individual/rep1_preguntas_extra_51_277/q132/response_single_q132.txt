TOPIC: How does dynamic allocation of active communication channels affect coherent integration and range resolution in multi-band stitching?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Dynamic allocation of active communication channels affects coherent integration and range resolution in multi-band stitching by introducing uncertainties in signal amplitude, phase, and frequency. This can lead to a decrease in signal coherence and an increase in noise floor, ultimately degrading the range resolution. The variability in channel powers, phase discontinuities, and non-adjacency of bands can further complicate the processing.
