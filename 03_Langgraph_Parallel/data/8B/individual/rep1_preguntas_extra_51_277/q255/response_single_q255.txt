TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between subcarrier interval sizing for CFO robustness and frequency planning in multi-beam architectures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In multi-beam architectures, subcarrier interval sizing for CFO robustness and frequency planning involve trade-offs. Larger subcarrier intervals (e.g., δfra = 1.25 kHz) can mitigate CFO effects but may lead to increased bandwidth usage and reduced spectral efficiency. Smaller intervals (e.g., δfra = 2fdmax) offer better spectral efficiency but are more susceptible to CFO-induced errors.
