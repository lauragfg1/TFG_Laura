TOPIC: What operational challenges arise from intermodal dispersion in TEM/TE1 feeding networks during wideband payload operations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Intermodal dispersion in TEM/TE1 feeding networks during wideband payload operations leads to signal attenuation, distortion, and timing jitter. This arises due to differences in propagation velocities between coaxial cables (TEM) and optical fibers (TE1). The high bandwidth requirements of wideband payloads exacerbate these effects, necessitating careful design and optimization of the intermodal interfaces.
