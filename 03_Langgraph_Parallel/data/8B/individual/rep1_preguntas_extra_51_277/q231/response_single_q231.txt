TOPIC: How does full frequency reuse in multibeam satellites impact link budget trade-offs between co-channel interference and spectral efficiency?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Full frequency reuse in multibeam satellites increases spectral efficiency by reusing frequency resources across neighboring beams. However, it also introduces co-channel interference, which degrades system performance. The link budget trade-off between co-channel interference and spectral efficiency is critical, as excessive interference can negate the benefits of increased spectral efficiency.
