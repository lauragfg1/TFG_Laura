TOPIC: What sustainable engineering strategies are essential to balance the scalability of LEO satellite constellations with the urgent need to address orbital debris accumulation?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To balance LEO satellite constellation scalability with orbital debris accumulation, essential sustainable engineering strategies include: (1) designing inclined orbits to reduce collision risks; (2) implementing debris mitigation technologies, such as drag sails or de-orbiting systems; and (3) adopting responsible satellite disposal practices. Additionally, incorporating space-based radar concepts for debris detection can enhance risk management.
