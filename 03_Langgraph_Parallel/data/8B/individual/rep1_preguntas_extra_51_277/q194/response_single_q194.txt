TOPIC: What challenges arise in antenna design for gateways to handle rapid switching and maintain phase coherence in multi-GW configurations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Antenna design for gateways in multi-GW configurations faces challenges in maintaining phase coherence during rapid switching. The Context implies that channel phase information is required for techniques like equal gain combining (EGC) and maximum ratio combining (MRC). However, these methods demand challenging synchronization processes to ensure accurate phase alignment between multiple gateway antennas.
