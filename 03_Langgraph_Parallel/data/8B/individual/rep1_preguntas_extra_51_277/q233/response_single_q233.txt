TOPIC: How do phase noise and outdated CSI from long round-trip times influence robust precoding design for link budget reliability?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Phase noise and outdated Channel State Information (CSI) due to long round-trip times significantly impact robust precoding design for link budget reliability. Imperfect CSI leads to inaccurate precoding matrices, causing signal-to-interference-and-noise ratio (SINR) degradation. Robust beamforming methods, such as max-min optimization under bounded uncertainty, can counteract this effect. However, the long round-trip times in satellite systems necessitate consideration of phase noise and outdated CSI in precoder design to ensure link budget reliability.
