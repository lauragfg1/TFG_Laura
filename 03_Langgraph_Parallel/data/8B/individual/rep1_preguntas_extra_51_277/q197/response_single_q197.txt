TOPIC: What beamforming strategies optimally balance implementation complexity and SINR enhancement when satellite transmitters employ MRT despite imperfect CSI?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To balance implementation complexity and SINR enhancement when satellite transmitters employ MRT despite imperfect CSI, robust beamforming strategies can be employed. Specifically, the Determined Bounded Model or Random Model can be used to account for CSI imperfections. Additionally, techniques such as Max Min optimization under bounded uncertainty can be applied to maximize worst-case received SINR.
