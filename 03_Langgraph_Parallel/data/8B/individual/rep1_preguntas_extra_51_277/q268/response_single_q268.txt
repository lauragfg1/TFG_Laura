TOPIC: How does the polarization preservation fidelity of satellite-to-ground optical links influence continuous variable quantum key distribution protocol selection?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The polarization preservation fidelity of satellite-to-ground optical links affects continuous variable quantum key distribution (CV-QKD) protocol selection by introducing background noise and affecting entanglement distribution. CV-QKD is more robust to background light, but its performance can be degraded due to polarization fluctuations. The choice between DV-QKD and CV-QKD depends on the specific requirements of the satellite-to-ground link.
