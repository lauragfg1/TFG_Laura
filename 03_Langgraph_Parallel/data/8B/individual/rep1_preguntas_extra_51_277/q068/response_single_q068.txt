TOPIC: How can beamforming and transponder allocation adapt to dynamic Doppler shifts in LEO mega-constellations for SC-FDMA uplinks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To adapt to dynamic Doppler shifts in LEO mega-constellations for SC-FDMA uplinks, beamforming and transponder allocation can be dynamically adjusted based on the estimated Doppler shift. This can be achieved through real-time monitoring of user terminal velocities and satellite positions. The frequency bandwidths for feeder links (BW_i_FD) and user links (BW_i_UD) can also be adjusted accordingly to minimize frequency synchronisation errors.
