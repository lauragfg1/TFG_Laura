TOPIC: How does full frequency reuse impact inter-beam interference margins in link budget calculations for high SE systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Full frequency reuse (FFR) leads to significant inter-beam interference, also called co-channel interference (CCI), which moves the power-limited link budget to an interference-limited regime. Users located at the edges of the beams suffer from severe CCI and experience a strong degradation of their achievable carrier-to-interference-plus-noise power ratio (CINR).
