TOPIC: How do PD and PR cell selections impact link budget margins considering their axial ratio and bandwidth trade-offs?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The selection between PD and PR cells impacts link budget margins due to their axial ratio and bandwidth trade-offs. PD cells provide a wider transmission coefficient bandwidth but are limited by the feed's axial ratio, whereas PR cells offer narrower operation bandwidths but can achieve similar gain bandwidths due to polarization performance.
