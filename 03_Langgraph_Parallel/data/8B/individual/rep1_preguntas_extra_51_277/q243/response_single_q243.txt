TOPIC: How do GEO satellite propagation delays influence link budget margins for latency-sensitive applications?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
GEO satellite propagation delays significantly impact link budget margins for latency-sensitive applications due to the high fixed latency (~ few hundred milliseconds) caused by signal propagation at approximately 36,000 km. This additional delay negatively affects Quality of Service (QoS) and applications not tolerant against high latencies.
