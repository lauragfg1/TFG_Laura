TOPIC: How can advancements in satellite miniaturization and reusable launch vehicles bridge the cost gap between LEO "round dance" systems and GEO constellations while mitigating space debris risks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Advancements in satellite miniaturization can reduce the cost per unit, potentially closing the cost gap between LEO 'round dance' systems and GEO constellations. Reusable launch vehicles with higher payload capacity can also decrease deployment costs. However, space debris risks remain a concern due to the increased number of satellites in LEO orbits. Mitigation strategies include implementing collision avoidance maneuvers and responsible satellite disposal methods.
