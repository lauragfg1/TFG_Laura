TOPIC: How do orbital altitude and inclination choices in GNSS constellations balance global coverage versus signal power margins?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Orbital altitude and inclination choices in GNSS constellations balance global coverage versus signal power margins by trading off between higher altitudes providing larger coverage areas per satellite but requiring lower inclinations to reach high latitude target points, and lower altitudes allowing for more inclined orbits to reach polar regions but resulting in faster satellite motion and increased Doppler frequency offset.
