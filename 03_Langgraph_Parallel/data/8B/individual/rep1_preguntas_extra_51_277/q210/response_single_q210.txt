TOPIC: What antenna designs optimize SWaP constraints while maintaining link margins for small UAVs in Ku/Ka bands?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For small UAVs in Ku/Ka bands, low-profile broadband antennas with high gain and narrow beamwidth are suitable. Designs that optimize SWaP constraints include patch antennas, microstrip antennas, and reflector antennas. These designs should consider frequency band, modulation techniques, antenna size, gain, and interference effects to maintain link margins.
