TOPIC: What antenna design considerations address coverage challenges for GEO satellites serving fixed RNs with ultra-high path losses?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For GEO satellites serving fixed RNs with ultra-high path losses, antenna design considerations should focus on increasing the gain-to-noise ratio. This can be achieved through the use of high-gain antennas with a large beamwidth to ensure coverage of the entire service area. Additionally, adaptive antenna systems or smart antennas that can adjust their radiation pattern in real-time may also help mitigate ultra-high path losses.
