TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between antenna sidelobe suppression and coverage area granularity when designing for high frequency reuse?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In designing for high frequency reuse, antenna sidelobe suppression and coverage area granularity present trade-offs due to the need to balance interference mitigation with beamforming efficiency. Increasing sidelobe suppression can reduce inter-beam interference but may lead to reduced coverage area granularity, as beams become narrower and more directional.
