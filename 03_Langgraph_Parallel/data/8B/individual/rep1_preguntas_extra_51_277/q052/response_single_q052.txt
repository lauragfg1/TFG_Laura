TOPIC: What interference mitigation techniques are critical for TDMA-based MAC protocols in high-density airport scenarios?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For TDMA-based MAC protocols in high-density airport scenarios, critical interference mitigation techniques include reducing transmission rates to minimize interference effects, using dedicated channels, and adaptive allocation of frequencies with variable frequency reuse factors. Additionally, sophisticated scheduling for channel access can help mitigate multiple access interference.
