TOPIC: Are existing link budget margins sufficient to handle SINR degradation from accumulated inter-beam interference in full frequency reuse scenarios?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Existing link budget margins are unlikely to be sufficient to handle SINR degradation from accumulated inter-beam interference in full frequency reuse scenarios. Inter-beam interference can significantly degrade the achievable carrier-to-interference plus noise power ratio (CINR), moving the link budget from a power-limited regime to an interference-limited regime.
