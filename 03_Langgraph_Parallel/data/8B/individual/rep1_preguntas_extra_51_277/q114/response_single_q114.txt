TOPIC: How does transponder granularity impact spectral efficiency in multi-beam payloads serving fluctuating rural user demand?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Transponder granularity impacts spectral efficiency in multi-beam payloads serving fluctuating rural user demand by affecting the ability to dynamically allocate resources. Coarser transponder granularities may lead to underutilization of spectrum due to fixed allocation, while finer granularities enable more flexible resource management, potentially improving spectral efficiency.
