TOPIC: How do elevation-dependent beam divergence and pointing losses influence satellite coverage area for optical ground stations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Elevation-dependent beam divergence and pointing losses reduce satellite coverage area for optical ground stations by increasing signal loss due to atmospheric disturbances, particularly at lower elevations. The availability of the optical link is influenced by local cloud cover and path-integrated aerosol content, leading to reduced data rates and increased pointing uncertainties.
