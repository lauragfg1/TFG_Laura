TOPIC: What operational challenges arise from mechanical beam scanning versus phased arrays for dynamic traffic in multibeam LEO networks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Mechanical beam scanning in multibeam LEO networks is prone to operational challenges such as increased power consumption, thermal issues, and mechanical wear. In contrast, phased arrays offer faster beam steering with lower latency, but require accurate and fast estimation of angle of arrival (AOA) for optimal performance.
