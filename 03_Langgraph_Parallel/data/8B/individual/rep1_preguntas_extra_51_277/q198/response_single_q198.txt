TOPIC: How should frequency reuse ratios be adjusted in hybrid networks to mitigate co-channel interference from terrestrial systems while maintaining satellite link coverage?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To mitigate co-channel interference from terrestrial systems while maintaining satellite link coverage, frequency reuse ratios should be adjusted by considering the specific characteristics of the hybrid network. The Context mentions that in full frequency reuse scenarios, the useful signal power at the receiver is greatly reduced due to intra-system interferences. Therefore, a compromise between frequency reuse and interference mitigation is necessary.
