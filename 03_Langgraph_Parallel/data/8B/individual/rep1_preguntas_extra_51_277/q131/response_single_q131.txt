TOPIC: What antenna design trade-offs optimize link budget for passive radar SNR while maintaining coverage in maritime scenarios?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To optimize link budget for passive radar SNR in maritime scenarios, consider increasing antenna gain while minimizing beamwidth to reduce interference. Use a phased array design with adaptive beamforming to steer and shape the radiation pattern. Select an antenna with high efficiency (η > 0.6) and low noise temperature (Tn < 100 K). Ensure sufficient transmitter power (Pt > 10 dBm/m²) and consider using a frequency band with minimal atmospheric absorption.
