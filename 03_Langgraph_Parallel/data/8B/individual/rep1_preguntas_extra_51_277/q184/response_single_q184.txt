TOPIC: How does the Stackelberg game's interference pricing model optimize power and spectrum efficiency across dynamic network loads?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The Stackelberg game's interference pricing model optimizes power and spectrum efficiency by dynamically adjusting the interference price based on the satellite system's profit and user's power allocation. This approach maximizes cost efficiency for the satellite users while balancing inter-cell interference, thereby improving overall network performance.
