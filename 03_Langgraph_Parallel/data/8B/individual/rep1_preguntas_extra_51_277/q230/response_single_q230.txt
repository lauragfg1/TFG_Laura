TOPIC: What operational challenges arise when balancing QoS constraints and EE via SCA-based precoding in dense user scenarios?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In dense user scenarios, SCA-based precoding may lead to operational challenges in balancing QoS constraints and EE due to increased interference, reduced signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), and higher computational complexity. This can result in decreased system capacity, increased latency, and reduced overall network performance.
