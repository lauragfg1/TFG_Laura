TOPIC: How does the limited 350-500 MHz gain bandwidth affect frequency planning for wideband Ku-band satellite transponders?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The limited gain bandwidth in Ku-band satellite transponders affects frequency planning by restricting the available spectrum for wideband operations. The reduced bandwidth necessitates more efficient modulation schemes and coding techniques to maximize data throughput, while minimizing signal distortion.
