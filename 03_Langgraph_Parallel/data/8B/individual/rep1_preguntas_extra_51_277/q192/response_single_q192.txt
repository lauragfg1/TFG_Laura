TOPIC: What are the trade-offs between increasing redundant gateways versus higher fade margins in achieving 99.9% feeder link availability?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Increasing redundant gateways (e.g., n + p scheme) can provide higher feeder link availability, but it comes with increased complexity and cost. Higher fade margins through uplink power control can also improve availability, but its effectiveness is limited due to heavy rain attenuation. A trade-off between these two approaches must be made considering the specific system requirements and constraints.
