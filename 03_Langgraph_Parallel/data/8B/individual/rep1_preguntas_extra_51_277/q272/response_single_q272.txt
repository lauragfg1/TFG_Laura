TOPIC: Which power allocation algorithms maximize network throughput for entangled photon pair distribution across dynamic optical inter-satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To maximize network throughput for entangled photon pair distribution across dynamic optical inter-satellite links, power allocation algorithms such as Joint Power and Carrier Allocation (JPCA) [1] or Energy Efficient Power Allocation (EEPA) [2] can be employed. These algorithms optimize power allocation based on traffic demands and channel conditions to minimize interference and maximize signal-to-noise ratio.
