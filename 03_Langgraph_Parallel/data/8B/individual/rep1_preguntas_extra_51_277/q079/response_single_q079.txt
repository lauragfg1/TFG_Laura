TOPIC: How does frequency planning address atmospheric absorption and interference in mmWave/THz bands for hybrid satellite-terrestrial networks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Frequency planning in mmWave/THz bands for hybrid satellite-terrestrial networks must address atmospheric absorption and interference. This can be achieved through adaptive coding and modulation, data rate adaptation, link power control, site diversity, and onboard adaptive power allocation. Additionally, careful selection of frequency bands with minimal atmospheric absorption is crucial to minimize signal degradation.
