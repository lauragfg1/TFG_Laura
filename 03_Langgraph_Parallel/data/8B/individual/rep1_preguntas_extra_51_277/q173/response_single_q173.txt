TOPIC: What operational challenges arise in coordinating beam hopping and power management with dynamic gateway switching?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Operational challenges arise in coordinating beam hopping and power management with dynamic gateway switching due to time-varying traffic demands, self-interference generated by co-channel beams, and the need for adaptive bandwidth allocation. The system must balance frequency reuse schemes with reduced on-board power amplifier usage, while managing interference and ensuring QoS guarantees.
