TOPIC: How does mechanical beam steering in PD/PR TAs affect orbital slot efficiency and coverage pattern adaptability?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Mechanical beam steering in PD/PR TAs affects orbital slot efficiency and coverage pattern adaptability by introducing scan loss, which can be ≤2.8 dB for PD TAs and ≤3 dB for PR TAs (as per the reference context). This may compromise the satellite's overall performance, particularly in applications requiring polarization diversity and switching between adjacent cells.
