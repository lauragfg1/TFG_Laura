TOPIC: What are the challenges of integrating real measured channel data into antenna pattern modeling for EE-optimal precoding?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Integrating real measured channel data into antenna pattern modeling for EE-optimal precoding poses challenges due to the need for accurate and consistent representation of complex propagation effects. The XPD (Cross-Polarization Discrimination) values of 15dB in open rural, suburban, and urban environments may not accurately capture non-linear effects introduced by TWTA amplifiers, which can distort transmitted waveforms.
