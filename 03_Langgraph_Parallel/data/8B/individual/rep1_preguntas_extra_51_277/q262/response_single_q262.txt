TOPIC: How does the GEO orbital configuration influence phase stability and associated precoding scheme complexity in multibeam systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The GEO orbital configuration influences phase stability due to its long propagation path, leading to high correlation between signals at the satellite side. This requires robust beamforming techniques to mitigate signal-to-interference-and-noise ratio degradation and minimize transmission power consumption.
