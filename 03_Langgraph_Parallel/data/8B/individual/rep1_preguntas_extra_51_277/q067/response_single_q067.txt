TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between transparent vs regenerative payloads for PHY/MAC layer latency in hybrid terrestrial-satellite architectures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Transparent payloads may incur higher latency due to signal degradation and re-transmission, whereas regenerative payloads can reduce latency by amplifying and re-modulating signals. However, this comes at the cost of increased complexity and power consumption. A trade-off between these two approaches exists, with hybrid solutions potentially offering a balance between latency reduction and system complexity.
