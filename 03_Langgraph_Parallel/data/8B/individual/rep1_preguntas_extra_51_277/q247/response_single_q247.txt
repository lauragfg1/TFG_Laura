TOPIC: How should redundancy be factored into satellite transponder design to maintain KPIs during signal degradation?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To maintain KPIs during signal degradation, redundancy should be factored into satellite transponder design by implementing error-correcting codes and using multiple transponders in a redundant configuration. This can include techniques such as Forward Error Correction (FEC) and Multiple Input Multiple Output (MIMO). The goal is to ensure that even if one or more transponders fail, the signal can still be recovered with minimal loss of data.
