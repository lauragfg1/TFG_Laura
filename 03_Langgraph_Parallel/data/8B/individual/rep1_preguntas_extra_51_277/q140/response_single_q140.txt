TOPIC: How does the reduced magnication factor in the proposed design affect interference mitigation strategies in densely populated orbital slots?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The reduced magnification factor in the proposed design would exacerbate interference mitigation challenges in densely populated orbital slots. As the beamforming gain is reduced, the signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR) degrades. This necessitates more aggressive interference suppression techniques, such as advanced digital beamforming or frequency reuse schemes, to maintain system performance.
