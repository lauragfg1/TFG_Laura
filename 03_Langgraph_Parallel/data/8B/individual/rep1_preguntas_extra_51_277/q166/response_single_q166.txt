TOPIC: How does ECRA's MRC SINR model influence transponder linearity requirements in high-interference scenarios?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In high-interference scenarios, ECRA's MRC SINR model increases the demand on transponder linearity due to the weighted combination of replicas' signals. This leads to a higher signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) requirement for accurate detection and decoding. The increased sensitivity and noise tolerance necessitate more stringent linearity specifications for the transponder, typically expressed in terms of adjacent channel power rejection (ACPR).
