TOPIC: How does cloud attenuation influence required link budget margins for optical LEO downlinks in different geographic regions?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Cloud attenuation significantly impacts optical LEO downlinks, requiring increased link budget margins in regions with high cloud cover. The availability of these links is influenced by local cloud cover, necessitating ground stations to be located in areas with minimal cloud cover. This results in varying data rates due to free space loss and atmospheric transmissivity, which depend on the link elevation.
