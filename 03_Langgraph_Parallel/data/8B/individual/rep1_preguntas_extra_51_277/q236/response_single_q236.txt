TOPIC: How can link budget calculations account for spatially variable SINR due to overlapping beams in a multigroup multicast precoding system?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Link budget calculations can account for spatially variable SINR due to overlapping beams in multigroup multicast precoding systems by considering the interference between beams and users. The average user throughput, as calculated by equation (5), should be adjusted to reflect the reduced SINR caused by beam overlap. This can be done by applying a correction factor to the link budget calculations, taking into account the number of overlapping beams and their corresponding power allocations.
