TOPIC: How are link budget margins calculated when accounting for time-varying rain attenuation versus fixed gaseous absorption losses?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
When accounting for time-varying rain attenuation versus fixed gaseous absorption losses, link budget margins are calculated by removing the estimated gaseous contribution and adding a fade margin to compensate for clear sky effects. The fade margin is typically set based on empirical models or measurement data, such as those provided in ITU-R Recommendation P.618.
