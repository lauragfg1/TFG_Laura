TOPIC: What beamforming strategies optimize spectral efficiency in multi-beam LEO systems with moving coverage?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To optimize spectral efficiency in multi-beam LEO systems with moving coverage, rate-splitting multiple access (RSMA) and analog beamforming optimization are effective strategies. RSMA allows for simultaneous transmission to multiple users, reducing interference and increasing spectral efficiency. Analog beamforming enables the formation of directional beams that can adapt to changing user locations and reduce inter-beam interference.
