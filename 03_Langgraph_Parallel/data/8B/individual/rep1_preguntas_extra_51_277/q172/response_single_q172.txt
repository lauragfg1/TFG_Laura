TOPIC: How does the proposed MSSC switching rate affect network stability compared to traditional SSC in payload resource allocation?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The proposed MSSC (Multi-Switching Scheme for Convergence) switching rate affects network stability by improving it compared to traditional SSC. The MSSC's ability to estimate SNR without phase information and reroute traffic via terrestrial fibre interconnection reduces the impact of link failures, resulting in improved network stability.
