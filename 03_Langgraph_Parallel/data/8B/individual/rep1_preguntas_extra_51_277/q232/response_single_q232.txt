TOPIC: What antenna design considerations balance inter-beam interference reduction with flexible coverage adaptation in precoding-enabled satellites?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To balance inter-beam interference reduction with flexible coverage adaptation in precoding-enabled satellites, consider using linear precoding techniques that exploit spatial degrees of freedom. Precoding enables interference mitigation while improving spectral efficiency. Antenna design should focus on managing interferences through channel state information (CSI) at the transmitter.
