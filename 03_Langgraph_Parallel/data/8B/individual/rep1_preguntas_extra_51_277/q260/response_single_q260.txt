TOPIC: How do phase perturbations in GEO satellite channels impact link budget calculations and required power margins for SINR constraints?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Phase perturbations in GEO satellite channels can impact link budget calculations and required power margins for SINR constraints. Atmospheric impairments, such as amplitude attenuation and phase disturbances, decrease signal power and CNR, leading to capacity degradation similar to SISO systems. To mitigate these effects, proper satellite antenna gains and optimization of the channel matrix are necessary.
