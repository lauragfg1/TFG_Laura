TOPIC: What phase stability thresholds for ultra-stable oscillators are critical to maintain robust precoding performance under mobility-induced CSI errors?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The phase stability threshold for ultra-stable oscillators critical to maintain robust precoding performance under mobility-induced CSI errors is related to the coherence time of the channel. For satellite communications, a coherence time of around 1-10 ms is typical. Oscillators with a phase noise floor below -120 dBc/Hz at 1 MHz offset can provide sufficient stability.
