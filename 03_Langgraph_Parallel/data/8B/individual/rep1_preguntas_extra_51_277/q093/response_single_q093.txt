TOPIC: How does the 5-minute C/N reporting interval constrain payload resource allocation and network scheduling algorithms?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 5-minute C/N reporting interval constrains payload resource allocation and network scheduling algorithms by limiting the frequency at which channel state information can be updated. This may lead to suboptimal power allocation, as the optimal power distribution may change more frequently than every 5 minutes.
