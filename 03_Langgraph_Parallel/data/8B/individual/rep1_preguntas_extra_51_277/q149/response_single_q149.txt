TOPIC: Can the triangular grid with 15% larger spacing introduce scan angle limitations or grating lobes in dense satellite constellations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Yes, introducing a triangular grid with 15% larger spacing in dense satellite constellations can introduce scan angle limitations and grating lobes due to the inherent properties of periodic arrays. The increased spacing may not be sufficient to mitigate the effects of spatial interference, leading to a degradation in system performance.
