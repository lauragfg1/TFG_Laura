TOPIC: What interference management strategies are critical for full-frequency reuse in multi-beam LEO satellite constellations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For full-frequency reuse in multi-beam LEO satellite constellations, critical interference management strategies include linear precoding and multi-user detection. These techniques exploit channel state information (CSI) at the transmitter to mitigate inter-beam interference.
