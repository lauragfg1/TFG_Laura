TOPIC: How does dynamic power allocation across beams address localized rain events while maintaining network-wide QoS requirements?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Dynamic power allocation across beams can address localized rain events by optimizing transmission power based on channel conditions and interference levels. This approach ensures that beamforming is adapted to the changing weather conditions, minimizing signal degradation and maintaining network-wide QoS requirements.
