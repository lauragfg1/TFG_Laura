TOPIC: How do LEO and MEO satellite constellations balance link budget margins against atmospheric attenuation in Ka/V/Q bands for sparsely populated regions?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
LEO and MEO satellite constellations balance link budget margins against atmospheric attenuation in Ka/V/Q bands by employing adaptive coding and modulation, data rate adaptation, and onboard adaptive power allocation to mitigate impairments. Site diversity and link power control are also used to compensate for increased atmospheric effects at higher frequencies.
