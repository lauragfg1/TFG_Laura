TOPIC: How can real-time traffic be prioritized in dynamic resource allocation without underutilizing satellite capacity during off-peak?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Real-time traffic can be prioritized in dynamic resource allocation using Rate-Based Dynamic Capacity (RBDC) or Constant Rate Assignment (CRA), which provide capacity guarantees. These methods ensure that critical applications receive sufficient bandwidth, preventing underutilization during off-peak periods.
