TOPIC: What transceiver aperture size and wavelength considerations balance diffraction loss and turbulence-induced beam wandering in downlink quantum channels?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To balance diffraction loss and turbulence-induced beam wandering in downlink quantum channels, consider an optical system with a large aperture size (>0.5 m) to mitigate scintillation effects and ensure negligible detector noise. A wavelength of around 1550 nm is suitable for minimizing atmospheric losses while maintaining sufficient signal strength.
