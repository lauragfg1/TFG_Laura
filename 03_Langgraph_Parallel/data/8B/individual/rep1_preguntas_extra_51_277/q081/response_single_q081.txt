TOPIC: How does beam squint effect influence frequency-selective beamforming strategies for extremely large aperture arrays?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Beam squint effect in extremely large aperture arrays causes frequency-selective beamforming challenges due to different frequencies having different beam directions. This results in reduced antenna gain and spatial multiplexing capabilities. To mitigate this, interference-rejecting precoding/combining schemes like zero-forcing (ZF) and minimum mean square error (MMSE) beamforming are required in a distributed or hierarchical way.
