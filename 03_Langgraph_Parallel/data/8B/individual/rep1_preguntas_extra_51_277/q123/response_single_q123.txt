TOPIC: What challenges arise in calculating link budgets when dynamically reallocating bandwidth across beams using a digital channelizer?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
When dynamically reallocating bandwidth across beams using a digital channelizer, challenges arise in calculating link budgets due to the changing frequency allocation and potential inter-beam interference. The dynamic nature of the system makes it difficult to accurately predict signal attenuation, noise figure, and other parameters that affect link budget calculations.
