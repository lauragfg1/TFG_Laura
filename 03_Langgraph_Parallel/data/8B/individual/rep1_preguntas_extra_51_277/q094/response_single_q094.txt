TOPIC: How does the lens's secant elevation pattern impact link budget margins given varying atmospheric attenuation at Ka-band for LEO?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The lens's secant elevation pattern impacts link budget margins by altering the beam divergence, which is influenced by atmospheric attenuation at Ka-band for LEO. As atmospheric turbulence causes scintillation, the signal intensity varies, affecting the received power. This results in reduced link margin due to increased uncertainty in the received signal.
