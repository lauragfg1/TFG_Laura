TOPIC: What payload constraints limit dynamic beam allocation flexibility in multi-user LEO satellite systems with mobile terminals?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Payload constraints limiting dynamic beam allocation flexibility in multi-user LEO satellite systems with mobile terminals include: power limitations, thermal management, and physical antenna size. These constraints restrict the number of beams per satellite, typically fewer than or equal to 74 beams for LEO networks.
