TOPIC: What beam allocation algorithms ensure dynamic spectrum sharing between terrestrial and aerial networks in mixed-deployment scenarios?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The beam allocation algorithms that ensure dynamic spectrum sharing between terrestrial and aerial networks in mixed-deployment scenarios are: Demand Matching, Total Power Minimization, and Utilized Bandwidth Minimization. These algorithms adaptively allocate wireless resources according to heterogeneous traffic density, ensuring efficient use of idle satellite spectrum.
