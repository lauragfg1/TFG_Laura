TOPIC: What interference mitigation strategies are required when combining non-adjacent Inmarsat channels for radar while maintaining network QoS?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To mitigate interference when combining non-adjacent Inmarsat channels for radar while maintaining network QoS, consider implementing techniques such as dynamic band allocation, adjacent channel rejection (ACR), and band stitching. Dynamic band allocation can help manage the appearance of signals within the overall downlink band. ACR can reduce interference between adjacent channels. Band stitching enables combining non-adjacent bands to improve range resolution.
