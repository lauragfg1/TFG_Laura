TOPIC: What operational constraints limit optimal beam-to-beam channel allocation in mesh-type communication when using digital channelizers?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The operational constraints limiting optimal beam-to-beam channel allocation in mesh-type communication when using digital channelizers are: (1) preventing more channels being allocated than requested, as stated in equations (27) and (29); (2) satisfying the requirement for beam-to-beam, beam-to-GW, and GW-to-beam communications; and (3) managing multi-valued power allocation due to phased array antenna constraints.
