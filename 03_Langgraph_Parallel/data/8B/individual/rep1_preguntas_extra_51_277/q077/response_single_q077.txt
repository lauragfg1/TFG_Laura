TOPIC: How do orbital configurations of LEO satellites impact beamforming design for minimizing path loss and maximizing coverage efficiency?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
LEO satellite orbital configurations impact beamforming design by affecting the line-of-sight (LOS) and shadowed-Rician (SR) fading components. The LOS component is influenced by the elevation angle at the coverage center, which varies with the satellite orbit type. To minimize path loss and maximize coverage efficiency, beamforming designs should account for these orbital configuration-dependent effects on the SR fading parameters.
