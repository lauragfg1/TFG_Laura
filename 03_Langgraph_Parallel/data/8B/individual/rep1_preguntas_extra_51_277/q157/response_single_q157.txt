TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise from co-locating 20/30 GHz beams in multi-satellite networks to avoid interference?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Co-locating 20/30 GHz beams in multi-satellite networks poses frequency planning challenges due to the proximity of Ka and Ku frequency bands. The overlap between these bands can lead to co-channel interference, requiring careful frequency allocation and reuse strategies to mitigate adjacent beam interference.
