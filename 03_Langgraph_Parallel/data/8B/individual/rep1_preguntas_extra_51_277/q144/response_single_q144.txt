TOPIC: What payload calibration challenges arise from combining optical beamforming ICs with dual-reflector geometries in high-frequency phased arrays?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Combining optical beamforming ICs with dual-reflector geometries in high-frequency phased arrays introduces challenges related to payload calibration, including non-linear phase distributions along array elements and significant spot beam deviation from the array center during scanning. This limits the number of simultaneously illuminated array elements, reducing achievable effective isotropic radiated power (EIRP) levels and scan performance.
