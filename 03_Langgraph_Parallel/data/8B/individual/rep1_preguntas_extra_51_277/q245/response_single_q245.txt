TOPIC: How can MIMO techniques be adapted for Ka-band satellite links to mitigate rain fading without compromising throughput?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To adapt MIMO techniques for Ka-band satellite links and mitigate rain fading without compromising throughput, consider using combined precoding with multi-user MIMO. This approach can increase channel capacity while accounting for the inherent rank deficiency of the MIMO channel matrix due to LOS operation and absence of scattering effects in satellite channels.
