TOPIC: How do shadowed Rician parameters (m, b, ω) affect link budget calculations considering interference fluctuations at both terrestrial relay and destination nodes?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Shadowed Rician parameters (m, b, ω) affect link budget calculations by introducing fading severity and multipath components. The average power of the line-of-sight (LOS) component (ω) and the scatter (2b) influence signal-to-noise ratio (SNR). The Nakagami parameter (m) models the distribution of the LOS component. Interference fluctuations at terrestrial relay and destination nodes can be mitigated by considering these parameters in link budget calculations.
