TOPIC: What coverage trade-offs emerge when steering the RA beams to ±50° given spillover and scan loss in small apertures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Steering RA beams to ±50° in small apertures compromises coverage due to spillover and scan loss. Theoretical maximum gain is reduced, and average unit cell loss increases, leading to a narrower beamwidth and higher sidelobes. As a result, the effective aperture size decreases, causing a reduction in received signal power.
