TOPIC: What strategies optimize DAMA processes to reduce overhead while maintaining fair capacity allocation across terminals?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
DAMA (Dynamic Capacity Allocation) processes can be optimized by implementing a hybrid approach combining static and dynamic capacity allocation. This involves assigning a base capacity to each terminal for low-priority traffic, while reserving dynamically allocated capacity for high-priority or real-time services based on current traffic demands.
