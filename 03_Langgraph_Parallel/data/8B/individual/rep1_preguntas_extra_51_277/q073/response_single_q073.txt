TOPIC: What guard time optimizations address differential delay variations in RA preambles for GEO user terminals?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Two possible guard time optimizations address differential delay variations in RA preambles for GEO user terminals: (1) defining novel format options with increased guard times, but this would require modifications to the current NR RA procedure; and (2) having a system architecture that ensures lower differential delays by identifying the minimum elevation angle at the coverage centre.
