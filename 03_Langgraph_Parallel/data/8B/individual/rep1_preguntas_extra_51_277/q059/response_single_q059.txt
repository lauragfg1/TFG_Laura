TOPIC: How does dual-mode feeding (TEM/TE1) influence power allocation and payload thermal management in multi-beam systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Dual-mode feeding (TEM/TE1) in multi-beam systems allows for flexible power allocation and thermal management by enabling the use of different modes to optimize performance. The TEM mode is typically used for low-power signals, while TE1 mode is used for high-power signals. This flexibility can lead to improved payload efficiency and reduced thermal stress.
