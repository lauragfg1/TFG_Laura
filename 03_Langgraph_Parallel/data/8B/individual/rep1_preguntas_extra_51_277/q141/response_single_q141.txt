TOPIC: What are the implications of operating across 20-40 GHz on frequency planning for adjacent beams given the increased number of active elements in the FPA?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Operating across 20-40 GHz with increased number of active elements in FPA will lead to increased beam squinting due to dispersive optical true time delay (TDD) devices. This can be mitigated by using light sources of different wavelengths or employing frequency-dependent TTDs. The resulting channel matrix may admit single-stream transmission, but careful frequency planning is required to minimize interference between adjacent beams.
