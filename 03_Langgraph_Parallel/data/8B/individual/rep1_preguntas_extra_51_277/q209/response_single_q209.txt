TOPIC: How do LEO and GEO satellite configurations balance latency and coverage trade-offs for UAV data links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
LEO (Low Earth Orbit) satellite configurations have lower latency compared to GEO (Geostationary Earth Orbit) due to shorter signal propagation times. However, LEO satellites offer less coverage area and require more satellites for global coverage. For UAV data links, LEO satellites' advantages in latency are ideal for applications requiring real-time communication. Trade-offs between latency and coverage can be balanced by using constellations of LEO satellites.
