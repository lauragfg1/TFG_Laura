TOPIC: How does TDD satellite system design account for large RTT when optimizing antenna gain and coverage for delay-sensitive RLNC applications?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
TDD satellite system design accounts for large Round Trip Time (RTT) by dividing transmission into multiple rounds and using feedback to optimize antenna gain and coverage. This approach is necessary due to the propagation delay in satellite communications, which can be several milliseconds.
