TOPIC: What link budget trade-offs arise when adjusting elevation angles and spatial filtering for dynamic power allocation in MSS?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Adjusting elevation angles and spatial filtering for dynamic power allocation in MSS introduces trade-offs between signal gain, interference mitigation, and system complexity. Increasing elevation angles can reduce interference but may lead to increased path loss and reduced signal strength. Spatial filtering can improve signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) but may require more complex antenna designs and increase system latency.
