TOPIC: What network congestion risks emerge from using mechanical beam steering versus electronic methods in high-throughput satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Mechanical beam steering in high-throughput satellite systems introduces network congestion risks due to increased latency and signal propagation delay. The mechanical movement causes additional time for signal processing, leading to higher packet delays and increased likelihood of congestion. In contrast, electronic beam steering methods offer faster switching times, reducing the risk of congestion.
