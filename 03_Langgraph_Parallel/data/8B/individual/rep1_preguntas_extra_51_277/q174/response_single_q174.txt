TOPIC: Can spectrum sharing between feeder and user links be optimized post-Ka band migration without causing congestion?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Spectrum sharing between feeder and user links can be optimized post-Ka band migration by relocating the feeder link to the Q/V band (40/50 GHz), which offers higher bandwidth. However, heavy rain attenuation in this frequency band necessitates the use of gateway diversity techniques to ensure required availability. Optimizing spectrum sharing requires careful consideration of link budget and fade mitigation techniques.
