TOPIC: How do GEO and LEO constellations trade off coverage area versus latency in 5G NTN architectures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
GEO constellations trade off coverage area for higher latency due to their large distance (36,000 km) introducing minimum one-way latency of 238 ms. In contrast, LEO constellations offer reduced propagation delays and can provide global coverage with lower latency (down to 2 ms).
