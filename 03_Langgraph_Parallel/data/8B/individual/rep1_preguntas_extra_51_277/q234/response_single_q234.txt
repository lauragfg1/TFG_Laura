TOPIC: How do current frequency separation techniques in multibeam satellites limit system capacity scaling despite mitigating co-channel interference?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Current frequency separation techniques in multibeam satellites, such as dividing the frequency band into four sub-bands or employing orthogonal polarizations, limit system capacity scaling by introducing inefficiencies and constraints on resource allocation. These methods mitigate co-channel interference but do not fully utilize available bandwidth, hindering scalability.
