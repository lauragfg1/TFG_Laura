TOPIC: How does the inclined geostationary orbit's periodic C/N variation impact satellite antenna design and link budget calculations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The inclined geostationary orbit's periodic C/N variation impacts satellite antenna design and link budget calculations by requiring adjustments to maintain optimal spacing between earth station antennas. A displacement of 0.05° in longitude results in a variation of relative longitude, affecting the optimal spacing. However, this effect is negligible on channel capacity, and related effects can be neglected.
