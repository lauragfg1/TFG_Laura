TOPIC: Can oblique projection in beam coverage impact inter-cell interference differently than orthographic projection in GEO satellites?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Yes, oblique projection in beam coverage can impact inter-cell interference differently than orthographic projection in GEO satellites due to the varying angular selectivity and non-ideal spatial filtering by the satellite antenna. The angle between the selected user's position and the central line of the corresponding beam affects the interference strength.
