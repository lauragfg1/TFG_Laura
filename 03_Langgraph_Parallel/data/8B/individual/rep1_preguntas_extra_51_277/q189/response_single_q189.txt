TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between orbital altitude and achievable SNR when deploying massive MIMO with full frequency reuse?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
When deploying massive MIMO with full frequency reuse, achievable Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) decreases with increasing orbital altitude due to increased path loss. As altitude increases, the received signal power is reduced, while noise remains relatively constant, leading to a decrease in SNR. This trade-off is inherent to satellite communications and cannot be avoided without significant system redesign.
