TOPIC: How does frequency planning balance subcarrier spacing flexibility against Doppler tolerance in LEO-based NB-IoT uplinks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Frequency planning in LEO-based NB-IoT uplinks balances subcarrier spacing flexibility against Doppler tolerance by minimizing the differential Doppler effect (δfdi) to within LTE's 950 Hz constraint. This is achieved through careful selection of subcarrier frequencies and symbol group granularity, ensuring that the frame structure seen by the satellite does not contain overlapping information among subcarriers.
