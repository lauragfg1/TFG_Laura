TOPIC: How does gateway separation distance affect spatial correlation and link budget margins in Q/V band feeder links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
As gateway separation distance increases, spatial correlation between rain attenuations on feeder links decreases due to reduced overlap in rain cell distributions. This decrease in spatial correlation leads to increased variability in link budgets, potentially requiring larger fade margins or more robust transmit diversity techniques to ensure availability.
