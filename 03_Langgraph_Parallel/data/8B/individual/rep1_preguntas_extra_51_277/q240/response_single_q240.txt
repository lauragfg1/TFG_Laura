TOPIC: What operational challenges arise in balancing power allocation between traffic-bearing beams and precoded pilot fields in superframe structures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Interbeam interference and limited power flexibility can arise when balancing power allocation between traffic-bearing beams and precoded pilot fields in superframe structures. This is due to the dependency of interbeam interference on the power allocated to each beam, which can limit system performance and flexibility.
