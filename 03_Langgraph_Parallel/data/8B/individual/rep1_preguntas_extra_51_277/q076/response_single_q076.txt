TOPIC: How do HARQ retransmission limits balance throughput and latency in geostationary satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
HARQ retransmission limits balance throughput and latency in geostationary satellite links by controlling the maximum number of parallel HARQ processes, which is determined by the overall HARQ processing time (th_a_rq) and transmission time interval (tti). A higher tti allows for more parallel processes, increasing throughput but also increasing latency. The minimum number of parallel processes is obtained as a function of th_a_rq and tti.
