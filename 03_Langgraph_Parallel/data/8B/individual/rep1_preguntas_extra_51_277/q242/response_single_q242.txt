TOPIC: How does imperfect CSIT from quantized feedback constrain real-time interference management capabilities in multibeam networks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Imperfect CSIT from quantized feedback limits real-time interference management in multibeam networks by introducing residual interference. This occurs when the number of transmit antennas is insufficient to place precoders in the null space of unintended groups, allowing only primary inter-group interference elimination. Consequently, beamforming and power control cannot fully mitigate interference, leading to overloaded systems and decreased performance.
