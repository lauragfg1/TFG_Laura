TOPIC: How does beam steering affect polarization purity and interference mitigation in congested multi-satellite scenarios?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In congested multi-satellite scenarios, beam steering can affect polarization purity and interference mitigation by causing partial radiating of transmit signals to adjacent beams through antenna sidelobes. This results in accumulated interference, degrading the Signal-to-Interference-plus-Noise Ratio (SINR). Employing orthogonal polarizations or frequency band separation can mitigate this effect.
