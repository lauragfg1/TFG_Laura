TOPIC: How does the digital channelizer's traffic multiplexing on feeder links alter network efficiency under asymmetric beam traffic demands?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The digital channelizer's traffic multiplexing on feeder links improves network efficiency by dynamically adjusting frequency bandwidth allocation between beams, accommodating asymmetric beam traffic demands. This flexibility enables efficient utilization of available channels, reducing idle channels and increasing overall system capacity.
