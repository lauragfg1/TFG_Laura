TOPIC: How does multicast precoding complexity impact payload operation when scheduling frames across hundreds of dynamically allocated beams?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Multicast precoding complexity impacts payload operation when scheduling frames across hundreds of dynamically allocated beams due to unequal data payloads amongst users and the need for practical system implementations to consider precoding on a frame-by-frame basis. This leads to challenges in joint processing of multiple streams, resulting in reduced capacity and increased interference.
