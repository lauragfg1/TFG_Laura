TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between maintaining axial ratio below 3dB and achieving wide-angle beam steering up to 45°?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Achieving wide-angle beam steering up to 45° may compromise maintaining an axial ratio below 3dB due to increased scanning loss and potential cross-polarization levels. The trade-offs involve optimizing antenna design, selecting suitable materials, and balancing performance metrics such as gain, scanning loss, and axial ratio.
