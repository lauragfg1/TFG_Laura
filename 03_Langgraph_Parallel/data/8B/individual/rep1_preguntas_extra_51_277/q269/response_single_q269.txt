TOPIC: What beamforming techniques optimize spatial overlap between local oscillator and quantum signal in free-space homodyne detection under atmospheric distortion?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To optimize spatial overlap between local oscillator and quantum signal in free-space homodyne detection under atmospheric distortion, analog beamforming techniques such as adaptive optics or spatial light modulators can be employed. These methods adjust the phase front of the local oscillator to match the distorted wavefront of the quantum signal, enhancing spatial overlap and detection efficiency.
