TOPIC: Which trade-offs between lens compactness and gain roll-off are critical for maintaining reliable downlink during satellite orbital motion?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To maintain reliable downlink during satellite orbital motion, critical trade-offs between lens compactness and gain roll-off involve minimizing aberrations and distortion while ensuring sufficient signal-to-noise ratio (SNR). This requires balancing the lens's focal length, diameter, and material to optimize transmission efficiency. A smaller lens may compromise on SNR, while a larger lens may introduce excessive mass and complexity.
