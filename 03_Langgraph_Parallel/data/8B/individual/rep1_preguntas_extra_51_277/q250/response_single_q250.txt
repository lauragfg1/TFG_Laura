TOPIC: What beam allocation algorithms best balance congestion relief and spectral efficiency in hybrid satellite-terrestrial backhaul?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The beam allocation algorithms that best balance congestion relief and spectral efficiency in hybrid satellite-terrestrial backhaul are Joint Power and Carrier Allocation (JPCA) [1] and Flexible Bandwidth Allocation Scheme (FBAS) [2]. These algorithms adjust the incoming traffic rate based on channel conditions, maximizing system throughput while stabilizing the network. JPCA optimizes power and carrier allocation for individual SINR constraints, while FBAS allocates bandwidth dynamically based on traffic demands and channel conditions.
