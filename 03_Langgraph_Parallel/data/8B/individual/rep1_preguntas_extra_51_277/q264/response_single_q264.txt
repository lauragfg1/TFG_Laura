TOPIC: How can dynamic power allocation balance network efficiency and outage probability in multibeam satellite payloads with phase noise?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Dynamic power allocation can balance network efficiency and outage probability in multibeam satellite payloads with phase noise by optimizing the Average Unmet System Capacity (M N AUSC) and Average Satisfaction Index (M ASI). This can be achieved by minimizing the power consumption while maximizing the used bandwidth, utilizing metrics such as ps[i] or pk[i], and considering the Uniform Power 4CR and Demand-based Power 4CR. The noise power density (N0) of -204 dBW/MHz should also be taken into account.
