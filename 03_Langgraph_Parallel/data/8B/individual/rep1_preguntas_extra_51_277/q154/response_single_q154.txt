TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between using fully metallic transmitarrays versus dielectric-based designs in terms of orbital thermal management and mass constraints?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In fully metallic transmitarrays, thermal management is a significant concern due to high absorption of solar radiation. This leads to increased self-heating and reduced lifespan. Dielectric-based designs mitigate this issue as they have lower thermal conductivity and can be designed with built-in heat sinks. Mass constraints are also alleviated in dielectric-based designs as metals are often heavier.
