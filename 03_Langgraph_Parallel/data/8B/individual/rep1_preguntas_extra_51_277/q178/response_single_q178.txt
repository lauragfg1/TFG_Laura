TOPIC: What link budget trade-offs exist between RLNC overhead and forward error correction in high-PER satellite channels with strict throughput-PDR constraints?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In high-PER satellite channels with strict throughput-PDR constraints, RLNC overhead and forward error correction (FEC) exhibit a trade-off. Increasing FEC strength reduces PER but increases latency and bandwidth usage, which may compromise throughput. Conversely, higher RLNC overhead can improve throughput but may degrade PER if not properly managed.
