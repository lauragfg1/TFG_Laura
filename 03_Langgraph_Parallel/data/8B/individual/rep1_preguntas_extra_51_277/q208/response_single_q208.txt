TOPIC: What operational constraints does mechanical beam steering impose on real-time payload resource allocation during dynamic traffic shifts?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Mechanical beam steering imposes operational constraints on real-time payload resource allocation due to its mechanical nature, which introduces latency and limits flexibility in response to dynamic traffic shifts. The mechanical movement required for beam steering can cause delays in adjusting to changing traffic patterns, making it challenging to efficiently allocate resources in real-time.
