TOPIC: What challenges emerge in maintaining Nash equilibrium for multi-user power allocation with imperfect satellite beam orthogonality?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The challenges in maintaining Nash equilibrium for multi-user power allocation with imperfect satellite beam orthogonality arise due to interference between users and the resulting non-convex optimization problem. The Stackelberg game model used in the proposed algorithm assumes perfect orthogonality, but real-world satellite beams exhibit imperfections, leading to deviations from optimal power allocation.
