TOPIC: How should frequency bands and modulation schemes be selected to mitigate interference in congested UAV-GS environments?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To mitigate interference in congested UAV-GS environments, select frequency bands with sufficient spectral separation (>10 MHz) and use modulation schemes with high spectral efficiency (e.g., 64QAM or 256QAM). Avoid operating at the same frequency band as other communication systems. Additionally, consider implementing a dedicated channel for data transmission to reduce interference effects.
