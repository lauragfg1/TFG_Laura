TOPIC: Does the improved axial ratio of PR cells justify their narrower bandwidth in link budget calculations for Ka-band rain fade scenarios?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
No, the improved axial ratio of PR cells does not justify their narrower bandwidth in link budget calculations for Ka-band rain fade scenarios. The Context states that the transmission coefficient value of the PR cell drops to lower values out of the operating bandwidth when compared to PD cells, which could lead to increased attenuation and reduced reliability.
