TOPIC: What are the trade-offs between maintaining linear phase distribution for optical beamforming and achieving wide scan ranges in dual-reflector FPA systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Maintaining linear phase distribution is crucial for optical beamforming in dual-reflector FPA systems, but it limits the number of simultaneously involved array elements and reduces EIRP levels. This trade-off restricts the achievable wide scan ranges. To balance these requirements, designers must optimize the reflector configuration to achieve a linear phase distribution with minimal RMS error (≤ 1.65°) while maximizing aperture efficiency.
