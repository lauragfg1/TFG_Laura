TOPIC: How does co-frequency operation between satellite and terrestrial networks impact interference mitigation strategies?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Co-frequency operation between satellite and terrestrial networks significantly impacts interference mitigation strategies, as both systems can interfere with each other. The available literature suggests that co-frequency interferences may occur heavily in uplink transmissions if both networks operate on the same frequency in the same geographical area.
