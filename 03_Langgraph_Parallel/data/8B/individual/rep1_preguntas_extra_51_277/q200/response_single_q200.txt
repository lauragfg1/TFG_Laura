TOPIC: How do buffer sizes and orbital parameters interact to affect achievable throughput in optical downlink systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The achievable throughput in optical downlink systems is affected by buffer sizes and orbital parameters through their interaction with data rate and latency. As buffer size increases, more data can be stored, allowing for higher data rates to be transmitted without overflowing the buffer. However, large buffers may not compensate for high latency caused by poor network availability due to weather or other factors. A large, worldwide optical ground station (OGS) network is necessary to achieve low latency and high throughput.
