TOPIC: What antenna design trade-offs exist between beam gain efficiency and robustness to phase errors in multibeam architectures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In multibeam architectures, beam gain efficiency and robustness to phase errors are trade-offs due to the conflicting requirements of high antenna gain and low sensitivity to phase perturbations. Higher gain beams are more susceptible to phase errors, while lower gain beams provide better robustness but may not achieve maximum coverage.
