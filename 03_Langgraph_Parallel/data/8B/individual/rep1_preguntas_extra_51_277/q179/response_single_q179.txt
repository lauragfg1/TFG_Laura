TOPIC: How does orbital configuration impact frequency planning trade-offs for RLNC schemes requiring continuous coverage and low latency in multi-user broadcasts?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Orbital configuration impacts frequency planning trade-offs for RLNC schemes requiring continuous coverage and low latency in multi-user broadcasts by affecting signal-to-interference ratio (SINR) distribution. In full frequency reuse scenarios, SINR is reduced due to intra-system interferences, leading to increased outage probability. Orbital configuration also influences interference levels, which must be mitigated through frequency planning.
