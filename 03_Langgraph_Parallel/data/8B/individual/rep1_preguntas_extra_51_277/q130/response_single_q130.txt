TOPIC: How do GEO versus LEO satellite orbits impact power flux density and resolution trade-offs in passive radar systems using Inmarsat and Iridium?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
GEO satellites, like Inmarsat, have larger power flux densities due to their higher altitude and wider beamwidths. LEO satellites, such as Iridium, have lower power flux densities but offer lower delay and pointing requirements. This trade-off affects the resolution in passive radar systems, with GEO offering higher SNRs and signal-to-clutter ratios but requiring more favorable geometries.
