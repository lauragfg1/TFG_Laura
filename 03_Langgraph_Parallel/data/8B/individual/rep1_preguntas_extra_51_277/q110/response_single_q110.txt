TOPIC: How do crosslink requirements in mesh satellite architectures affect feeder link budgets and overall system cost per Mbps?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Crosslink requirements in mesh satellite architectures increase feeder link budgets due to additional transmission power and bandwidth allocated for inter-satellite communication. This leads to higher system cost per Mbps as more resources are devoted to maintaining network connectivity and ensuring QoS guarantees.
