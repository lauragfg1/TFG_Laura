TOPIC: How does the use of polarized waves in Ka-band user and feeder links impact interference management and coverage flexibility?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The use of polarized waves in Ka-band user and feeder links can impact interference management by reducing cross-polarization interference, but may also increase sensitivity to polarization mismatch. This can affect coverage flexibility by introducing limitations on beamforming and spatial multiplexing techniques.
