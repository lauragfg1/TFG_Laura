TOPIC: Can simultaneous uplink/downlink beam reconfiguration cause interference in tightly packed Ku-band spectrum allocations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Yes, simultaneous uplink/downlink beam reconfiguration can cause interference in tightly packed Ku-band spectrum allocations. When an earth station is in line with GSO and NGSO satellites, its main beam can create and receive interference. Additionally, the side lobe gains of the beampatterns of many NGSO terminals on the ground can cause considerable harmful interference to the GSO satellite.
