TOPIC: How does orbital configuration affect the feasibility of frequency reuse ratios in flexible beam allocation for HTS systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Orbital configuration affects the feasibility of frequency reuse ratios in flexible beam allocation for HTS systems by enabling or limiting the spatial separation between beams. In geostationary (GEO) orbits, high-throughput satellites (HTS) can achieve narrower beams with frequency reuse across multiple beams, increasing capacity and decreasing costs per Mb/s.
