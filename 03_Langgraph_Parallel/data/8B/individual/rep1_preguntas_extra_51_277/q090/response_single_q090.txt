TOPIC: What payload modifications are needed to handle real-time C/N aggregation from millions of terminals without compromising transponder efficiency?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To handle real-time C/N aggregation from millions of terminals without compromising transponder efficiency, payload modifications are needed to accommodate increased data rates and packet sizes. This can be achieved by employing techniques such as Adaptive Coding and Modulation (ACM) and Incremental Redundancy (IR). ACM adjusts the modulation scheme based on channel conditions, while IR adds redundant packets to ensure reliable transmission.
