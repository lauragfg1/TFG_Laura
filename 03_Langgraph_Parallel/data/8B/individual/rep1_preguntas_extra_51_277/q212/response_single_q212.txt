TOPIC: What propagation loss models are critical for Ku vs C-band links in UAV communications under rain fade scenarios?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For Ku-band links, the free space path loss model is suitable due to its use in satellite networks. For C-band links, the UMI (Urban Microcellular Indoor) path loss model may be more relevant as it accounts for terrestrial environments. However, rain attenuation becomes more severe at higher frequencies like Ka-band and V-band. The ITU R model for specific attenuation can be used to estimate this effect.
