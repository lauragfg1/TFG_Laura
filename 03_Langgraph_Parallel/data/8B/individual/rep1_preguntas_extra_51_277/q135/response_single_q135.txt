TOPIC: What spectral efficiency challenges arise from real-time adaptation to sparsely distributed channels in Inmarsat's BGAN signals?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Inmarsat's BGAN signals exhibit spectral efficiency challenges due to real-time adaptation to sparsely distributed channels. The use of 16 QAM and 0.25 root raised cosine filters in each 200 kHz channel may lead to increased noise susceptibility, compromising the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR). Additionally, the high symbol rate of 151.2 ksymbol/s might exacerbate these issues.
