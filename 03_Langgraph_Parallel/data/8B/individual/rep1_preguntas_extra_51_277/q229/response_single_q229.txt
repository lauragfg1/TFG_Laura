TOPIC: How does nonlinear power amplifier operation influence payload power management during EE-maximizing precoding?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Nonlinear power amplifier operation introduces intermodulation products and nonlinearity, which degrade signal quality. To mitigate this, EE-maximizing precoding algorithms can be employed to optimize transmit power distribution while considering nonlinear effects as additional noise. This approach enables efficient use of power resources while maintaining acceptable signal-to-noise ratios.
