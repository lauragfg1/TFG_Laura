TOPIC: What redundancy mechanisms are critical for maintaining network efficiency in balloon-based HAPS with passive station-keeping?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The critical redundancy mechanisms for maintaining network efficiency in balloon-based HAPS with passive station-keeping are: (1) Redundant communication links to ensure continuous data exchange, and (2) Duplication of essential systems such as power, navigation, and control. This is due to the limitations of passive station-keeping, which relies on atmospheric conditions for stability.
