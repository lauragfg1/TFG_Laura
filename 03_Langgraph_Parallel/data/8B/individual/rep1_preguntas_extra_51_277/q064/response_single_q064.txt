TOPIC: How do GEO and LEO orbital altitudes impact link budget calculations for Ka vs S-band deployments in 5G satcom?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
GEO (Geostationary Earth Orbit) and LEO (Low Earth Orbit) altitudes impact link budget calculations for Ka vs S-band deployments in 5G satcom due to differences in signal propagation delay, free space path loss, and atmospheric attenuation. GEO orbits (~36,000 km) have longer delays and higher losses, favoring S-band's lower frequency (2-4 GHz). LEO orbits (~500-2000 km) reduce these effects, making Ka-band (26.5-40 GHz) more suitable for shorter-range, high-data-rate applications.
