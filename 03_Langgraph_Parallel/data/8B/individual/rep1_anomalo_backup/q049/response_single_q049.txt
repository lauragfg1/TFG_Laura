TOPIC: How does L-DACS integration impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
L-DACS integration may impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces due to the potential for frequency reuse. However, proper coordination is required to prevent interference, especially when operating on the same frequencies. The use of L-DACS1's self-adaptive slotted TDMA protocol may provide a solution for efficient data transmission while minimizing spectral overlap.
