TOPIC: How does defocused feed array placement optimize coverage versus scan loss in DBF architectures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Defocused feed array placement optimizes coverage versus scan loss in DBF (Digital Beamforming) architectures by reducing the impact of individual or multiple feed element failures. The defocused reflector concept, as described in [a-reflector-antenna-concept-robust-against-feed-failures-for-satellite-communications.txt], exhibits robustness when feed elements drop out, leading to a lower gain loss compared to conventional reflectors.
