TOPIC: How does the LPF's 25 GHz stopband impact interference mitigation in multi-beam satellite downlink frequency planning?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The LPF's (Low-Pass Filter) 25 GHz stopband can impact interference mitigation in multi-beam satellite downlink frequency planning by attenuating signals outside its passband. Given the Context specifies Ka band frequencies (27.5 - 31.0 GHz for uplink and 17.7 - 19.7 GHz for downlink), a filter with a 25 GHz stopband would not directly impact these frequencies, but could influence adjacent frequency bands.
