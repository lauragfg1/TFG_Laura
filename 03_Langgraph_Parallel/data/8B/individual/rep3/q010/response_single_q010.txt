TOPIC: How do interference mitigation techniques adapt when handling tunneled versus non-tunneled traffic in multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Interference mitigation techniques in multi-beam satellite systems adapt by adjusting power allocation and beamforming to minimize interference between beams. For tunneled traffic, techniques such as analog beamforming and precoding can be used to reduce intra-system interference. Non-tunneled traffic may require more complex approaches, including digital signal processing and adaptive modulation.
