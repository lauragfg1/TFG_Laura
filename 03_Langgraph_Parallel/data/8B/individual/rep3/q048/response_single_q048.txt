TOPIC: What antenna designs balance aerodynamic constraints with MIMO requirements for high-speed aircraft communication?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Large-scale MIMO systems with hundreds of antennas can be accommodated within aircraft airframes, offering high data rates, spectrum efficiency, energy efficiency, and low latency. The antenna design must balance aerodynamic constraints with MIMO requirements, considering factors such as frequency band, modulation techniques, antenna size, gain, beam width, and interference effects.
