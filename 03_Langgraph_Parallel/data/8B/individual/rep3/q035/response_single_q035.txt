TOPIC: How can frequency planning address interference between satellite uplinks and broadband power line communication systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Frequency planning can address interference between satellite uplinks and broadband power line communication systems by allocating non-overlapping frequency bands. The satellite frequency bands specified in the context are S (2.65-2.69 GHz), L (1.635-1.66 GHz), Ku (14.0-14.5 GHz), Ka (27.5-31.0 GHz), and C (5.9-6.4 GHz) for downlink frequencies. Power line communication systems typically operate in the 10-500 MHz range, which is far from these satellite frequency bands.
