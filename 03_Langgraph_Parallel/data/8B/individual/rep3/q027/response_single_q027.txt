TOPIC: What power allocation trade-offs arise from differing scan losses between quasi-TEM and quasi-TE1 modes for multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The differing scan losses between quasi-TEM and quasi-TE1 modes in multi-beam satellite systems result in power allocation trade-offs due to variations in antenna gain, beamwidth, and radiation patterns. This leads to unequal power distribution among beams, necessitating dynamic power allocation strategies to maintain optimal system performance.
