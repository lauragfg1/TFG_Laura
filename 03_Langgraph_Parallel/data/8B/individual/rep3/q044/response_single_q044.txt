TOPIC: How does the 14.6% bandwidth affect frequency planning for multi-satellite networks with adjacent channel interference risks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 14.6% bandwidth affects frequency planning by increasing the risk of adjacent channel interference due to reduced spectral separation between channels. This can lead to increased bit error rates and decreased system capacity. To mitigate this, multi-satellite networks may employ techniques such as power backoff, filtering, or more stringent frequency allocation policies.
