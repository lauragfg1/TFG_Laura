TOPIC: What beam allocation strategies optimize spectrum efficiency in hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To optimize spectrum efficiency in hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand, beam allocation strategies should prioritize dynamic resource allocation based on time-variant traffic distribution. This involves allocating more bandwidth to cells with hot traffic and utilizing techniques like spectrum sharing or dynamic spectrum access to maximize energy efficiency.
