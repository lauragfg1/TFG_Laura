TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows due to potential interference between satellites and ground stations operating on the same frequencies. This can occur in scenarios where satellites cross the equator line, causing beams to point towards the nadir direction. Effective frequency allocation and coordination are essential to minimize interference and ensure efficient use of the available spectrum.
