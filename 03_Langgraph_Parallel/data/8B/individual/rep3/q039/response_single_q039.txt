TOPIC: How do MPLS and IPsec VPNs differ in mitigating congestion for real-time QoS in multi-telectric utility backbones?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
MPLS and IPsec VPNs differ in mitigating congestion for real-time QoS in multi-electric utility backbones. MPLS relies on traffic aggregates, prioritizing packets based on DSCP fields, whereas IPsec VPNs provide end-to-end encryption and authentication, allowing for more granular control over QoS requirements.
