TOPIC: What feed element spacing balance minimizes dB loss while maintaining beamforming flexibility in defocused systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To minimize dB loss while maintaining beamforming flexibility in defocused systems, feed element spacing should balance between pattern optimization and robustness against feed failures. A suitable approach is to use a feed per beam (FPB) structure with a moderate spacing of approximately half the wavelength, ensuring sufficient beamforming flexibility without compromising on gain.
