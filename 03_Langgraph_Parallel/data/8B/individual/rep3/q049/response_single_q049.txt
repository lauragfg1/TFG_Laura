TOPIC: How does L-DACS integration impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
L-DACS integration may impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces due to potential interference. The use of L-band frequencies by both systems could lead to frequency reuse issues, particularly if not managed properly. Tight coordination is required for efficient spectrum sharing, especially with the increasing scarcity of radio spectrum.
