MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Defocused feed array placement optimizes coverage versus scan loss in DBF (Digital Beamforming) architectures by reducing the impact of failed elements. The defocused reflector concept, as shown in Fig. 2 and Table II, exhibits improved robustness against feed failures compared to conventional reflectors.
