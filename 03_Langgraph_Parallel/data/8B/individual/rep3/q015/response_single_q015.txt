TOPIC: How do distributed satellite constellations impact orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Distributed satellite constellations impact orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation by requiring more complex synchronization and resource allocation strategies. As the number of satellites increases, the likelihood of interference also grows, necessitating efficient power allocation and admission control techniques to mitigate its effects.
