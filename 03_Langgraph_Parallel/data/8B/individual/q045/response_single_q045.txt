TOPIC: Could the hybrid feeding structure introduce scalability challenges in dynamic payload power allocation for large-scale arrays?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Yes, the hybrid feeding structure can introduce scalability challenges in dynamic payload power allocation for large-scale arrays. As the array size increases, the length of SIW (Substrate Integrated Waveguide) increases, leading to high insertion loss. This can limit the flexibility of the system and affect the total system performance.
