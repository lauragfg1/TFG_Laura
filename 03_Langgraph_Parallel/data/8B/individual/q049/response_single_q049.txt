TOPIC: How does L-DACS integration impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
L-DACS integration impacts spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces by utilizing the L-band frequency range, which is shared with A2G communication systems. L-DACS1 and L-DACS2 systems operate in the 960-1215 MHz and 1215-1400 MHz frequency bands, respectively. The use of TDD technique in L-DACS2 and TDMA protocol in L-DACS1 allows for efficient spectrum sharing and minimizes interference between A2G and satellite links.
