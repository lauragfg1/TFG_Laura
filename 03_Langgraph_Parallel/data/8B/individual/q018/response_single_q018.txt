TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows due to the risk of in-line interference between satellites and ground stations. This can be mitigated by implementing dynamic frequency allocation (DFA) and adaptive resource management (ARM) techniques. The frequency reuse factor (FRF) should be optimized to minimize interference, considering the satellite's beamwidth and the ground station's antenna gain. The ITU-R Rec. S.1426-1 provides guidelines for frequency planning in satellite systems.
