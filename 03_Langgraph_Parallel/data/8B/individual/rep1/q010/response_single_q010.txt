TOPIC: How do interference mitigation techniques adapt when handling tunneled versus non-tunneled traffic in multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In multi-beam satellite systems, interference mitigation techniques adapt differently for tunneled versus non-tunneled traffic. For non-tunneled traffic, beamforming and frequency reuse are employed to minimize inter-cell interference. For tunneled traffic, advanced signal processing techniques such as precoding and post-processing are used to mitigate intra-cell interference.
