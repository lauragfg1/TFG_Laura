TOPIC: How do interference mitigation techniques adapt when handling tunneled versus non-tunneled traffic in multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Interference mitigation techniques in multi-beam satellite systems are crucial for handling both tunneled and non-tunneled traffic. When dealing with tunneled traffic, interference mitigation techniques focus on optimizing beamforming to minimize cross-talk between adjacent beams. For non-tunneled traffic, these techniques prioritize frequency band separation and orthogonal polarization to reduce inter-cell interference.
