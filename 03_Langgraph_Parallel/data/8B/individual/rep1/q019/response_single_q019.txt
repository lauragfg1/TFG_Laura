TOPIC: How do on-board storage constraints influence payload power allocation between immediate and time-tagged service prioritization?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
On-board storage constraints influence payload power allocation by prioritizing immediate services, which do not occupy on-board memory, over time-tagged services. Time-tagged services require data packets to be stored on limited on-board memory between upload completion and operation starting times. This constraint necessitates efficient resource management to ensure sufficient capacity for both service types.
