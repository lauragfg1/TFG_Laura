TOPIC: How does the LPF's 25 GHz stopband impact interference mitigation in multi-beam satellite downlink frequency planning?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The LPF's (Low-Pass Filter) 25 GHz stopband can significantly impact interference mitigation in multi-beam satellite downlink frequency planning. Since the Ka band is specified for uplink frequencies (27.5-31.0 GHz), and not explicitly mentioned for downlink, assuming a similar band might be used. In this case, the LPF's stopband would likely fall within or near the adjacent beam interference (ABI) region, potentially exacerbating ABI effects.
