TOPIC: Could the hybrid feeding structure introduce scalability challenges in dynamic payload power allocation for large-scale arrays?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Yes, the hybrid feeding structure can introduce scalability challenges in dynamic payload power allocation for large-scale arrays due to increased complexity and interbeam interference. As mentioned in the Context, interbeam interference is dependent on the power allocated to each beam, affecting system performance and limiting flexibility.
