TOPIC: How does defocused reflector gain loss impact link budget margins for SNR compared to conventional designs?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The defocused reflector gain loss impacts link budget margins for SNR by introducing a significant broadening and a gain loss in the order of 6 dB, as observed in Fig. 2a. This is due to multipath propagation between the reflector and feed array, which cannot be operated with high co-polar isolation in frequency band reuse scenarios.
