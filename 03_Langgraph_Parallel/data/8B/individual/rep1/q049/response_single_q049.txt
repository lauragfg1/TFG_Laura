TOPIC: How does L-DACS integration impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
L-DACS integration may impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces due to frequency reuse. If not managed properly, both networks can interfere with each other, requiring tight coordination between operators for shared spectrum management.
