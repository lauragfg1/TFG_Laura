TOPIC: What antenna design considerations optimize ground station visibility during short service windows in LEO constellations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To optimize ground station visibility during short service windows in LEO constellations, consider using antennas with narrow beamwidths (half-power beam width, HPBW) to minimize satellite-to-ground distance and increase signal strength. The inclination of 74º and orbit altitude of around 900 km suggest a high latitude area coverage requirement, necessitating antenna designs that compensate for the increased path loss.
