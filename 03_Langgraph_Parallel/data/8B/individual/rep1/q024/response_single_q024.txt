TOPIC: What are the trade-offs in using additive manufacturing for CTS array fabrication versus traditional methods in terms of deployment cost and scalability?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Additive manufacturing (AM) for CTS array fabrication offers advantages in terms of design freedom, compactness, mass, simplicity, cost, and production time. However, it introduces surface roughness that affects microwave performance, potentially increasing losses. Traditional methods may provide better control over thickness and surface impedance but are limited by split block approach shortcomings.
