TOPIC: What interference mitigation techniques are viable for overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Interference mitigation techniques viable for overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations include: Adaptive Frequency Reuse (AFR) to reduce co-channel interference, and Analog Beamforming to suppress multipath interference. Additionally, Rate Splitting Multiple Access (RSMA) can be used to mitigate interference in high traffic density environments.
