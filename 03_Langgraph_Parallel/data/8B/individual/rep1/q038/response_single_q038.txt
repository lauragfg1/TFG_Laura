TOPIC: What beam allocation strategies optimize spectrum efficiency in hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To optimize spectrum efficiency in hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand, adaptive beam allocation strategies are essential. The Context suggests using multibeam antenna technology to fulfill spectrum reuse between various cells. Each beam's wireless resource should be allocated adaptively according to heterogeneous traffic density. In time division multiplex satellite systems, rational time slot and transmit power resource allocation are crucial.
