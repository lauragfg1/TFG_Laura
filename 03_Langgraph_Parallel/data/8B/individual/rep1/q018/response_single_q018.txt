TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows, including potential in-line interference between geostationary (GSO) and non-geostationary (NGSO) satellites. This can occur due to their beams pointing to the same location and operating on the same frequency.
