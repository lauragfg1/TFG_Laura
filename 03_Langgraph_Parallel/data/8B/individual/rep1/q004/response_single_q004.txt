TOPIC: How would the 0.25 dB passband loss affect link budget margins in low-noise satellite receiver frontends?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
A 0.25 dB passband loss in low-noise satellite receiver frontends would decrease link budget margins by reducing the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR). This is because a lower SNR means that more power is required to achieve the same level of noise performance, increasing the risk of saturation and distortion.
