TOPIC: Can MVDR beamforming sufficiently compensate for pattern degradation in array-fed reflectors during element failures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
MVDR beamforming can partially compensate for pattern degradation in array-fed reflectors during element failures, but it is not sufficient to completely restore the original gain patterns. The defocused reflector concept shows improved robustness against feed failures compared to conventional reflectors.
