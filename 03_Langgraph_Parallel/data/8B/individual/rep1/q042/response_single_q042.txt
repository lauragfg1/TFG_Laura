TOPIC: How does the 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna impact link budget calculations for GEO-LEO satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna impacts link budget calculations by directly affecting the received power. Since the received power is inversely proportional to the square of the distance and directly proportional to the antenna gain, a more efficient antenna will result in higher received power. This means that for GEO-LEO satellite links, a 41% efficient antenna will increase the link margin, allowing for longer communication distances or increased data rates.
