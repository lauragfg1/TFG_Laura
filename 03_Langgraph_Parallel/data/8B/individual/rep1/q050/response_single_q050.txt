TOPIC: What beamforming strategies ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations, adaptive beamforming strategies that account for rapidly fluctuating frequency selective fading and Rician distributed fading are necessary. These strategies should exploit the global time reference provided by GPS for synchronization among network nodes, enabling scheduling of contention-free transmissions.
