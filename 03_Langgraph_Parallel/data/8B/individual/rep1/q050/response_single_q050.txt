TOPIC: What beamforming strategies ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To ensure consistent LOS (Line-of-Sight) connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations, adaptive beamforming strategies that account for Rician distributed fading and rapidly fluctuating frequency selective fading are necessary. Techniques like Dynamic Beamforming Schemes with Non-Stationary Spatial Channel Properties should be employed to mitigate the impact of visibility regions on the antenna array.
