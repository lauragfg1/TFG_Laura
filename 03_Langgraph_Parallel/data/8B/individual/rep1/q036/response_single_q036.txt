TOPIC: What link budget trade-offs exist when balancing data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
When balancing data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links, link budget trade-offs exist between increasing transmission power to compensate for atmospheric attenuation and reducing modulation order or using error correction codes to maintain reliability. High atmospheric attenuation due to gases, rain, and clouds requires high transmission powers above 50 W to support high modulation orders.
