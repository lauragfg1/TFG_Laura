TOPIC: What antenna design considerations minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration, consider using antennas with a narrow bandwidth and a high Q-factor to reduce noise sensitivity. Implementing a filter or notch to reject interfering frequencies can also help. Additionally, employing an antenna with a low impedance match to the power line can mitigate signal distortion.
