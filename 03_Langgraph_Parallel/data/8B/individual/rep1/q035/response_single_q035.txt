TOPIC: How can frequency planning address interference between satellite uplinks and broadband power line communication systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Frequency planning can address interference between satellite uplinks and broadband power line communication systems by allocating non-overlapping frequency bands. Considering the frequency bands allocated to satellite communications (e.g., 2.65-2.69 GHz for S-band, 1.635-1.66 GHz for L-band), it is essential to avoid using these frequencies for power line communication systems to minimize interference.
