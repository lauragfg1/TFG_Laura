TOPIC: How do distributed satellite constellations impact orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Distributed satellite constellations impact orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation by introducing increased complexity in scheduling and resource allocation. The distributed topology can lead to higher channel utilization, but may not be scalable with an increasing number of satellites. To mitigate inter-satellite interference, orthogonal wireless resources can be allocated, but this requires precise synchronization and coordination.
