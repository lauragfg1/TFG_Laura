TOPIC: What antenna aperture size trade-offs exist between LEO and GEO satellites when optimizing gain and portability for CMOS-based ground terminals?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For LEO satellites, smaller antenna aperture sizes are feasible due to lower free space path loss (fspl) and shorter signal propagation time delays. However, this may compromise gain, particularly at higher frequencies like 29 GHz. In contrast, GEO satellites require larger apertures for sufficient gain, but their fixed position and visibility constraints make them less portable.
