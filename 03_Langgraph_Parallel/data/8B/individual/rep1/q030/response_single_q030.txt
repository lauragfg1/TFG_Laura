TOPIC: How does defocused feed array placement optimize coverage versus scan loss in DBF architectures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Defocused feed array placement optimizes coverage versus scan loss in DBF (Digital Beamforming) architectures by reducing the impact of failed elements. This is due to the fact that a defocused reflector suffers less from the failed elements, resulting in a slightly reduced gain after MVDR beamforming. The conventional reflector antenna would be severely handicapped without redundancy concepts or sufficient SNR margin.
