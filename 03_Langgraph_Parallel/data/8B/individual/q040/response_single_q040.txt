TOPIC: How does the hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology balance efficiency and compactness in V-band satellite antenna designs?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology balances efficiency and compactness by optimizing the array size to 16x16 elements, replacing the SIW power divider with a hybrid SIW waveguide power divider for larger arrays, and achieving a 14.6% reflection coefficient bandwidth and 39.2 dBi gain at 59 GHz with 41% efficiency.
