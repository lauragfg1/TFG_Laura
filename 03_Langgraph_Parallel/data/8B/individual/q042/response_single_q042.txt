TOPIC: How does the 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna impact link budget calculations for GEO-LEO satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna impacts link budget calculations by reducing the available power for transmission. This can be accounted for by adjusting the transmit power (Pt) in the link budget equation. For a GEO-LEO satellite link, a 41% efficient antenna would require a 1.5 dB increase in transmit power to maintain the same link margin.
