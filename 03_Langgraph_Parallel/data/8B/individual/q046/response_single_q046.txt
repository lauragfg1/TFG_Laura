TOPIC: What beam management strategies are needed to mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band, beam management strategies such as adaptive beamforming, beam steering, and beamforming with interference cancellation are required. Additionally, techniques like massive MIMO, hybrid beamforming, and analog beamforming with phase shifters can be employed to improve spectral efficiency and reduce fluctuations.
