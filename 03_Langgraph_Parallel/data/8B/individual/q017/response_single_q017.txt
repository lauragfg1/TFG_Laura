TOPIC: How are link budgets affected when prioritizing high-revenue services versus navigation safety margins in dynamic scheduling?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
When prioritizing high-revenue services versus navigation safety margins in dynamic scheduling, link budgets are affected by the trade-off between available capacity, latency, and jitter. High-revenue services may require higher bandwidth and lower latency, increasing the link budget's power consumption and heat dissipation. Navigation safety margins, on the other hand, prioritize low latency and high reliability, which can lead to increased link budget complexity and power consumption.
