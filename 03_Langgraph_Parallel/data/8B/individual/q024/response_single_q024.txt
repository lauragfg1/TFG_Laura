TOPIC: What are the trade-offs in using additive manufacturing for CTS array fabrication versus traditional methods in terms of deployment cost and scalability?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Additive manufacturing (AM) for CTS array fabrication offers advantages in terms of design freedom, reduced size, weight, and cost. However, it introduces surface roughness affecting microwave performance, which can be mitigated by characterizing and optimizing the AM process. Traditional methods, such as milling and electroerosion, are limited to E-band and unsuitable for complex structures. AM enables Ka-band operation, but deployment cost and scalability may be higher due to equipment and material costs.
