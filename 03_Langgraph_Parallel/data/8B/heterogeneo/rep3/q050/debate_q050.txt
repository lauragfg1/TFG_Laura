The rapidly fluctuating frequency selective fading, combined with the high velocity of aircraft and Rician distributed fading, poses significant difficulties in maintaining stable connectivity among aircraft.

[PNM] Dynamic beamforming strategies are essential for maintaining consistent LOS connectivity in A2A links, particularly in dynamically changing flight formations. To mitigate frequency selective fading, adaptive equalization or filtering algorithms can be implemented at the receiver to compensate for the channel dispersion. For Rician distributed fading, techniques such as spatial diversity, transmit diversity, or precoding can be used to improve link reliability by reducing the impact of deep fades. Additionally, exploiting global time references provided by GPS for synchronization among network nodes can help in scheduling contention-free transmissions and improving overall connectivity.
