We are discussing the trade-offs in using Additive Manufacturing (AM) for Continuous Transverse Stub (CTS) array fabrication versus traditional methods in terms of deployment cost and scalability.

**Invitation to Discussion:**

Experts, let's dive into the technical aspects of AM for CTS array fabrication. What are the advantages and disadvantages of using AM compared to traditional methods, such as milling and electroerosion techniques? How do the resolution and tolerances of AM impact the microwave performance of CTS arrays?
------------------------------
[DLB] In terms of deployment cost, AM offers lower initial setup costs but higher material and post-processing expenses. For CTS arrays, AM's fine resolution reduces surface roughness, enhancing microwave performance. However, the complexity of AM-produced corrugations can introduce additional fabrication costs. Traditional milling and electroerosion, while more expensive initially, ensure precise thickness control, impacting inductance and capacitance, and thus array factor and interference.
------------------------------
[PNM] In terms of deployment cost and scalability, AM offers several advantages over traditional methods for CTS array fabrication. AM allows for complex designs with original tridimensional shapes, which can result in substantial improvements in terms of compactness, mass, simplicity, and production time. This can lead to reduced manufacturing costs and increased scalability, as AM allows for rapid prototyping and mass production of identical parts. However, AM may introduce additional costs due to the need for specialized equipment and materials, and the resolution and tolerances of AM can impact the microwave performance of CTS arrays, potentially leading to increased losses and reduced efficiency. Additionally, AM may not be suitable for all components of a CTS array, as some components may require more precise machining techniques. Overall, the trade-offs between AM and traditional methods will depend on the specific requirements of the CTS array and the cost and availability of the necessary equipment and materials.
------------------------------
