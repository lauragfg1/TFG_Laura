Larger arrays also increase the physical footprint, impacting spacecraft design and cost.

Larger array sizes, such as those exceeding 16x16, can lead to higher insertion loss due to the increased length of SIW, which results in a significant drop in radiation efficiency from approximately 70% to 27.5%. This is the first experimental comparison of radiation efficiency for SIW arrays with different aperture sizes.
