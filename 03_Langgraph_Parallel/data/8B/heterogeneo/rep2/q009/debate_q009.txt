TOPIC: What payload transponder allocation strategies balance spectral efficiency with dynamic QoS demands from ML traffic classification?
==================================================

[MODERATOR] **
------------------------------
[DLB] The payload transponder allocation strategies must balance spectral efficiency with dynamic QoS demands. For instance, ECMP approaches distribute traffic across multiple links to optimize bandwidth usage, reducing fixed latency impacts but increasing complexity in path selection. Conversely, DVB-S2/RCS2 assumes all satellite traffic, simplifying path calculation but limiting flexibility. The choice impacts budget in terms of performance: complex algorithms reduce overhead but increase processing costs, while simpler allocations may underutilize capacity, affecting spectral efficiency and overall cost.
------------------------------
[PNM] To balance spectral efficiency with dynamic QoS demands from ML traffic classification in satellite networks, payload transponder allocation strategies should employ QoS-OSPF [74] with RSVP [57] for path calculation and resource blocking along the calculated path. ECMP approaches [97]–[100] can be utilized for distributing traffic onto multiple links to improve spectral efficiency, while ensuring QoS guarantees. Application requirements such as capacity, latency, and jitter can be signaled to enable admission control, enhancing scalability and prioritizing traffic. However, high fixed latencies on links may negatively impact QoS and are not considered by these strategies.
------------------------------
[MODERATOR] **Summary of Key Points:**

* Payload transponder allocation strategies must balance spectral efficiency with dynamic QoS demands.
* ECMP approaches optimize bandwidth usage but increase complexity in path selection.
* DVB-S2/RCS2 simplifies path calculation but limits flexibility.
