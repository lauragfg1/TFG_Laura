[DLB] **Frequency Planning Challenge:**

The 14.6% bandwidth (approximately 200 MHz) in the Ku band poses significant challenges for frequency planning due to adjacent channel interference risks. To mitigate this, we can employ techniques such as:

1. **Frequency Reuse**: Allocating the same frequency band to multiple satellites with careful planning to minimize interference.
2. **Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing (OFDM)**: Utilizing OFDM to divide the available bandwidth into multiple sub-carriers, reducing adjacent channel interference.
3. **Cyclic Prefix (CP) Insertion**: Adding a CP to each symbol to mitigate inter-symbol interference and reduce the impact of adjacent channel interference.

These techniques can help optimize frequency allocation within the limited bandwidth, minimizing interference and ensuring reliable communication between satellites.
