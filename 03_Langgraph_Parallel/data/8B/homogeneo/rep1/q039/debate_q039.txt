[PNM] **MPLS-based VPNs**

In MPLS-based VPNs, traffic is routed based on pre-computed paths with sufficient bandwidth. This approach ensures efficient resource allocation but may lead to resources blocking along calculated paths. Additionally, link up/down detection and application signaling of required capacity are supported.

**IPsec VPNs**

IPsec VPNs provide secure communication by encrypting and authenticating data packets. However, they do not natively support QoS guarantees or admission control mechanisms, relying on underlying network protocols for congestion management. This may lead to reduced scalability and increased latency in high-traffic scenarios.

**Key differences**

MPLS-based VPNs prioritize traffic based on pre-computed paths, while IPsec VPNs rely on underlying network protocols for congestion management. MPLS-based VPNs offer more efficient resource allocation but may be less scalable than IPsec VPNs in high-traffic environments.
