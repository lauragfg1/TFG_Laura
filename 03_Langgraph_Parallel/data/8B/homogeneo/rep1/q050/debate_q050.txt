TOPIC: What beamforming strategies ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations?
==================================================

[MODERATOR] **Technical Problem:**

Ensuring consistent Line of Sight (LOS) connectivity for Air-to-Air (A2A) links in dynamically changing flight formations is crucial for reliable communication between aircraft. The rapidly fluctuating frequency selective fading, high velocity, and Rician distributed fading characteristics of the aeronautical channel pose significant challenges to beamforming strategies.

**Discussion Invitation:**

Experts from both academia and industry, please join us in discussing effective beamforming strategies that can adapt to the dynamic flight formations and ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links. What techniques or approaches have you employed or researched to address this challenge?
------------------------------
[DLB] **Technical Insight:**

The rapidly fluctuating frequency selective fading, high velocity, and Rician distributed fading characteristics of the aeronautical channel pose significant challenges to beamforming strategies for A2A links. The budget implication is a reduced signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) due to increased interference from adjacent beams and satellite noise.

**Physical Mechanism:**

The array factor of the antenna array is affected by the dynamic flight formations, causing a shift in the main lobe direction. This results in increased side-lobe levels, leading to co-channel interference and reduced SNR.

**Budget Implication:**

The reduced SNR translates to a higher bit-error-rate (BER), which increases the required transmit power, resulting in a higher energy consumption and decreased system capacity.

These approaches can mitigate the effects of frequency selective fading and ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links.
