[DLB] **Answer**

To optimize spectrum efficiency in hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand, beam allocation strategies should focus on minimizing inter-beam interference. This can be achieved by allocating beams with orthogonal polarization, reducing the beamwidth, and increasing the inter-beam spacing. The cost of these design choices is a reduction in system capacity, as the number of beams that can be supported is decreased. However, this is offset by improved spectrum efficiency, resulting in a net gain in performance. The budget implications of this design choice are a trade-off between capacity and interference management.
