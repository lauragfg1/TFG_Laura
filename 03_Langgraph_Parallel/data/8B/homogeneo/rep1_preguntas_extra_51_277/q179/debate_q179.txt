The orbital configuration affects frequency planning trade-offs through Doppler shift, which depends on satellite velocity (v) and frequency (f). For a constellation at altitude h, the maximum Doppler shift is Δf = 2v \* f / c, where c is the speed of light. This shift increases with lower altitudes and higher velocities.
