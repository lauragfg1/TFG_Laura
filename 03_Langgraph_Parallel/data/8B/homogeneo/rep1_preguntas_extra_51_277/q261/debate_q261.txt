[DLB] **Beam Gain Efficiency vs Robustness Trade-offs**

To increase beam gain efficiency, multibeam antennas can be designed with smaller spacing between elements, but this increases interference due to grating lobes. Conversely, larger spacing reduces interference but decreases beam gain efficiency. The cost of this trade-off is a reduction in system capacity and an increase in required transmission power (M N AUP). In the face of phase perturbations, robust beamforming techniques can mitigate interference effects, but at the expense of additional computational complexity and potential performance degradation due to imperfect CSI.
