[DLB] Given the context, I'll focus on the budget implications of inter-beam interference (IBI) in full frequency reuse scenarios. The critical aspect is that IBI moves the link from a power-limited to an interference-limited regime, reducing the effective SINR.

**Physical Mechanism:** In FFR, adjacent beams reuse the same frequency band, causing co-channel interference. This interference reduces the signal strength at the receiver, making it harder for users at beam edges to maintain a good SINR.

**Budget Implications:**

1. **Capacity Reduction**: IBI leads to a reduction in capacity due to increased interference.
2. **Increased Power Consumption**: To compensate for IBI, transmitters must increase power, leading to higher energy consumption and potential thermal issues.
3. **Link Budget Margin Reduction**: The existing link budget margins are not sufficient to handle the SINR degradation caused by accumulated IBI.

These implications highlight the need for innovative solutions to mitigate IBI in FFR scenarios, such as advanced beamforming techniques or more efficient resource allocation algorithms.
