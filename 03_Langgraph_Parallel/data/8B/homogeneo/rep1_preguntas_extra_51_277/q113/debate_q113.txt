[DLB] **Redundancy Mechanisms for HAPS with Passive Station-Keeping**

Given the harsh weather conditions, limited autonomy, and high latency of HAPS, redundancy mechanisms are critical to maintain network efficiency. To minimize budget implications on performance, capacity, and interference:

* Use **Array Factor Diversity**: Implement multiple antenna arrays with different beamforming patterns to increase diversity gain and reduce interference.
* Employ **Interference-Aware Resource Allocation**: Allocate resources based on real-time interference measurements to minimize co-channel interference.
* Optimize **Spacing Effects**: Adjust antenna spacing to balance signal-to-noise ratio, mutual coupling, and far-field effects.

These mechanisms ensure reliable communication services while minimizing the budget implications of design choices.
