[DLB] **Mechanical Beam Scanning vs Phased Arrays for Dynamic Traffic**

Mechanical beam scanning increases power consumption, leading to thermal management issues and reduced lifespan. In contrast, phased arrays offer flexible beamforming with lower power requirements. However, they introduce additional complexity and cost due to the need for multiple RF chains and high-precision timing control.

**Budget Implications:**
Phased arrays incur higher "cost" in terms of increased signal processing complexity (due to more RF chains) and reduced spectral efficiency (due to beamforming overhead). Mechanical beam scanning incurs higher "cost" in terms of power consumption and thermal management.
