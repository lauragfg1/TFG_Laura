[DLB] **Operational Constraints Limiting Optimal Beam-to-Beam Channel Allocation**

The key operational constraint is the limited processing frequency bandwidth (BW) of digital channelizers, which restricts the number of beams that can be simultaneously processed. For example, in mesh-type communication, a satellite with 10 beams may have only 250 MHz of available BW per beam (see Context). This limitation affects the system's ability to allocate channels efficiently between beams, leading to reduced flexibility and capacity.

**Impact on System Performance**

The limited processing frequency bandwidth reduces the system's ability to adapt to changing traffic demands, resulting in:

1. Reduced average satisfaction index (ASI) due to unused offered capacity.
2. Increased average unmet system capacity (AUSC).
3. Decreased average used bandwidth (AUB).

These constraints highlight the need for optimal channel allocation strategies that account for these limitations and maximize system performance.
