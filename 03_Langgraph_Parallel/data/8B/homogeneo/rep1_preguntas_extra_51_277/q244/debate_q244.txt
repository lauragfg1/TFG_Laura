[DLB] To optimize coverage versus frequency reuse efficiency, consider the trade-off between:

* Increasing the number of beams (M) to improve frequency reuse, but reducing the beamforming network complexity and increasing the cost of the antenna system.
* Using shaped reflector antennas with single feed per beam (SFPB) architecture, which provides high gain and low side lobe level, but requires 3 or 4 reflectors, leaving little space for additional missions.
* Employing multiple feeds per beam (MFPB) architecture, which achieves contiguous coverage with one or two main reflectors, but may lead to lower aperture efficiency and increased complexity.

Optimizing coverage versus frequency reuse efficiency leads to trade-offs between:

* Larger reflectors (more complex architecture) → Higher aperture efficiency, but increased cost and complexity
