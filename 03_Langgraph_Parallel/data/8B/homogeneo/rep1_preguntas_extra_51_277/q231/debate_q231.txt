[DLB] **Impact of Full Frequency Reuse on Interference**

In multibeam satellites, full frequency reuse increases co-channel interference (CCI) between adjacent beams due to overlapping frequencies. This CCI degrades signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), reducing system capacity and increasing the required transmit power.

**Physical Mechanism: Array Factor**

The array factor of a satellite's antenna pattern determines the inter-beam interference. With full frequency reuse, the array factor of adjacent beams aligns, causing constructive interference between them. This increases the CCI, degrading SNR and reducing system capacity.

**Budget Implication: Reduced Capacity**

Full frequency reuse reduces the available capacity per beam due to increased CCI. To mitigate this, more power is required, further reducing capacity. The "budget" of reduced performance and capacity must be considered when designing systems with full frequency reuse.
