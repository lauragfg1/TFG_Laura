[PNM] [MODERATOR] **

During peak congestion in hybrid satellite-aerial networks, managing power distribution is crucial to prevent capacity bottlenecks. The dynamic allocation of resources between satellite and terrestrial links exacerbates this issue due to varying latency and jitter characteristics on satellite links. Moreover, the lack of coordination between satellite and terrestrial network operators can lead to resource competition and interference, further straining power distribution. Efficient load balancing techniques, such as explicit load balancing (ELB), are essential to distribute traffic based on requirements and link capabilities, ensuring optimal power allocation.
