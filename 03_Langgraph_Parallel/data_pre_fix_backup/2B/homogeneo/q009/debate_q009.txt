To mitigate interference,  transponders should be spaced farther apart, but this increases the cost of the satellite system. The trade-off between spectral efficiency and cost must be carefully considered.  

Furthermore, the transponder's array factor, which determines the directionality and power gain of the signal, is crucial.  A larger array factor can capture more power, leading to higher spectral efficiency, but also increases the cost of the antenna system.  The optimal transponder configuration must strike a balance between these factors.
