TOPIC: What beam allocation strategies optimize spectrum efficiency in hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Dynamic spectrum access and sharing between satellite systems and terrestrial networks are crucial for optimizing spectrum efficiency during peak demand.  Adaptive beam allocation strategies, such as those utilizing Time Division Multiplexing (TDM) and power control, can dynamically allocate resources to cells with high traffic density.  Cognitive satellite communication, where satellites share spectrum with terrestrial networks, is another promising technique for spectrum efficiency.
