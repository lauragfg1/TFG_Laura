TOPIC: What payload transponder allocation strategies balance spectral efficiency with dynamic QoS demands from ML traffic classification?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Spectral efficiency and dynamic QoS demands from ML traffic classification require a dynamic transponder allocation strategy. This strategy should prioritize traffic with higher QoS requirements by dynamically allocating transponders based on real-time traffic analysis.  Metrics like DSCP and TCP flags can be utilized to classify traffic and allocate transponders. The strategy should also consider factors like network congestion and QoS-aware routing.
