TOPIC: What orbital configurations justify prioritizing V-band (57-66 GHz) over optical systems for inter-satellite communication?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
V-band (57-66 GHz) offers a higher data rate and lower latency for inter-satellite communication compared to optical systems.  V-band is capable of transmitting data rates exceeding 10 Gbps, exceeding the capabilities of optical systems.  This is crucial for high-demand applications like real-time data transfers and mission-critical communications.
