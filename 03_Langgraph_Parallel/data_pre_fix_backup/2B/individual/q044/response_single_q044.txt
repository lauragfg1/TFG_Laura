TOPIC: How does the 14.6% bandwidth affect frequency planning for multi-satellite networks with adjacent channel interference risks?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
14.6% bandwidth is a specific percentage of the total available bandwidth. Frequency planning for multi-satellite networks with adjacent channel interference requires careful consideration of bandwidth allocation to minimize interference.  Standards like ITU-R M.1140-2 (2019) provide guidance on channel spacing to mitigate interference.  Utilizing techniques like orthogonal frequency-division multiplex (OFDM) and dynamic channel allocation can further optimize frequency planning.
