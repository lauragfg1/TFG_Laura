TOPIC: What beam management strategies are needed to mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
To mitigate efficiency fluctuations in the 57-66 GHz band, beam management strategies such as massive beamforming are crucial. This approach leverages high-gain beams and optimized beamforming to enhance signal propagation in challenging environments. This can improve spectral efficiency and reduce fluctuations in signal strength. The beamforming strategy should be designed to minimize path loss and blockage effects.  Consider standards like 3GPP Release 15 or beyond, which define the allowable frequency range and specific beamforming techniques for mmWave systems.
