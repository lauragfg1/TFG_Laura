TOPIC: How does the transceiver's variable gain distribution between RF and baseband impact dynamic range and noise performance trade-offs in different satellite orbital configurations?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Variable gain distribution between RF and baseband affects dynamic range by modulating the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) at the receiver. This influences the achievable SNR and, consequently, the data rate. The trade-off between dynamic range and noise performance is dependent on the satellite's orbital configuration, the transceiver's gain control, and the signal-to-noise ratio. Higher gain can improve data rates but also increases noise levels.  Satellite ground-stations are subject to atmospheric noise and signal processing limitations.
