TOPIC: How does the 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna impact link budget calculations for GEO-LEO satellite links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
The 41% antenna efficiency directly impacts link budget calculations by reducing the received power. This reduction in power translates to a lower signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), impacting the achievable data rates and link quality. The specific impact depends on the antenna gain, receiver noise temperature, and other link budget parameters.
