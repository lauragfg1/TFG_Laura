TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Overlapping satellite visibility windows create frequency planning challenges due to the potential for interference between ground stations managing distinct satellite networks.  This can occur when ground stations operate on the same frequencies or are located in close proximity, leading to inter-system interference.  Coordination between operators is crucial to manage these overlapping windows and minimize potential interference.
