TOPIC: How does transponder allocation balance network efficiency against thermal/power constraints during peak service demand periods?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Transponder allocation in satellite systems strives to balance network efficiency and thermal/power constraints through dynamic bandwidth allocation. Techniques like power control and adaptive modulation, guided by standards like SATCOM protocols, ensure efficient utilization of transponders. This mitigates thermal and power constraints during peak demand periods.
