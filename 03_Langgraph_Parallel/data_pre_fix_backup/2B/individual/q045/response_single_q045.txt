TOPIC: Could the hybrid feeding structure introduce scalability challenges in dynamic payload power allocation for large-scale arrays?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Hybrid feeding structures, while potentially increasing efficiency, could introduce scalability challenges for dynamic payload power allocation in large-scale arrays.  The increased complexity of the array necessitates sophisticated control systems to manage power allocation across multiple beams, potentially limiting the flexibility and scalability of the system.
