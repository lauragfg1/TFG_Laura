TOPIC: How does defocused reflector gain loss impact link budget margins for SNR compared to conventional designs?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Defocused reflector systems exhibit a greater gain loss compared to conventional designs, typically around 6 dB, due to the broadened beam patterns. This increased loss translates to a reduction in link budget margins for SNR, particularly noticeable at the center of the beam. This increased loss can be mitigated by additional feed elements at the array's borders or by employing a curved feed array, as shown in Fig. 6.
