TOPIC: How does interleaved array element spacing affect coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression in Ka-band satellite links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Interleaved array element spacing directly impacts coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression in Ka-band satellite links.  The spacing influences the beam's directivity and the relative strength of the side lobes.  A tighter spacing typically results in narrower beams, leading to higher antenna gain and reduced grating lobe suppression.  Conversely, a wider spacing might provide a broader beam with a lower gain.  The impact of interleaved spacing on these aspects is influenced by the specific array configuration and antenna design, with the 20-30 GHz Ka-band being a crucial consideration.
