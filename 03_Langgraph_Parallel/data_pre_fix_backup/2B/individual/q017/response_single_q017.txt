TOPIC: How are link budgets affected when prioritizing high-revenue services versus navigation safety margins in dynamic scheduling?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Link budgets are inherently complex and dynamic, impacting satellite resource allocation. Prioritizing high-revenue services necessitates careful consideration of bandwidth demands, latency requirements, and signal-to-noise ratios. Navigation safety margins demand stringent resource allocation to ensure mission-critical functionality.  Dynamic scheduling, however, presents challenges in achieving optimal balance between these competing priorities.
