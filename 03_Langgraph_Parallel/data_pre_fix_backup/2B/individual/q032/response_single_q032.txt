TOPIC: What feed element spacing balance minimizes dB loss while maintaining beamforming flexibility in defocused systems?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Defocused reflector systems optimize beamforming flexibility and minimize dB loss by employing a spatial arrangement that balances feed element spacing. This minimizes sidelobes and maintains gain while leveraging the system's inherent robustness against individual or multiple feed element failures.
