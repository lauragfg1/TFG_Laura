TOPIC: What interference mitigation techniques are viable for overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Techniques for mitigating interference in overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations include: TDMA-based multiple access schemes, adaptive frequency reuse, and hybrid MAC protocols. These methods leverage frequency allocation, time-division, and pre-allocation strategies to optimize channel access and minimize interference.
