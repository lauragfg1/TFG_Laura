TOPIC: How would the 0.25 dB passband loss affect link budget margins in low-noise satellite receiver frontends?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
0.25 dB passband loss primarily impacts the receiver's sensitivity to signal-to-noise ratio (SNR).  This loss reduces the effective power received, potentially impacting link budget margins, particularly in low-noise receiver frontends.  The impact is amplified in challenging channel conditions.
