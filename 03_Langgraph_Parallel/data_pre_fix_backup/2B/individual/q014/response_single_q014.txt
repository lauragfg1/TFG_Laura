TOPIC: What antenna aperture size trade-offs exist between LEO and GEO satellites when optimizing gain and portability for CMOS-based ground terminals?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
LEO satellites typically utilize smaller antenna apertures than GEO satellites to achieve higher orbital speeds and reduced free space path loss. This trade-off results in a compromise between antenna gain and portability for CMOS-based ground terminals.  The optimal aperture size depends on specific application requirements, such as desired bandwidth and signal-to-noise ratio (SNR).
