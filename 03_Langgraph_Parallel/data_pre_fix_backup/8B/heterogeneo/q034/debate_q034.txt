[PNM] Antenna design plays a crucial role in minimizing signal attenuation in PLC systems. In high-noise power line environments, the use of appropriate antennas can help mitigate signal distortion and improve communication reliability. To achieve this, consider the following antenna design aspects:

1. Frequency selection: Antenna design should be optimized for the specific frequency bands used in the PLC system to reduce signal attenuation.

2. Impedance matching: Proper impedance matching between the antenna and the transmission line is essential to minimize signal reflection and ensure efficient power transfer.

3. Radiation pattern: Antennas with a narrow radiation pattern can help minimize signal leakage and interference to other devices in the power network.

4. Grounding: Adequate grounding of the antenna is necessary to reduce noise and improve signal quality.

5. Antenna placement: Positioning antennas strategically, such as at the center of a power line or at the terminal points, can help reduce signal attenuation and improve coverage.

6. Material selection: Using materials with low loss characteristics, such as low-loss coaxial cable for feed lines, can help minimize signal attenuation.

7. Shielding: Shielding the antenna and feed lines can help reduce noise and improve signal quality.

8. Polarization: Proper polarization matching between the transmitter and receiver antennas can help minimize signal loss due to polarization mismatch.

By addressing these antenna design considerations, it is possible to minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments, thereby improving the reliability of PLC integration.
