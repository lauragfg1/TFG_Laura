TOPIC: What beamforming strategies ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations?
==================================================

[MODERATOR] **Technical Problem:**

Ensuring consistent Line-of-Sight (LOS) connectivity for Air-to-Air (A2A) links in dynamically changing flight formations is a critical challenge in aeronautical ad-hoc networking. The rapidly fluctuating frequency selective fading, combined with the high velocity of aircraft, results in Rician distributed fading, making it difficult to maintain stable connectivity among aircraft.

**Inviting Discussion:**

Experts, let's dive into the technical aspects of beamforming strategies that can ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations. What are the most promising approaches, and how can we mitigate the effects of frequency selective fading and Rician distributed fading?
------------------------------
[DLB] To ensure consistent LOS connectivity in A2A links, beamforming strategies like adaptive beamforming can mitigate Rician fading. By dynamically adjusting the beamforming weights based on the aircraft's position and velocity, the system can optimize the signal path, reducing interference and maintaining performance. This approach incurs higher computational overhead but significantly enhances reliability, especially in high-velocity scenarios. The cost in terms of capacity is minimal as the system can utilize the full bandwidth more efficiently, but the initial implementation cost for advanced processors and algorithms is high.
------------------------------
[PNM] Beamforming strategies for maintaining consistent LOS connectivity in A2A links of aeronautical ad-hoc networks (AANETs) should focus on adaptive beamforming techniques that can mitigate Rician distributed fading and frequency selective fading. Adaptive beamforming algorithms, such as Minimum Mean Square Error (MMSE) and Maximum Likelihood (ML), can adjust the beamforming weights in real-time based on the rapidly changing channel conditions. These algorithms can exploit the global time reference provided by GPS for synchronization among network nodes, enabling contention-free transmissions and minimizing interference. Furthermore, directional antennas can be utilized to enhance the LOS connectivity by focusing the transmitted signal towards the intended receiver, reducing the effects of frequency selective fading. However, the high velocity of aircraft in AANETs necessitates fast beamforming algorithms with low computational complexity to ensure real-time adjustments.

2. **Frequency Selective Fading and Rician Distributed Fading:** Rapidly fluctuating frequency selective fading and Rician distributed fading due to high aircraft velocities and opposite directions of flight make it challenging to maintain LOS connectivity.
