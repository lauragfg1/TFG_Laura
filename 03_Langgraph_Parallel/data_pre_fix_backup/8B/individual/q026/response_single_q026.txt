TOPIC: How does the 45° scan range affect link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users compared to broadside beams?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 45° scan range affects link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users by increasing the free-space path loss due to the increased distance between the antenna and the user. This results in a higher signal attenuation, typically in the range of 1-2 dB, depending on the specific frequency and antenna characteristics. This effect is more pronounced for edge-of-coverage users due to the increased path loss.
