TOPIC: How does the 11.6% bandwidth of the CTS antenna influence frequency planning and spectral efficiency for broadband satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 11.6% bandwidth of the CTS antenna influences frequency planning and spectral efficiency by allowing for a 52.9% relative bandwidth at broadside with an axial ratio (AR) lower than 3 dB. This enables efficient use of spectrum, adhering to the minimum bandwidth of 1 MHz for inter-satellite links, and supports high-throughput configurations with reduced link margin.
