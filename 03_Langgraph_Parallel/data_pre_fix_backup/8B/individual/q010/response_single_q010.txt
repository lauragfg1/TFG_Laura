TOPIC: How do interference mitigation techniques adapt when handling tunneled versus non-tunneled traffic in multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Interference mitigation techniques in multi-beam satellite systems adapt by employing dynamic power allocation, adaptive coding and modulation, and interference cancellation. For tunneled traffic, techniques such as interference cancellation and adaptive modulation are effective. Non-tunneled traffic requires more robust methods, including dynamic power allocation and adaptive coding. The ITU-R recommendations ITU-R S.1431 and ITU-R S.1325 provide guidelines for mitigating inline interference, including satellite diversity and transmission cessation.
