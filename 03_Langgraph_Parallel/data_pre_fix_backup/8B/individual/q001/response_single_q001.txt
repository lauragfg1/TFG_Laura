TOPIC: How does interleaved array element spacing affect coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression in Ka-band satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Interleaved array element spacing affects coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression in Ka-band satellite links by reducing side lobes and improving antenna gain. A spacing of λ/2 to λ/4 is optimal for grating lobe suppression, where λ is the wavelength. This is in accordance with the Ka-band frequency range of 17-21.2 GHz. The use of interleaved array elements also reduces the impact of inter-element spacing on the antenna's radiation pattern.
