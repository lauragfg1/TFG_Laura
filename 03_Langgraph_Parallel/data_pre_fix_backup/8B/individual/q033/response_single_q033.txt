TOPIC: How do GEO vs LEO satellite orbits affect latency and coverage in hybrid networks for rural substation monitoring?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
GEO satellites impose higher latency due to longer round-trip delays (up to 240 ms) compared to LEO satellites (sub-50 ms). LEO satellites offer reduced latency and improved coverage, especially in rural areas, due to their lower altitude and reduced signal delay. This makes LEO satellites more suitable for hybrid networks requiring real-time communication, such as rural substation monitoring.
