TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between array size and radiation efficiency for SIW arrays in high-gain inter-satellite antennas?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For SIW arrays in high-gain inter-satellite antennas, increasing the array size beyond 16x16 leads to a trade-off between good performance and compact configuration. Larger array sizes result in higher insertion loss due to increased SIW length, decreasing radiation efficiency from 70% to 27.5%. This is attributed to the high insertion loss of the substrate, estimated at 0.034 dB/mm at 60 GHz.
