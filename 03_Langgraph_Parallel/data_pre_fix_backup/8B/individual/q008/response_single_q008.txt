TOPIC: How does antenna design influence the accuracy of encrypted traffic classification systems in heterogeneous satellite networks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Antenna design influences the accuracy of encrypted traffic classification systems in heterogeneous satellite networks through signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and frequency stability. A well-designed antenna ensures a stable and strong signal, reducing errors in packet classification. Specifically, the antenna's gain, beamwidth, and polarization can affect the classification accuracy. For example, a 3 dB gain in antenna gain can improve SNR by 2 dB, leading to better classification performance.
