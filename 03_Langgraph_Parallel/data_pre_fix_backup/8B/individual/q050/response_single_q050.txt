TOPIC: What beamforming strategies ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations, adaptive beamforming strategies can be employed. Specifically, large-scale MIMO (Multiple-Input Multiple-Output) aided adaptive modulation schemes can be used to optimize beamforming and maintain LOS connectivity. This can be achieved through the use of 4-8 receive antennas and 16-64 transmit antennas, with a beamforming bandwidth of up to 100 MHz. The adaptive modulation scheme can be based on standards such as 3GPP LTE or IEEE 802.11ad.
