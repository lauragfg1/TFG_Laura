TOPIC: How does defocused reflector gain loss impact link budget margins for SNR compared to conventional designs?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The defocused reflector gain loss of 1.52 dB to 1.70 dB (see fig. 7b) can significantly impact link budget margins for Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) compared to conventional designs. This is because the defocused reflector suffers from a reduced gain, especially in the presence of feed element failures, which can lead to a reduced SNR and increased bit error rate (BER).
