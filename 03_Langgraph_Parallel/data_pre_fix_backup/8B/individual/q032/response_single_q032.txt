TOPIC: What feed element spacing balance minimizes dB loss while maintaining beamforming flexibility in defocused systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
A feed element spacing balance that minimizes dB loss while maintaining beamforming flexibility in defocused systems is typically achieved when the spacing is around 0.6 to 0.8 times the wavelength (λ) of the signal. This balance is critical to ensure that the defocused reflector concept is robust against feed failures and maintains its performance in the presence of element dropouts.
