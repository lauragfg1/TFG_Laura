TOPIC: What orbital configurations justify prioritizing V-band (57-66 GHz) over optical systems for inter-satellite communication?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Orbital configurations with high satellite density and limited inter-satellite connectivity justify prioritizing V-band (57-66 GHz) over optical systems for inter-satellite communication. Specifically, for p ≤ 6, where p is the number of satellites in a plane, and with a communication range to ensure full inter plane connectivity of l(cid:2) adj(7, 40) = 3527 km, V-band offers better performance due to its higher bandwidth and lower latency compared to optical links.
