TOPIC: What antenna design considerations optimize ground station visibility during short service windows in LEO constellations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To optimize ground station visibility during short service windows in LEO constellations, consider the following antenna design considerations: (1) Half Power Beam Width (HPBW) ≤ 10° to minimize the number of satellites required for global coverage, (2) Inclination ≤ 87.5° to reduce the number of satellites in view, and (3) Satellite altitude ≤ 1200 km to increase the visibility time. Refer to TR 38.821 for specific antenna pattern calculations.
