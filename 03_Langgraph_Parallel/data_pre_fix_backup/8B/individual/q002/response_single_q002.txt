TOPIC: How does the LPF's 25 GHz stopband impact interference mitigation in multi-beam satellite downlink frequency planning?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The LPF's 25 GHz stopband can impact interference mitigation in multi-beam satellite downlink frequency planning by introducing out-of-band emissions that may interfere with adjacent frequency bands. This can be mitigated by designing the LPF with a stopband rejection of at least 30 dB, as specified in the ITU-R S.465-6 standard, to ensure compliance with satellite frequency planning regulations.
