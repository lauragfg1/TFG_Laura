TOPIC: What antenna design considerations minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments, antenna design considerations should focus on maximizing gain and minimizing noise coupling. This can be achieved by using antenna designs with high directivity, such as patch antennas or slot antennas, and optimizing their placement to reduce electromagnetic interference (EMI). IEEE Standard 802.15.4 (2003) provides guidelines for PLC antenna design and placement to minimize signal attenuation.
