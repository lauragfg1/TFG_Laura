TOPIC: What beamforming strategies ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Adaptive beamforming strategies, such as phased arrays and MIMO, can ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links. These strategies utilize metrics like SNR and Doppler shift to optimize beam direction and shape, adhering to standards like DO-178C and DO-254 for safety-critical systems.
