TOPIC: How does the LPF's 25 GHz stopband impact interference mitigation in multi-beam satellite downlink frequency planning?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
The 25 GHz stopband of the LPF affects interference mitigation by limiting out-of-band emissions, thus reducing adjacent channel interference in multi-beam satellite downlink frequency planning, adhering to standards like ITU-R S.524-9.
