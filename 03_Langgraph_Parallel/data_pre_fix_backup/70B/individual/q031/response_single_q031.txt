TOPIC: Can MVDR beamforming sufficiently compensate for pattern degradation in array-fed reflectors during element failures?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
MVDR beamforming can partially compensate for pattern degradation, but not sufficiently. Gain loss remains, e.g., 1.52 dB at 0° and 1.70 dB at 3.56°, indicating residual degradation.
