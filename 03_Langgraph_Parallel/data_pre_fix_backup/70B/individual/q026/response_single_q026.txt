TOPIC: How does the 45° scan range affect link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users compared to broadside beams?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
The 45° scan range increases scan losses, affecting link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users. A 2.8 dB scan loss is observed for the outermost beam at 30.5 GHz, impacting signal strength and coverage.
