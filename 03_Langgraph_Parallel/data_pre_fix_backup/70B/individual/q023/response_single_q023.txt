TOPIC: How does the dual-mode operation of the CTS antenna impact frequency reuse and coverage patterns in Ka-band satellite architectures?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Dual-mode CTS antenna enables frequency reuse by allocating same spectrum to multiple beams, increasing coverage gain. Ka-band operation allows smaller antenna sizes, but is susceptible to weather conditions. Beamforming techniques mitigate interference, enhancing spectral efficiency.
