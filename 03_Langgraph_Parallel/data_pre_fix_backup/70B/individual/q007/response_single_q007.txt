TOPIC: Can satellite orbital configurations optimize ML-based QoS by reducing propagation delays for latency-sensitive traffic classes?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Satellite orbital configurations can reduce propagation delays. Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellites have lower latency (~20-30 ms) compared to Geostationary Orbit (GEO) satellites (~240-280 ms), optimizing ML-based QoS for latency-sensitive traffic.
