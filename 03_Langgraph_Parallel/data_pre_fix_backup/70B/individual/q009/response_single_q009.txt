TOPIC: What payload transponder allocation strategies balance spectral efficiency with dynamic QoS demands from ML traffic classification?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Adaptive Coding and Modulation (ACM) and Dynamic Resource Allocation (DRA) strategies balance spectral efficiency with dynamic QoS demands. ACM adjusts modulation and coding based on channel conditions, while DRA allocates resources based on traffic demands, ensuring efficient use of bandwidth and meeting QoS requirements.
