TOPIC: What link budget trade-offs exist when balancing data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Link budget trade-offs for satellite backhaul involve balancing data rate and reliability. Adverse weather conditions, such as rain attenuation, can impact signal strength. To mitigate, operators can adjust modulation schemes, coding rates, or antenna sizes to maintain reliability, potentially at the cost of reduced data rates.
