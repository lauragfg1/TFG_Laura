TOPIC: What antenna design considerations minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
To minimize signal attenuation, antenna designs should consider narrowband frequencies (e.g., 2-30 MHz), directional antennas, and shielding to reduce electromagnetic interference (EMI). IEEE standards (e.g., IEEE 1901) provide guidelines for broadband PLC.
