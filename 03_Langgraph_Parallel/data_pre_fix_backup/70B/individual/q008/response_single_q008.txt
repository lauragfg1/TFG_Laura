TOPIC: How does antenna design influence the accuracy of encrypted traffic classification systems in heterogeneous satellite networks?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Antenna design doesn't directly influence encrypted traffic classification accuracy. Instead, signal processing and machine learning algorithms impact classification. Antenna design affects signal quality, which can indirectly impact classification by altering signal-to-noise ratios, measured in decibels (dB), and bit error rates (BER).
