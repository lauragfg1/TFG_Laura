TOPIC: What antenna design considerations optimize ground station visibility during short service windows in LEO constellations?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Antenna design considerations for LEO constellations include narrow beamwidths, high gain, and beam steering to maximize visibility during short service windows. Half Power Beam Width (HPBW) and antenna pattern definition as per TR 38.821 are crucial for optimal performance.
