TOPIC: How do MPLS and IPsec VPNs differ in mitigating congestion for real-time QoS in multi-telectric utility backbones?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
MPLS mitigates congestion through traffic engineering and QoS guarantees, while IPsec VPNs rely on prioritization and encryption. MPLS provides better support for real-time QoS with guaranteed bandwidth and low latency.
