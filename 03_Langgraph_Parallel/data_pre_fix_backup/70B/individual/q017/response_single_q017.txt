TOPIC: How are link budgets affected when prioritizing high-revenue services versus navigation safety margins in dynamic scheduling?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Link budgets are affected by prioritizing high-revenue services over navigation safety margins through dynamic scheduling, as it may compromise carrier-to-interference (C/I) ratios, leading to reduced signal quality and potential outages. ETSI and ITU standards provide guidelines for link budget calculations and margins.
