TOPIC: What antenna aperture size trade-offs exist between LEO and GEO satellites when optimizing gain and portability for CMOS-based ground terminals?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
LEO satellites require smaller antenna aperture sizes (~74 cm) for similar gain due to shorter distances, while GEO satellites need larger apertures for comparable performance. CMOS-based ground terminals can optimize gain and portability with smaller aperture sizes, leveraging LEO's lower free-space path loss.
