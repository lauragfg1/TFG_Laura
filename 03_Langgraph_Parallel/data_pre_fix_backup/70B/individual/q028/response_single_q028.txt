TOPIC: How does defocused reflector gain loss impact link budget margins for SNR compared to conventional designs?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Defocused reflector gain loss is 1.52 dB at 0° and 1.70 dB at 3.56°, impacting link budget margins for SNR. Conventional designs suffer more, with up to 6 dB loss, affecting system reliability and performance.
