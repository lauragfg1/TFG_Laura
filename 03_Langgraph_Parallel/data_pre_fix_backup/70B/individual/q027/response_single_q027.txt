TOPIC: What power allocation trade-offs arise from differing scan losses between quasi-TEM and quasi-TE1 modes for multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Differing scan losses between quasi-TEM and quasi-TE1 modes affect power allocation in multi-beam satellite systems. Quasi-TE1 mode typically exhibits lower scan losses, allowing for more efficient power allocation and increased system capacity. This trade-off is crucial in optimizing system performance, considering metrics like EIRP and G/T.
