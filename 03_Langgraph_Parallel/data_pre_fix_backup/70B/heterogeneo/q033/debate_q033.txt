[DLB] In addressing the challenge of rural substation monitoring using satellite technology, a hybrid network combining GEO and LEO satellites presents a balanced approach. GEO satellites offer broad, continuous coverage ideal for vast, remote areas, making them suitable for routine data transmissions that can tolerate higher latency. Conversely, LEO satellites, with their lower orbit, provide reduced latency, which is crucial for real-time critical data such as fault detection. 

The integration of these technologies requires intelligent data prioritization and routing mechanisms to manage different data types based on urgency and transmission requirements. While GEO satellites are less affected by Doppler effects, their signals may face interference over long distances. LEO satellites, though prone to Doppler shifts, offer better signal penetration in obstructed areas.

The implementation of such a hybrid network involves increased complexity and cost, including the need for sophisticated management systems to handle varying signal strengths and latencies. Despite these challenges, the hybrid approach enhances network reliability and performance, offering a robust solution for rural substation monitoring.

4. **Mitigating Physical Obstructions**:
