One possible solution is **Centroid-Based Beamforming (CBB)**, which adjusts the beam direction towards the centroid of all connected aircraft within a certain distance.

**Benefits:**
- **Simplicity:** CBB requires less computational resources compared to dynamic beamforming with adaptive antenna arrays, making it more cost-effective.
- **Robustness:** By directing the beam towards the centroid of connected aircraft, CBB can maintain LOS connectivity even when some aircraft temporarily lose LOS.

**Challenges:**
- **Limited Precision:** CBB may not be as effective in scenarios with a large number of aircraft or rapid changes in their positions, as it relies on the approximation of the centroid.
- **Latency:** Calculating the centroid and adjusting the beam direction may introduce some latency, which could impact real-time communication.
