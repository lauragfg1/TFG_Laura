[DLB_Expert]:
**Technical Insight**

In TDD satellite systems with large RTT, optimizing antenna gain and coverage for delay-sensitive RLNC applications faces significant challenges. Large RTT necessitates frequent transmission rounds, reducing the effective use of feedback and increasing packet drop rates. To mitigate this, designers must carefully balance antenna gain to minimize bit error rates (BER) while ensuring sufficient coverage. However, higher gain can lead to increased interference, reducing overall system capacity. Thus, the cost in terms of performance and capacity must be carefully managed.

**Budget Implications**

- **Performance Cost**: Higher gain reduces BER but increases interference, potentially degrading overall throughput.
- **Capacity Cost**: Frequent transmission rounds due to large RTT limit the number of users each beam can support, reducing total system capacity.

How can we optimize these trade-offs to ensure reliable transmission while maximizing system efficiency?
--------------------------------------------------
