### Beamforming Strategies for Consistent LOS Connectivity in A2A Links

To ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations, several beamforming strategies are under investigation. One approach involves coordinated multi-point (CoMP) and coordinated multipoint (CoMP) techniques, which can enhance reliability and provide a more stable connection. These techniques help in maintaining consistent LOS paths by leveraging multiple paths and reducing the impact of channel variations.

Another strategy is dynamic beamforming, which dynamically adjusts the beamforming weights based on the current channel conditions and flight patterns. This approach ensures that the strongest LOS path is always used, thereby improving the reliability and stability of the communication links. Additionally, techniques like adaptive beamforming can be employed to optimize the beamforming weights in real-time based on the channel statistics, further enhancing performance.

To achieve consistent LOS connectivity, it is crucial to consider the impact of spatial non-stationary characteristics, such as visibility regions where channel energy is significant. This requires a detailed understanding of the channel model and the implementation of dynamic beamforming schemes that can adapt to these changes.

In summary, beamforming strategies that can dynamically adjust weights, adapt to channel variations, and optimize for spatial non-stationary characteristics are essential for ensuring consistent LOS connectivity in A2A links in dynamic flight formations.
