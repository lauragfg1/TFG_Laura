One approach could be adopting frequency reuse factors, which determine the number of non-overlapping frequency channels available for use by multiple satellites. However, these techniques may introduce additional complexities and require careful consideration of hardware impairments and channel estimation errors.

The optimal solution would likely involve a combination of these strategies, taking into account the specific requirements and characteristics of the multi-satellite network, such as orbital constraints, spacecraft size and power, and propagation effects. It is essential to strike a balance between interference mitigation and maximizing coverage and data rate within the available 14.6% bandwidth.
