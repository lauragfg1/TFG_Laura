   - **Limitations:**
     - Increases thermal loads due to continuous operation of transponders.

2. **Perceptive Scheduling:**
   - **Advantages:**
     - Considers both channel capacity and backhaul frame size, optimizing resource allocation.
     - Reduces unnecessary power consumption by dynamically adjusting transponder usage.
   - **Limitations:**
     - More complex implementation and may introduce latency in scheduling decisions.
