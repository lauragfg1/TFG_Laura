[PNM] MPLS and IPsec VPNs differ in their approaches to congestion mitigation for real-time QoS in multi-utility backbones. MPLS, with RSVP-TE, calculates a shortest path with sufficient bandwidth, blocks resources along the calculated path, detects link status, and allows applications to signal required capacity, providing detailed admission control. In contrast, IPsec VPNs rely on IP routing for path selection in the backhaul segment, assuming a sufficiently provisioned network, focusing mainly on QoS guarantees by managing available capacity without considering high fixed latencies negatively impacting QoS.
