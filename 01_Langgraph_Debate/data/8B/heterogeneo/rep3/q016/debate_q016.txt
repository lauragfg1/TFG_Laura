[DLB] To optimize ground station visibility during short service windows in LEO constellations, consider the array factor to maximize directivity. For instance, using a phased array antenna can reduce interference from co-orbiting satellites by precisely steering beams. The spacing between antennas should be minimized to enhance spatial efficiency but must balance with cost; too small spacing increases manufacturing complexity and potential cross-talk. The trade-off is crucial as closer spacing reduces the effective area for user coverage, impacting capacity and performance.

3. Minimize interference from neighboring satellites and other sources by optimizing frequency reuse and spatial isolation.
4. Leverage advanced signal processing techniques, such as MIMO (Multiple Input Multiple Output), to improve link reliability and capacity.
5. Consider using phased array antennas for their agility and ability to electronically steer the beam towards the satellite.
6. Incorporate prediction algorithms to anticipate satellite movement and adjust the antenna's orientation accordingly.
7. Optimize power allocation between satellites based on their proximity to the ground station, ensuring maximum coverage during service windows.
