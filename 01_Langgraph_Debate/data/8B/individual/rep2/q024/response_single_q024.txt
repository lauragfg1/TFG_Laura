TOPIC: What are the trade-offs in using additive manufacturing for CTS array fabrication versus traditional methods in terms of deployment cost and scalability?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Additive manufacturing (AM) for CTS array fabrication offers advantages in terms of design freedom, compactness, mass reduction, and cost savings. However, it introduces surface roughness-related losses, which can be evaluated using techniques such as waveguide characterization. Traditional methods, like CNC machining, are more suitable for quasi-optical beamformers but may not offer the same level of complexity and compactness as AM.
