TOPIC: How can frequency planning address interference between satellite uplinks and broadband power line communication systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To address interference between satellite uplinks and broadband power line communication systems, frequency planning can utilize techniques such as orthogonal frequency division multiple access (OFDMA) or cognitive radio to dynamically allocate frequencies. Additionally, employing filters with high selectivity and rejecting out-of-band emissions can help mitigate interference.
