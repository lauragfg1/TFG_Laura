TOPIC: How do on-board storage constraints influence payload power allocation between immediate and time-tagged service prioritization?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
On-board storage constraints influence payload power allocation by prioritizing immediate services, which do not occupy on-board memory, over time-tagged services that require data packet storage. This is due to the limited capacity of on-board memory buffers, as specified in constraint (7) pk[i] ≤ ptotal.
