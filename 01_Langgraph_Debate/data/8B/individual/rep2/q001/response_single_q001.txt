TOPIC: How does interleaved array element spacing affect coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression in Ka-band satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Interleaved array element spacing in Ka-band satellite links affects coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression. As interleaving increases, array gain improves due to reduced side lobes, but at the expense of increased mutual coupling between elements. This leads to a trade-off between beamforming gain and side-lobe level. The optimal interleaved spacing depends on the specific Ka-band frequency band used, as it influences the antenna's radiation pattern and grating lobe characteristics.
