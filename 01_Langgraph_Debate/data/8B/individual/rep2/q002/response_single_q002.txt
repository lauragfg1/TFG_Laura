TOPIC: How does the LPF's 25 GHz stopband impact interference mitigation in multi-beam satellite downlink frequency planning?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The LPF's (Low-Pass Filter) 25 GHz stopband will impact interference mitigation in multi-beam satellite downlink frequency planning by attenuating signals within this range. This can lead to reduced co-channel interference and increased spectral efficiency, but may also limit the usable bandwidth for high-frequency applications.
