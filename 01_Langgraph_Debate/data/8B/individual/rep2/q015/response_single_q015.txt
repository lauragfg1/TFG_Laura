TOPIC: How do distributed satellite constellations impact orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In distributed satellite constellations, orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation are impacted by the need to balance signal strength with interference levels. As constellation density increases, so does the likelihood of interference, which can be mitigated through techniques such as orthogonal resource allocation or beamforming. However, these methods may compromise coverage areas.
