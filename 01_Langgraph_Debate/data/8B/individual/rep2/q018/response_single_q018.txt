TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
When coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows, frequency planning challenges arise due to potential interference between satellites and ground stations operating on the same frequencies. This is particularly concerning for moving terminals (MTs) that can connect to both wireless terrestrial and satellite networks. Tight coordination is required to manage shared spectrum and prevent interference.
