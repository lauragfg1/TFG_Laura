TOPIC: How does the 14.6% bandwidth affect frequency planning for multi-satellite networks with adjacent channel interference risks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Adjacent channel interference risks in multi-satellite networks are mitigated by ensuring sufficient frequency separation between channels. With a 14.6% bandwidth, as mentioned in the Context, we can infer that this is likely referring to the Ku band, which has a standard bandwidth allocation of 500 MHz (12.75-13.25 GHz). To minimize adjacent channel interference, satellites should be spaced by at least half of this bandwidth, or approximately 250 MHz.
