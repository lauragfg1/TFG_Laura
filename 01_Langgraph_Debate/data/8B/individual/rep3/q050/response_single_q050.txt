TOPIC: What beamforming strategies ensure consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamically changing flight formations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For consistent LOS connectivity in A2A links, dynamic beamforming strategies are necessary to adapt to changing flight formations. Adaptive array processing and phase control can be used to steer beams towards the aircraft, ensuring a stable line of sight (LOS) connection. The use of spatial non-stationary channel properties, such as visibility regions, can also inform beamforming decisions.
