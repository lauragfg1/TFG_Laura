TOPIC: How does the 45° scan range affect link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users compared to broadside beams?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 45° scan range affects link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users by introducing additional losses due to increased off-focus illumination. This results in higher Side Lobe Levels (SLLs) and a more pronounced beam squint, leading to reduced signal strength and increased interference. The quasi-TE1 mode excitation exacerbates the issue, causing anisotropy in the Corrugated Periodic Patch Waveguide (CPPW).
