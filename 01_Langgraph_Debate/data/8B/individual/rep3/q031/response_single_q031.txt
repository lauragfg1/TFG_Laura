TOPIC: Can MVDR beamforming sufficiently compensate for pattern degradation in array-fed reflectors during element failures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
MVDR beamforming can partially compensate for pattern degradation in array-fed reflectors during element failures, but it is not sufficient to completely restore the original gain. The performance comparison in Table II shows a significant gain loss in the center at 0° and in the direction of the failed elements.
