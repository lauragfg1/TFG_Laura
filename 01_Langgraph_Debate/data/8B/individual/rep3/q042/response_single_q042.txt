TOPIC: How does the 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna impact link budget calculations for GEO-LEO satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 41% efficiency of the proposed antenna impacts link budget calculations by increasing the effective transmit power. This can be represented as an increase in the antenna gain, which is directly proportional to the square of the efficiency. Assuming a typical satellite communication system, this would result in a 1.4-1.6 dB increase in the carrier-to-noise ratio (CNR) for GEO-LEO links.
