TOPIC: What beam management strategies are needed to mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band, adaptive beamforming techniques are required. The use of high-gain beams and narrower beamwidths in higher frequency bands necessitates reconsideration of system parameters such as beamforming and scheduling granularity. Implementing analog or hybrid beamforming solutions can help reduce the impact of phase noise and improve spectral efficiency.
