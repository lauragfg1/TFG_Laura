TOPIC: What link budget trade-offs exist when balancing data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In adverse weather, satellite backhaul links face high atmospheric attenuation due to gases, rain, and clouds. To balance data rate and reliability, trade-offs exist between transmission power, modulation order, and antenna gain. Increasing transmission power above 50 W can support high modulation orders, but this may not be feasible due to limited satellite power resources. Antenna gain can also be increased, but this may compromise beamwidth and coverage.
