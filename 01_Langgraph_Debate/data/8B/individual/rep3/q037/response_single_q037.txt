TOPIC: How does adaptive modulation impact power margins in satellite transponders for fluctuating rural substation traffic?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Adaptive modulation can significantly impact power margins in satellite transponders for fluctuating rural substation traffic by adjusting the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and minimizing the effects of near-far problem. This technique can be used to optimize transmission power, reducing the risk of saturation and maintaining a stable SNR.
