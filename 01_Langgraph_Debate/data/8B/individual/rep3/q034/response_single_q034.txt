TOPIC: What antenna design considerations minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration, consider using low-loss antenna designs such as microstrip antennas or patch antennas. These designs have lower insertion loss and are less susceptible to electromagnetic interference (EMI). Additionally, employing frequency-hopping spread spectrum techniques can help mitigate noise effects.
