TOPIC: How are link budgets affected when prioritizing high-revenue services versus navigation safety margins in dynamic scheduling?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Link budgets are affected by prioritizing high-revenue services versus navigation safety margins in dynamic scheduling due to changes in resource allocation and utilization. As revenue-generating traffic is prioritized, available capacity for safety-critical applications may decrease, potentially leading to increased latency or jitter. This can be mitigated by implementing admission control mechanisms that ensure sufficient resources are allocated for critical services.
