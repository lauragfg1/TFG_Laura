TOPIC: What are the trade-offs in using additive manufacturing for CTS array fabrication versus traditional methods in terms of deployment cost and scalability?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Additive manufacturing (AM) techniques, such as stereolithography (SLA), offer advantages in fabricating complex CTS arrays with original tridimensional shapes. However, the surface roughness introduced by SLA affects microwave performance and may increase total deployment cost due to potential losses. Scalability is improved, but traditional methods like milling and electroerosion techniques are more suitable for smaller structures.
