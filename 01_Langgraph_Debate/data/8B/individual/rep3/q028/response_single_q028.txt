TOPIC: How does defocused reflector gain loss impact link budget margins for SNR compared to conventional designs?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The defocused reflector gain loss, typically in the order of 6 dB, negatively impacts link budget margins for SNR. This is because the reduced gain leads to increased signal power requirements at the receiver, which can be challenging to meet, especially in scenarios with high noise levels or limited transmit power.
