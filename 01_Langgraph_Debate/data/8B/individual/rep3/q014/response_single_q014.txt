TOPIC: What antenna aperture size trade-offs exist between LEO and GEO satellites when optimizing gain and portability for CMOS-based ground terminals?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For LEO satellites, a larger antenna aperture size is beneficial due to the smaller signal propagation time delay and lower free space path loss. However, portability becomes an issue for CMOS-based ground terminals. In contrast, GEO satellites have fixed positions and wide earth surface coverage but suffer from higher signal propagation delays and larger FSPL. A 74 cm size aperture antenna is used in the context, which might be a compromise between LEO's benefits and GEO's coverage.
