TOPIC: How can satellite link budgets be optimized for high throughput and low latency in global aeronautical coverage?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To optimize satellite link budgets for high throughput and low latency in global aeronautical coverage, consider using advanced modulation schemes like DVB-S2/RCS2 or Multi-Packet Reception (MPR) to increase capacity. Implementing bandwidth-delay routing algorithms, such as Bandwidth Delay Satellite Routing, can help minimize latency. Additionally, optimize satellite constellation deployment strategies by considering uncertain areas of interest and incorporating metrics like capacity, link budget, and routing into the objective function.
