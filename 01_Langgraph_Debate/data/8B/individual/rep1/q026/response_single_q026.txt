TOPIC: How does the 45° scan range affect link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users compared to broadside beams?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 45° scan range affects link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users by increasing signal attenuation due to increased path loss and shadowing. This is because as the antenna scans, its gain decreases, and the signal is more susceptible to interference and absorption. In contrast, broadside beams have optimal gain and are less affected by these factors.
