TOPIC: What orbital configurations justify prioritizing V-band (57-66 GHz) over optical systems for inter-satellite communication?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
V-band (57-66 GHz) is prioritized over optical systems for inter-satellite communication due to its inherent characteristics that make it more suitable for this application. The V-band offers a higher frequency range, which enables the use of smaller and lighter antennas, reducing the complexity and power consumption of the system. Additionally, the V-band has less atmospheric interference compared to other frequency bands, making it a more reliable choice for inter-satellite communication.
