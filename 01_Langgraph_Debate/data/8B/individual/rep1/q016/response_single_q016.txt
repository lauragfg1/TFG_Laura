TOPIC: What antenna design considerations optimize ground station visibility during short service windows in LEO constellations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To optimize ground station visibility during short service windows in LEO constellations, consider designing antennas with high gain and narrow beamwidth to compensate for the satellites' rapid movement. The half-power beam width (HPBW) must be carefully chosen to ensure adequate coverage. A study suggests using a Walker star constellation layout with an inclination of 87.5°.
