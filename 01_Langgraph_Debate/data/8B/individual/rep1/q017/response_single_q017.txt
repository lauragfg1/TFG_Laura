TOPIC: How are link budgets affected when prioritizing high-revenue services versus navigation safety margins in dynamic scheduling?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Link budgets are affected by prioritizing high-revenue services versus navigation safety margins in dynamic scheduling due to changes in resource allocation and utilization. High-priority traffic may occupy available capacity, reducing resources for lower-priority traffic, including navigation safety-critical signals. This can lead to increased latency or packet loss, compromising link reliability and QoS guarantees.
