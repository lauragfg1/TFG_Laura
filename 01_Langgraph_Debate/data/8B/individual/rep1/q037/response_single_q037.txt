TOPIC: How does adaptive modulation impact power margins in satellite transponders for fluctuating rural substation traffic?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Adaptive modulation impacts power margins in satellite transponders by adjusting the transmission power to match the changing channel conditions, thereby maintaining a stable signal-to-noise ratio. For fluctuating rural substation traffic, adaptive modulation can help reduce power consumption and increase overall system efficiency.
