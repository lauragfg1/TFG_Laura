TOPIC: How does the 14.6% bandwidth affect frequency planning for multi-satellite networks with adjacent channel interference risks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 14.6% bandwidth affects frequency planning by increasing the risk of adjacent channel interference in multi-satellite networks. This is because a narrower bandwidth allows less margin for error when allocating frequencies to adjacent satellites, making it more challenging to mitigate interference. As a result, careful frequency planning and coordination are essential to ensure reliable communication.
