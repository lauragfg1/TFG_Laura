TOPIC: How do distributed satellite constellations impact orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Distributed satellite constellations impact orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation by increasing the number of potential communication paths, but also introduce challenges such as increased complexity in time scheduling and potential near-far problems. The optimal orbital configuration must balance these competing factors to achieve maximum channel utilization.
