TOPIC: How would the 0.25 dB passband loss affect link budget margins in low-noise satellite receiver frontends?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 0.25 dB passband loss would reduce link budget margins by approximately 0.25 dB, resulting in a decrease in signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and potentially leading to increased bit error rates or even link failure.
