TOPIC: How does the hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology balance efficiency and compactness in V-band satellite antenna designs?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology balances efficiency and compactness by optimizing the array scale, insertion loss, and radiation efficiency. The use of a novel feeding structure excites the microstrip sub-array with a wideband characteristic, achieving a reflection coefficient below -10 dB within the frequency band from 57 GHz to 66 GHz.
