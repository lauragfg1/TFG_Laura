TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between array size and radiation efficiency for SIW arrays in high-gain inter-satellite antennas?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For SIW arrays in high-gain inter-satellite antennas, there is a trade-off between array size and radiation efficiency. As array size increases, radiation efficiency decreases due to increased insertion loss caused by longer SIW transmission lines. This relationship is observed experimentally, with a 16x16 SIW sub-array exhibiting a total radiation efficiency of 41% compared to 27.5% for a 32x32 array.
