TOPIC: What interference mitigation techniques are viable for overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations, viable interference mitigation techniques include Rate-Splitting Multiple Access (RSMA) and Analog Beamforming. RSMA can provide high throughput while mitigating co-channel interference by splitting the available bandwidth among multiple groups of users. Analog Beamforming can reduce interference by directing the transmitted signal towards specific areas, reducing overlap with other signals.
