TOPIC: What link budget trade-offs exist when balancing data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
When balancing data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links, trade-offs exist due to atmospheric attenuation. High modulation orders require transmission powers above 50W to achieve 99.99% availability. However, high power consumption increases the link budget's cost and complexity. A trade-off between desired system performance, cost, and level of reliability is necessary.
