TOPIC: How do GEO vs LEO satellite orbits affect latency and coverage in hybrid networks for rural substation monitoring?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
GEO satellite orbits introduce large delays due to their stationary nature and distance from Earth's surface, whereas LEO satellites have lower latency and delay requirements. This affects coverage in hybrid networks for rural substation monitoring, with GEO systems potentially experiencing significant signal degradation and attenuation over long distances.
