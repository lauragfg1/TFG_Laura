TOPIC: What power allocation trade-offs arise from differing scan losses between quasi-TEM and quasi-TE1 modes for multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For multi-beam satellite systems, differing scan losses between quasi-TEM and quasi-TE1 modes result in power allocation trade-offs. Quasi-TEM mode typically exhibits lower scan losses, allowing for more efficient power allocation to beams with high demand. Conversely, quasi-TE1 mode experiences higher scan losses, necessitating increased power allocation to compensate for the reduced signal strength.
