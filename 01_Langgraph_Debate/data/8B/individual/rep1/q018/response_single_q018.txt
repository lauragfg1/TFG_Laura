TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows due to in-line interference between satellites and terrestrial networks. This includes mitigating interference between non-geostationary (NGSO) and geostationary (GSO) satellites, as well as between NGSO satellites of different constellations. Proper coordination is required to avoid collisions and ensure efficient use of the available spectrum.
