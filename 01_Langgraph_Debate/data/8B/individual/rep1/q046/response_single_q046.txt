TOPIC: What beam management strategies are needed to mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band, beam management strategies such as dynamic beamforming and adaptive array processing are necessary. These techniques adjust the phase and amplitude of individual antenna elements in real-time to optimize signal strength and minimize interference. Additionally, implementing a narrow beamwidth and using high-gain antennas can help reduce efficiency fluctuations.
