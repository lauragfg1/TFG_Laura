TOPIC: How does the 45° scan range affect link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users compared to broadside beams?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 45° scan range affects link budget calculations for edge-of-coverage users by increasing the path loss due to the increased distance between the satellite and the user terminal. This results in a higher signal attenuation, requiring more power to maintain the same signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) compared to broadside beams.
