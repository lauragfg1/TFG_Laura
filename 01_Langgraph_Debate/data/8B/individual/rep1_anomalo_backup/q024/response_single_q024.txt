TOPIC: What are the trade-offs in using additive manufacturing for CTS array fabrication versus traditional methods in terms of deployment cost and scalability?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Additive manufacturing (AM) for CTS array fabrication offers advantages in design freedom, compactness, and cost reduction. However, it may introduce surface roughness-related losses, affecting microwave performance. Traditional methods, such as milling and electroerosion, are better suited for structures without corrugations on both vertical and horizontal planes. AM's scalability is limited by its resolution and tolerances, but can achieve high precision at Ka band frequencies.
