TOPIC: How can frequency planning address interference between satellite uplinks and broadband power line communication systems?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Frequency planning can address interference between satellite uplinks and broadband power line communication systems by allocating non-overlapping frequency bands. The downlink frequency range for C-band is 3.7-4.2 GHz, which overlaps with the frequency range of broadband power line communication systems (typically in the range of 10-40 MHz). To mitigate this interference, satellite operators can use orthogonal polarizations or divide the frequency band into sub-bands.
