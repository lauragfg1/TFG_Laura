TOPIC: What beam management strategies are needed to mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band, adaptive beamforming techniques such as Dynamic Beam Training (DBT) and Multi-Beam Adaptive Array (MBAA) can be employed. These methods adjust the antenna's radiation pattern in real-time to compensate for changing channel conditions, ensuring a stable and efficient signal transmission.
