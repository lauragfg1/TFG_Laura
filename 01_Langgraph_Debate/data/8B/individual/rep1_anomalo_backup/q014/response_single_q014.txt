TOPIC: What antenna aperture size trade-offs exist between LEO and GEO satellites when optimizing gain and portability for CMOS-based ground terminals?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
For LEO satellites, antenna aperture size can be smaller due to lower free space path loss (fspl) and shorter signal propagation time delay. A typical 74 cm size aperture antenna is used with a 45 dBi gain at 29 GHz in the user terminal (UT). For GEO satellites, larger antennas are required due to higher fspl and longer signal propagation time delay.
