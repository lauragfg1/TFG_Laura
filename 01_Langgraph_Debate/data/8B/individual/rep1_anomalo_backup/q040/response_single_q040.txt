TOPIC: How does the hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology balance efficiency and compactness in V-band satellite antenna designs?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology balances efficiency and compactness by optimizing the array size, reducing insertion loss, and minimizing the length of SIW structures. This is achieved through a novel feeding structure that excites microstrip sub-arrays with wideband characteristics, allowing for high efficiency (up to 41%) and compact configuration.
