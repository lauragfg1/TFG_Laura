TOPIC: What beam allocation strategies optimize spectrum efficiency in hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To optimize spectrum efficiency in hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand, allocate more bandwidth to beams with high traffic density using dynamic resource allocation schemes. This approach ensures efficient use of idle satellite spectrum and minimizes inter-cell interference. Additionally, implement a pricing-based method for spectrum allocation to incentivize participants and enhance market-driven mechanism.
