TOPIC: What antenna design considerations minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
To minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments, PLC systems should employ antenna designs with high gain and directivity. This can be achieved using directional antennas such as Yagi-Uda or patch antennas. Additionally, using a narrow frequency band and implementing techniques like noise reduction filters can help mitigate the effects of high bit error rates due to electromagnetic interference.
