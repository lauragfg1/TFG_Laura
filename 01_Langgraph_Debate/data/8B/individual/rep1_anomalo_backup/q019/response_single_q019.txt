TOPIC: How do on-board storage constraints influence payload power allocation between immediate and time-tagged service prioritization?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
On-board storage constraints influence payload power allocation by limiting the capacity to store data packets for time-tagged services. This constraint prioritizes immediate services, which do not occupy on-board memory, over time-tagged services that require storage. As a result, power allocation is adjusted to accommodate the varying demands of each service type, ensuring efficient use of resources.
