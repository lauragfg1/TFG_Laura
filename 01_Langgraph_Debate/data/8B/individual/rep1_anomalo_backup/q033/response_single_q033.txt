TOPIC: How do GEO vs LEO satellite orbits affect latency and coverage in hybrid networks for rural substation monitoring?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
In hybrid networks for rural substation monitoring, GEO (Geostationary Earth Orbit) satellites impose large delays due to their high altitude and stationary footprint. In contrast, LEO (Low Earth Orbit) mega constellations offer lower delay and pointing requirements, making them more suitable for real-time applications. However, the Context lacks specific data on latency and coverage metrics for both GEO and LEO systems.
