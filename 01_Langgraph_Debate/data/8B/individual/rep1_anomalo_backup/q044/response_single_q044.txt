TOPIC: How does the 14.6% bandwidth affect frequency planning for multi-satellite networks with adjacent channel interference risks?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
The 14.6% bandwidth affects frequency planning by increasing the risk of adjacent channel interference, which can lead to signal degradation and reduced system performance. To mitigate this, a higher guard band may be required between channels to prevent interference. The exact impact depends on the specific satellite constellation and channel allocation policy.
