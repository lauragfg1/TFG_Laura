TOPIC: How does interleaved array element spacing affect coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression in Ka-band satellite links?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Interleaved array element spacing affects coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression in Ka-band satellite links by introducing periodic variations in the antenna radiation pattern. This can lead to reduced side-lobe levels, improved grating lobe suppression, and enhanced beam-forming capabilities. However, excessive interleaving may degrade main-beam gain and increase the occupied bandwidth.
