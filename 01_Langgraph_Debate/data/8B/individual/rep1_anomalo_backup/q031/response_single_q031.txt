ID: 31
TOPIC: Can MVDR beamforming sufficiently compensate for pattern degradation in array-fed reflectors during element failures?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
MVDR beamforming can partially compensate for pattern degradation in array-fed reflectors during element failures, but it may not be sufficient to completely restore the original gain. The defocused reflector concept shows better robustness than conventional reflectors, with a maximum gain loss of 1.52 dB in the zero-degree direction and 1.70 dB at 3.56°.
