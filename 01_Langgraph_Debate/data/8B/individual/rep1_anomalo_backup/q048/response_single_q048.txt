TOPIC: What antenna designs balance aerodynamic constraints with MIMO requirements for high-speed aircraft communication?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Large-scale MIMO systems with hundreds of antennas are suitable for high-speed aircraft communication, as they provide high data rates, spectrum efficiency, energy efficiency, and low latency. Aircraft airframes can accommodate antenna arrays, enabling large-scale MIMO schemes. However, aerodynamic constraints must be balanced with MIMO requirements.
