TOPIC: Could the hybrid feeding structure introduce scalability challenges in dynamic payload power allocation for large-scale arrays?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
Yes, the hybrid feeding structure could introduce scalability challenges in dynamic payload power allocation for large-scale arrays due to increased complexity and potential interference among polarizations. This is because each antenna requires a separate radio frequency (RF) chain, which increases the overall system complexity and power consumption.
