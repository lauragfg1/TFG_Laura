TOPIC: How are link budgets affected when prioritizing high-revenue services versus navigation safety margins in dynamic scheduling?
MODEL: llama3.1:8b
==================================================
When prioritizing high-revenue services versus navigation safety margins in dynamic scheduling, link budgets are affected by increased competition for limited resources. This can lead to reduced bandwidth allocation and potential congestion on critical links, negatively impacting QoS guarantees. To mitigate this, operators must carefully manage traffic engineering and admission control mechanisms to ensure sufficient capacity is allocated to both high-priority services and navigation safety-critical applications.
