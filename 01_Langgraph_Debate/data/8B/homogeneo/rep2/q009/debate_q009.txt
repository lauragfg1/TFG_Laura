The Context highlights the importance of analyzing physical mechanisms for QoS management. Let's focus on array factor, interference, and spacing effects in satellite communication systems.

Array Factor (AF): AF affects signal strength and beamforming efficiency. A higher AF leads to increased signal strength but also increases interference between adjacent beams. This trade-off impacts capacity and spectral efficiency.

Interference: Interference from adjacent beams or terrestrial sources degrades QoS. Spacing effects, such as grating lobes, can exacerbate this issue.

Spacing Effects: Proper spacing between antenna elements is crucial for minimizing grating lobes and ensuring beamforming accuracy.
