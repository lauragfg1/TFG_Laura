[DLB] **Frequency Reuse and Interference Management**

The physical mechanism behind frequency reuse is the **array factor**, which describes how signal power is distributed across the array's aperture. In satellite communication, the array factor is influenced by the spacing between antenna elements, known as the **grating lobe**.

**Impact on Transponder Allocation:**

* Increasing the number of transponders (elements) in the array can lead to **inter-element interference**, reducing overall system performance and capacity.
* Optimizing element spacing to minimize grating lobes can improve frequency reuse efficiency, but may introduce **array factor nulls**, reducing signal power and increasing thermal noise.

**Cost in terms of Performance:**

* Inefficient frequency reuse due to array factor limitations can lead to reduced spectral efficiency, capacity constraints, and increased interference.
* Optimizing element spacing and array design can mitigate these effects, but may require additional resources (e.g., more complex antenna designs) to manage grating lobes and nulls.
