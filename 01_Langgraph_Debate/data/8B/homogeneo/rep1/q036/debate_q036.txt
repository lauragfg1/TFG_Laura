[DLB] **Link Budget Trade-offs:**

In adverse weather conditions, satellite backhaul links face significant challenges due to increased atmospheric attenuation. To balance data rate and reliability, trade-offs exist in terms of link budget:

1. **Increased transmission power**: Higher powers (up to 50 W) are needed to compensate for atmospheric losses, which affects antenna size, weight, and power consumption.
2. **Lower modulation orders**: Reduced modulation orders (e.g., QPSK instead of 16QAM) can improve reliability but reduce data rate efficiency.
3. **Bandwidth allocation**: Allocating more bandwidth to satellite links increases capacity but may lead to congestion in terrestrial networks.

These trade-offs impact system performance, capacity, and interference, highlighting the need for optimized link design and resource allocation strategies.
