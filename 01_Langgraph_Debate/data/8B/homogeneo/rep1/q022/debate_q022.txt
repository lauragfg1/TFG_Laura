[PNM] **Interference Mitigation Techniques for Overlapping Service Transmissions**

Given the high percentage of areas with no reliable 2G signal from any operator, interference mitigation techniques are crucial to maintain high integrity transmission in satellite-terrestrial communication systems.

To address this issue, **Analog Beamforming** can be employed at the ground stations. By dynamically adjusting the antenna patterns, beamforming can reduce cochannel interference and improve signal-to-noise ratio (SNR). This technique is particularly effective in reducing mutual interference between aeronautical communications and terrestrial wireless communications.

Additionally, **Rate Splitting Multiple Access (RSMA)** can be used to mitigate interference by splitting each user's data into two streams: one common stream shared among all users and another private stream for individual users. This approach can improve the capacity of the communication system while reducing cochannel interference.
