2. **Power Minimization**: Adjust power levels for each beam to reduce unnecessary consumption and interference, thereby enhancing spectral efficiency.

3. **Carrier Allocation**: Assign frequencies strategically to minimize interference and maximize throughput, adapting dynamically as conditions change.

- **Interference Management**: Adjacent beams may cause interference, degrading signal quality.
- **Dynamic Spectrum Access**: Requires real-time monitoring and adaptive algorithms for effective spectrum use.
- **Resource Allocation Complexity**: Balancing multiple factors across numerous satellites and beams is complex.

**Potential Solutions:**
- **Advanced Algorithms**: Utilize AI and machine learning for predictive analytics to anticipate traffic patterns and adjust beam parameters proactively.
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: Implement feedback systems to enable immediate adjustments, optimizing beam switching dynamically.
