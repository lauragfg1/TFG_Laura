In rural areas with fluctuating traffic, adaptive modulation's power-saving benefits—such as reducing transmission power during low interference—are negated by IMD. As ACI increases, the system must raise power to maintain performance, diminishing power margin efficiencies. Higher-order modulations exacerbate this due to their sensitivity to nonlinear distortions, forcing trade-offs between spectral efficiency and power margins. Mitigation techniques like digital predistortion or reducing carriers per HPA are needed to preserve adaptive modulation's benefits without degrading performance.
