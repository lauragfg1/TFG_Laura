[MODERATOR] Ensuring consistent LOS connectivity for A2A links in dynamic flight formations is a significant challenge due to rapid fluctuations in frequency-selective fading and Rician distributed fading. [DLB] Dynamic beamforming in A2A links must balance array factor optimization against interference and fading effects. Coordinated Multi-Point (CoMP) beamforming can mitigate Rician fading by spatially diversifying signals, reducing dominant LOS path reliance. Direct-Sequence Spread Spectrum (DSSS) enhances robustness to frequency-selective fading by spreading signal energy across a wider bandwidth, trading off spectral efficiency for reduced ISI. Beam spacing and array geometry directly impact the trade-off between diversity gain and interference suppression, with tighter spacing improving spatial resolution but increasing mutual coupling effects. These strategies optimize link reliability at the cost of increased complexity and resource allocation overhead.
