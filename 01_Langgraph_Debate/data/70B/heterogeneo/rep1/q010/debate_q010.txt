[DLB] The physical mechanism for interference mitigation in multi-beam satellite systems hinges on array factor optimization and spatial filtering. For tunneled traffic, which often requires higher security and isolation, beamforming techniques allocate tighter beam patterns, reducing sidelobe interference and enhancing spatial separation. Non-tunneled traffic, typically requiring lower latency, employs wider beams with adaptive nulling to suppress interference dynamically. The trade-off is reduced capacity for tunneled traffic due to stricter beam constraints, while non-tunneled traffic gains flexibility but risks higher interference susceptibility.
