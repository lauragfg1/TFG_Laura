3. **Doppler Effects and Mobility:**

   - Utilizing beamforming or directional antennas could help isolate signals, reducing interference between A2G and satellite systems.
   - Array factors and interference patterns must be analyzed to optimize antenna configurations for both systems.

5. **Spectrum Allocation Strategies:**
   - Combining time-division with frequency-division techniques might offer a balanced approach, enhancing efficiency without increasing interference.
   - Dynamic power adjustment could further mitigate interference, though it requires sophisticated coordination between terrestrial and satellite networks.
