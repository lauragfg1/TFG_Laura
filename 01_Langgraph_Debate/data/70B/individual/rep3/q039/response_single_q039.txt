TOPIC: How do MPLS and IPsec VPNs differ in mitigating congestion for real-time QoS in multi-telectric utility backbones?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
MPLS provides traffic classification and prioritization, mitigating congestion for real-time QoS. IPsec VPNs ensure secure communication but may not prioritize traffic, potentially impacting QoS.
