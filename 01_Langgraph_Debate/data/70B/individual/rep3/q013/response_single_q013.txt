TOPIC: How does the transceiver's variable gain distribution between RF and baseband impact dynamic range and noise performance trade-offs in different satellite orbital configurations?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
The transceiver's variable gain distribution between RF and baseband affects dynamic range and noise performance trade-offs. Increasing RF gain enhances signal strength but also amplifies noise, while baseband gain adjustment impacts signal-to-noise ratio.
