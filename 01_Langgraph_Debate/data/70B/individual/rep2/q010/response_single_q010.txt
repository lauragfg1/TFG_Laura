TOPIC: How do interference mitigation techniques adapt when handling tunneled versus non-tunneled traffic in multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
Interference mitigation techniques in multi-beam satellite systems adapt by optimizing transmit beamforming and receive beamforming to minimize interference, with considerations for spatial diversity and band reuse. Techniques include analog beamforming and power allocation.
