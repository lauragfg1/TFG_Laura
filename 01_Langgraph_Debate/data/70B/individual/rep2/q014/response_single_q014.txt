TOPIC: What antenna aperture size trade-offs exist between LEO and GEO satellites when optimizing gain and portability for CMOS-based ground terminals?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
LEO satellites have smaller aperture sizes due to lower orbit altitudes, allowing for higher gain and portability in CMOS-based ground terminals. GEO satellites require larger apertures for comparable gain, impacting portability.
