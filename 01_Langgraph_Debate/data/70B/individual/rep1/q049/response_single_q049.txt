TOPIC: How does L-DACS integration impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
L-DACS integration may impact spectrum sharing by introducing new frequency bands, potentially causing interference with existing A2G and satellite links. However, L-DACS1's full-duplex capability and L-DACS2's half-duplex method could mitigate congestion.
