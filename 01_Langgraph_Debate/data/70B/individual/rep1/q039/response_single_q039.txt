TOPIC: How do MPLS and IPsec VPNs differ in mitigating congestion for real-time QoS in multi-telectric utility backbones?
MODEL: llama3.3:70b
==================================================
MPLS provides traffic classification and prioritization, while IPsec VPNs ensure secure communication. MPLS can reduce congestion by prioritizing real-time traffic, whereas IPsec VPNs rely on best-effort service. MPLS is preferred for large-scale networks with strict QoS requirements.
