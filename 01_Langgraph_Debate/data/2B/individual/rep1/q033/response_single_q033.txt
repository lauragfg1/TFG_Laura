TOPIC: How do GEO vs LEO satellite orbits affect latency and coverage in hybrid networks for rural substation monitoring?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
GEO satellites offer limited latency due to their fixed position and high altitude, while LEO satellites have lower latency due to their closer proximity to Earth. However, GEO's larger footprint provides greater coverage for rural areas.  For optimal performance, a hybrid network combining both GEO and LEO is necessary for near-global coverage with reduced latency.
