    "response": "Variable gain distribution between RF and baseband directly impacts dynamic range. Higher gain boosts SNR, reducing noise floor but potentially increasing transmit power requirements for a given signal level. Conversely, lower gain minimizes power consumption but may result in reduced SNR, impacting sensitivity to background noise. This trade-off depends on the specific orbital configuration (e.g., altitude, distance from Earth station) and desired transmission power."
