TOPIC: How would the 0.25 dB passband loss affect link budget margins in low-noise satellite receiver frontends?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
0.25 dB passband loss directly impacts receiver sensitivity and signal reception, potentially reducing link budget margins.  The impact on the receiver front-end is a decrease in SNR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio) which increases noise floor and reduces the achievable data rate.
