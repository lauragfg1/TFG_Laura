TOPIC: How does defocused reflector gain loss impact link budget margins for SNR compared to conventional designs?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Defocused reflector systems suffer a significant gain roll-off towards array borders, resulting in increased link budget margin requirements for SNR compared to conventional designs. The Context states that defocused reflectors experience a 6 dB gain loss due to this effect.
