TOPIC: What beam allocation strategies optimize spectrum efficiency in hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Beam allocation strategies for hybrid satellite-WiMAX networks during peak demand optimize spectrum efficiency by dynamically allocating resources based on real-time traffic demands. This strategy utilizes dynamic spectrum access and sharing, prioritizing beams in hot zones with higher user density, to maximize bandwidth utilization and minimize inter-cell interference.
