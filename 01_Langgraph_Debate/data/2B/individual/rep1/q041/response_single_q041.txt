TOPIC: What trade-offs exist between array size and radiation efficiency for SIW arrays in high-gain inter-satellite antennas?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Increasing array size for SIW subarrays in inter-satellite antennas leads to higher radiation efficiency, but also increases insertion loss due to longer transmission lines.  This tradeoff is evident as the maximum gain of a 64-element antenna exceeds that of a 16-element array, while the overall efficiency decreases with larger arrays.
