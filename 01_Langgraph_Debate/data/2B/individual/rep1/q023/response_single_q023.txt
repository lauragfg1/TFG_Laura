TOPIC: How does the dual-mode operation of the CTS antenna impact frequency reuse and coverage patterns in Ka-band satellite architectures?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
The dual-mode operation of the CTS antenna allows it to radiate horizontally (h) or vertically (v) polarized fields when excited by a quasi-TEM mode or a quasi-E1 mode, respectively. This enables frequency reuse and broader coverage patterns in Ka-band satellite architectures due to its ability to achieve both polarization modes.
