TOPIC: What beam management strategies are needed to mitigate efficiency fluctuations (>3 dB gain variance) across the 57-66 GHz band?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
To mitigate efficiency fluctuations in the 57-66 GHz band, high-gain beams with beamforming techniques are needed.  These techniques can improve spectral efficiency by reducing path loss and maximizing signal transmission to specific locations. However, the impact of rain attenuation on channel gains needs further investigation.
