TOPIC: How do interference mitigation techniques adapt when handling tunneled versus non-tunneled traffic in multi-beam satellite systems?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Interference mitigation techniques in multibeam satellite systems employ frequency band separation and orthogonal polarizations to minimize intercell interference.  This aims to reduce signal degradation and maintain optimal SINR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio). 4 subbands are commonly used, ensuring disjoint frequency bands for adjacent beams.
