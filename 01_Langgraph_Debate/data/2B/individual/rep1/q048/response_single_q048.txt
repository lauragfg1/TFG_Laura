TOPIC: What antenna designs balance aerodynamic constraints with MIMO requirements for high-speed aircraft communication?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Large-scale MIMO antenna systems for aircraft communication must balance aerodynamic constraints with high data rates.  This requires carefully designed antenna arrays that can accommodate hundreds of antennas while minimizing physical size and weight. The antenna design should consider factors like frequency band, modulation techniques, antenna gain, beam width, and interference mitigation. Achieving this balance is crucial for successful implementation in the demanding environment of aircraft communication.
