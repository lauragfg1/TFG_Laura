TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Overlap in satellite visibility windows creates interference challenges due to both NGSO and GSO satellites potentially operating on the same frequency.  This can lead to in-line interference between LEO satellites, especially when beams cross the equator line and operate in the same frequency. Effective coordination is crucial for mitigating this interference.
