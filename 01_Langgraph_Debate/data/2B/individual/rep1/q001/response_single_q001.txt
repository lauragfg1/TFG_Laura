TOPIC: How does interleaved array element spacing affect coverage patterns and grating lobe suppression in Ka-band satellite links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Interleaved array element spacing directly impacts coverage patterns by altering beam directivity and grating lobe suppression.  Increased spacing reduces the antenna's effective aperture, limiting gain, and potentially increasing sidelobe levels. This is particularly relevant in Ka-band links where spot beams offer high throughput but introduce interbeam interference due to non-zero sidelobes.
