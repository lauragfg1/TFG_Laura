TOPIC: What feed element spacing balance minimizes dB loss while maintaining beamforming flexibility in defocused systems?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Defocused reflector systems achieve robustness against feed element failures due to their inherent beam focusing characteristics.  The spacing between elements in such a system is crucial for balancing gain loss and maintaining flexibility in beamforming. This balance depends on the desired trade-off between power efficiency and spectral agility, which are subject to design constraints.
