TOPIC: How does the transceiver's variable gain distribution between RF and baseband impact dynamic range and noise performance trade-offs in different satellite orbital configurations?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Variable gain distribution impacts dynamic range and noise performance through modulation, coding schemes, antenna design, and receiver sensitivity.  The tradeoff is between higher bandwidth for increased data rates and reduced noise floor. This is influenced by the orbital configuration (e.g., Low Earth Orbit) and its impact on signal path loss and atmospheric attenuation.
