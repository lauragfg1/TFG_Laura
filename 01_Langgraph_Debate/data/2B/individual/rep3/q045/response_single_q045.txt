TOPIC: Could the hybrid feeding structure introduce scalability challenges in dynamic payload power allocation for large-scale arrays?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
A hybrid feeding structure, while potentially enhancing scalability, introduces challenges in dynamic payload power allocation for large-scale arrays. The complexity of the satellite payload increases with added antennas and the resulting inter-antenna interference. This necessitates careful planning and design to ensure efficient power allocation and optimal system performance.
