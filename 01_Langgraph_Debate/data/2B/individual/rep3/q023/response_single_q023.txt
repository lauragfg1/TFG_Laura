TOPIC: How does the dual-mode operation of the CTS antenna impact frequency reuse and coverage patterns in Ka-band satellite architectures?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
The dual-mode operation of the CTS antenna allows for horizontal (H) or vertical (V) polarization radiation when excited by quasi-TEM or quasi-TE1 modes, respectively. This enables frequency reuse in Ka-band satellite architectures as it avoids interference between beams, allowing for multiple frequencies to be used within a single beam.
