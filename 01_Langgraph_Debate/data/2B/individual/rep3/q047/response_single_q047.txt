TOPIC: How can satellite link budgets be optimized for high throughput and low latency in global aeronautical coverage?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Satellite link budgets must balance transmit power, receiver sensitivity, and channel bandwidth to maximize throughput.  High-throughput satellite links often require higher power for greater data rates but may face increased latency due to longer distances and signal propagation delays.  Utilizing advanced modulation schemes like DVB-S2X with wider spectral bandwidth can improve throughput while considering link budget constraints.  Low-latency requirements necessitate careful consideration of the impact of fixed latency on overall transmission time, especially in scenarios where high data rates are required.
