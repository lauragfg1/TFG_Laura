TOPIC: How does L-DACS integration impact spectrum sharing between A2G and satellite links in congested airspaces?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
L-DACS integration aims to mitigate spectrum congestion in airspaces by providing dedicated frequency channels for aeronautical communication. The L-band digital aeronautical communication system (L-DACS) operates on specific frequencies, potentially reducing interference with terrestrial networks like cellular.
