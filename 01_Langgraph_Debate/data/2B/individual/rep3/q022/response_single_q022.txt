TOPIC: What interference mitigation techniques are viable for overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Techniques for mitigating interference in overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations include spectrum sharing, frequency reuse, and adaptive allocation of frequencies. Techniques like TDMA (Time Division Multiple Access) can help manage cochannel interference by allocating specific time slots to different users.
