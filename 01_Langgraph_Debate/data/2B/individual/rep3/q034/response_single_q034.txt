TOPIC: What antenna design considerations minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Antenna design considerations for minimizing signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments include using high impedance matching techniques to reduce reflection and distortion.  Additionally, employing directional antennas with appropriate gain can focus transmission and minimize interference from unwanted sources.
