TOPIC: What antenna aperture size trade-offs exist between LEO and GEO satellites when optimizing gain and portability for CMOS-based ground terminals?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
LEO satellites have a smaller aperture size than GEO satellites due to their lower altitude and higher frequency usage. This results in lower antenna gain compared to GEO, but also reduces portability concerns.  The trade-off involves balancing signal strength and ground terminal complexity for optimal performance.
