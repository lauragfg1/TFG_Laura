TOPIC: What link budget trade-offs exist when balancing data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
{
    "response": "Satellite backhaul links in adverse weather, such as rain or clouds, face high atmospheric attenuation. This limits transmission power to achieve desired data rates and reliability.  The link budget must balance higher data rate demands with the need for reliable communication.  This requires trade-offs between power levels and modulation orders, aiming for a minimum of 50W transmit power to support high modulation orders."
