TOPIC: How does the hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology balance efficiency and compactness in V-band satellite antenna designs?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
The hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology optimizes antenna efficiency and compactness by combining the benefits of both structures. This approach utilizes microstrip patches, substrate integrated waveguide (SIW), and waveguide power dividers to achieve a wideband characteristic with reduced insertion loss. The resulting design balances performance requirements for high gain and low cost in compact configurations.
