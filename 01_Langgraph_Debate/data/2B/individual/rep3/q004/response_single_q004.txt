TOPIC: How would the 0.25 dB passband loss affect link budget margins in low-noise satellite receiver frontends?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
A 0.25 dB passband loss in the receiver frontends will reduce the overall link budget margin. This reduction in signal strength is directly proportional to the loss, impacting transmit power and receiver sensitivity. The receiver must compensate for this loss through adjustments to its gain settings.
