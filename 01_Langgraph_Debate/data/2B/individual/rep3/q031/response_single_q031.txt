TOPIC: Can MVDR beamforming sufficiently compensate for pattern degradation in array-fed reflectors during element failures?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
MVDR beamforming can partially compensate for pattern degradation in array-fed reflectors during element failures.  The provided data shows that MVDR beamforming can reduce gain loss, but a certain degree of degradation remains.  However, the degree of compensation depends on the specific failure scenario and the design of the reflector.
