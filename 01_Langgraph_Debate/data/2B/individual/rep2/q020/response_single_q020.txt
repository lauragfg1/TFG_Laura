TOPIC: What beam switching strategies minimize setup times while maintaining spectrum efficiency for multi-satellite ground station operations?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Beam switching strategies minimizing setup times while maintaining spectrum efficiency for multi-satellite ground station operations likely rely on adaptive beam selection based on signal strength and interference levels.  This approach, often called dynamic beamforming, can be implemented using techniques like time division multiplexing (TDM) or orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM).
