TOPIC: What antenna design considerations minimize signal attenuation in high-noise power line environments for reliable PLC integration?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Antenna design considerations for minimizing signal attenuation in high-noise power lines focus on reducing impedance mismatch and maximizing transmission efficiency. Employing low-profile, resonant antennas with narrow bandwidths and optimized impedance matching circuits can effectively mitigate interference from fluctuating power line noise.
