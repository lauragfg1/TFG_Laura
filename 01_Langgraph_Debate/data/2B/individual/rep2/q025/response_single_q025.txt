TOPIC: How does the 11.6% bandwidth of the CTS antenna influence frequency planning and spectral efficiency for broadband satellite links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
The CTS antenna's 11.6% bandwidth directly influences frequency planning and spectral efficiency. This relatively high percentage limits the available bandwidth for each channel, requiring more frequent channel reassignments to maintain a stable connection.  This necessitates careful consideration of frequency allocation strategies to maximize spectral efficiency while ensuring reliable data transmission.
