TOPIC: How does the hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology balance efficiency and compactness in V-band satellite antenna designs?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
The hybrid SIW-waveguide feeding topology optimizes for high efficiency and compactness in V-band satellite antenna designs. It achieves this by combining the advantages of both structures: the SIW's ability to handle wide bandwidth with the waveguide's compact size. This combination reduces insertion loss, leading to improved overall efficiency while maintaining a relatively small form factor.
