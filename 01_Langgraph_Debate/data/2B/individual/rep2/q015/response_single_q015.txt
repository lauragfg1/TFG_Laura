TOPIC: How do distributed satellite constellations impact orbital configuration trade-offs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Distributed satellite constellations necessitate tradeoffs between coverage and inter-satellite interference mitigation. LDMA's distributed topology maximizes channel utilization but can increase contention, impacting scalability.  Centralized topologies like Half duplex CDMA mitigate interference but come with increased latency and limited throughput.
