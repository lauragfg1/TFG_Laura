TOPIC: How does transponder allocation balance network efficiency against thermal/power constraints during peak service demand periods?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Transponder allocation aims to balance network efficiency and power constraints by optimizing resource utilization.  Dynamic allocation approaches, such as those using pricing or bandwidth control, are employed to manage traffic demand during peak service periods. This ensures efficient use of available bandwidth and minimizes thermal/power limitations.
