TOPIC: What interference mitigation techniques are viable for overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Techniques for mitigating interference in overlapping service transmissions include frequency reuse, channel allocation schemes like TDMA (Time Division Multiple Access), and adaptive frequency allocation based on varying cell densities. These methods can help reduce signal overlap and cochannel interference.
