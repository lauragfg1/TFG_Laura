TOPIC: How does the transceiver's variable gain distribution between RF and baseband impact dynamic range and noise performance trade-offs in different satellite orbital configurations?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Variable gain distribution in transceivers impacts dynamic range and noise performance through its influence on RF power output and receiver sensitivity.  Higher gain at the RF level increases signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), but also introduces more power loss, potentially reducing overall system efficiency. Conversely, increased receiver sensitivity minimizes noise floor but can limit SNR if the input signal is weak.
