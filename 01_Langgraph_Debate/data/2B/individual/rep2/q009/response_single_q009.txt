TOPIC: What payload transponder allocation strategies balance spectral efficiency with dynamic QoS demands from ML traffic classification?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Spectral efficiency and dynamic QoS demands for ML traffic classification require a strategy balancing payload transponder allocation. This can be achieved by employing dynamic allocation based on real-time QoS requirements, using techniques like ECMP or RSVP-TE.  These methods allocate resources dynamically to specific traffic flows based on their QoS demands, optimizing spectral efficiency while ensuring reliable delivery.
