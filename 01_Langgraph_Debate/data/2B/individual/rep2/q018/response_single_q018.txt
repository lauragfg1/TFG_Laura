TOPIC: What frequency planning challenges arise when coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Coordinating multiple ground stations with overlapping satellite visibility windows presents challenges related to interference mitigation.  In-line interference, particularly between NGSO and GSO satellites crossing the equator line or between neighboring NGSO satellites with intersecting beams in the same frequency band, is a significant concern. This necessitates careful planning and coordination of ground station deployments to minimize interference.
