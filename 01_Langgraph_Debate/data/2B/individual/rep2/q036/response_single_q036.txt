TOPIC: What link budget trade-offs exist when balancing data rate and reliability in adverse weather for satellite backhaul links?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
Satellite backhaul link budget trade-offs require balancing data rates (e.g., 150 Gbps) with reliability in adverse weather, such as rain and clouds. Atmospheric attenuation at W band (71-76 GHz for downlink, 81-86 GHz for uplink) necessitates high transmission power (above 50W) to overcome propagation challenges. Link budget calculations must account for atmospheric attenuation, link loss, and signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) to ensure reliable communication.
