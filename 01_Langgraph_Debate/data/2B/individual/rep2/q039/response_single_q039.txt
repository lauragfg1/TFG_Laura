TOPIC: How do MPLS and IPsec VPNs differ in mitigating congestion for real-time QoS in multi-telectric utility backbones?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
MPLS and IPsec VPNs differ in their approach to congestion mitigation for real-time QoS. MPLS uses label switching paths (LSP) to create independent paths, while IPsec VPNs rely on traffic prioritization based on DSCP field for prioritized data transmission.
