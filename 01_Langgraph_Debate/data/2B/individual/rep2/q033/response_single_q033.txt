TOPIC: How do GEO vs LEO satellite orbits affect latency and coverage in hybrid networks for rural substation monitoring?
MODEL: gemma2:2b
==================================================
GEO satellites offer large stationary footprints, leading to higher latency due to longer propagation delays. LEO satellites have lower latency (less than 1 second) and smaller footprints, but their coverage is less extensive. Hybrid networks leveraging both GEO and LEO offer global coverage with varying latency depending on location and network configuration.
