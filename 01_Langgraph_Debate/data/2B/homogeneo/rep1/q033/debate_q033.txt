[DLB] ## GEO vs LEO: A Trade-Off for Rural Substation Monitoring

**LEO:** Signal propagation in LEO is heavily influenced by antenna array factor (AF) due to the orbital plane's proximity to Earth. This necessitates careful design and placement for optimal coverage. The increased density of LEO satellites can lead to significant interference, requiring sophisticated signal processing and adaptive modulation techniques.
