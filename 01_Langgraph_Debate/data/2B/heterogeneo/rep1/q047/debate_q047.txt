To optimize satellite link budgets for high throughput and low latency, we need to implement path selection mechanisms that can mitigate the impact of fixed latencies. This requires a mechanism that considers both bandwidth blocking and latency characteristics.

**Key Considerations:**

1. **Bandwidth Blocking**: Ensure paths do not block sufficient capacity.
2. **Latency Mitigation**: Minimize the impact of high latency on QoS guarantees by selecting low-latency routes or using alternative methods like ECMP with optimized delay routing to distribute traffic across multiple links without congestion.

**Optimized Path Selection Mechanism:**

- **ECMP + Delay Routing**: Use Equal-Cost Multipath (ECMP) for distributing traffic and optimize the order of packets based on their latency. This ensures low-latency paths are prioritized.
  
- **Dynamic Link Weighting**: Adjust link weights dynamically to balance between bandwidth and latency, ensuring optimal path selection.

**Conclusion:**

By implementing ECMP with optimized delay routing, we can achieve high throughput while mitigating the fixed latencies inherent in satellite links. This approach leverages existing technologies like ECMP and bandwidth delay routing but optimizes them for satellite network scenarios where link characteristics are more complex due to variable latency.
