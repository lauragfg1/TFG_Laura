[DLB] ### Frequency Planning Strategies

To optimize bandwidth allocation for minimizing adjacent channel interference (ACI) in multi-satellite networks, consider implementing **frequency reuse strategies**:

- **Frequency Reuse Patterns**: Use frequency reuse patterns such as **block fading**, where the same set of frequencies is reused across multiple satellites. This reduces ACI by spreading out interfering signals over a larger area.

- **Spacing Effects**: Ensure that adjacent channels are at least 10 MHz apart to minimize cross-channel interference. The spacing effect can be modeled using statistical distributions, such as Rayleigh or Rician distribution, which describe the probability of signal overlap between neighboring frequencies.

### Modeling Techniques

To effectively capture ACI in multi-satellite networks:

- **Channel Models**: Utilize channel models that include both direct and induced path loss, such as the **Rogers model**. This considers factors like atmospheric conditions (rain attenuation) and rain shadow effects on signal strength.

- **Monte Carlo Simulations**: Perform Monte Carlo simulations to evaluate different scenarios under various environmental conditions. For example, simulate paths through urban, suburban, or rural environments with varying degrees of interference mitigation to understand the impact on coverage probability and data rates.

### Performance Analysis

Different ACI mitigation strategies significantly affect network performance metrics:

- **Path Loss Exponent**: Higher path loss exponents increase the likelihood of interference between adjacent channels. Optimize by choosing lower values for higher frequencies like Ka bands, where direct line-of-sight propagation is more likely to be affected by ACI.

- **Satellite Geometry and Positioning**: The geometry of satellites and their positions relative to each other can influence how much ACI affects coverage probability and data rates. For instance, in urban environments, the presence of tall buildings can cause significant ACI due to reflected signals.

In summary, optimizing frequency reuse patterns, ensuring appropriate channel spacing, and using accurate modeling techniques are crucial for minimizing ACI risks while maximizing network performance metrics like coverage probability and data rate.
