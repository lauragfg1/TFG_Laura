5. **Dynamic Scheduling Algorithms**: Effective scheduling algorithms can help balance resource allocation across different applications, ensuring that essential safety margins are maintained while maximizing revenue-generating traffic.

6. **Admission Control and Link Management**: Proper admission control mechanisms prevent overloading of critical links, which could otherwise degrade the service quality for high-revenue services or disrupt navigation systems.

By carefully considering these factors within the link budget framework, network operators can strike a balance that maximizes performance while ensuring the safety margins required for essential applications.
