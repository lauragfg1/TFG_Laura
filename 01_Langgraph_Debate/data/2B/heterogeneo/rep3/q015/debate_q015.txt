   - Techniques like TDM and frequency hopping help manage interference by allocating different frequencies or time slots, improving signal separation.
   - Advanced modulation methods ensure orthogonal transmission, minimizing cross-talk with other satellites.

4. **Inter-satellite Interference**:
   - Strategies such as space-time modulation reduce interference by ensuring each satellite's data is transmitted in a distinct manner, independent of others.

5. **Orbital Altitudes and Orbits**:
   - Satellites at higher altitudes have less interference due to greater distance from Earth, potentially reducing the need for complex configurations.

6. **Network Topology**:
   - Distributed systems can leverage network topology advantages, such as reduced overhead and better load balancing, optimizing coverage and interference management.

In summary, distributed satellite constellations balance coverage with reduced interference by allowing each satellite to manage its area independently. This flexibility enhances performance in dynamic environments while optimizing orbital configurations for efficiency and reliability.
