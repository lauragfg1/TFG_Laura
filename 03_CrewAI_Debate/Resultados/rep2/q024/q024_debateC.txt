1. **Surface Roughness Minimization**: Post-processing treatments like polishing can significantly reduce surface roughness, improving the array factor and maintaining directivity and gain. This is crucial for high-performance CTS arrays in densely packed configurations.

2. **Element Placement and Interference Management**: Strategically placing elements can mitigate interference. For example, using staggered or offset arrangements can reduce mutual coupling and improve overall array performance.

3. **Dense Packing Optimization**: Densely packing elements in less critical areas can enhance efficiency without compromising the array factor. This approach balances cost and design flexibility, allowing for more compact and cost-effective array designs.

4. **Trade-offs Between Surface Roughness and Performance**: Reducing surface roughness through AM can come at the cost of increased complexity and higher initial costs. Designers must carefully balance these factors to ensure that the array factor and link budget efficiency are maintained.
