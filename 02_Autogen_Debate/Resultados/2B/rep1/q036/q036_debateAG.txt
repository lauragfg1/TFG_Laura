Adverse weather conditions, such as storms, rain, and fog, degrade signal quality, reduce link capacity, and increase latency. The link budget must balance data rate, reliability, latency, and link distance to ensure consistent performance.

### 2. Modulation Schemes
- **QAM (Quadrature Amplitude Modulation):** Offers flexibility in data rate but is more susceptible to signal degradation. In adverse conditions, switching to lower modulation orders can maintain high data rates while mitigating signal degradation.
- **OFDM (Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing):** Robust against multipath fading and noise, but longer distances can reduce available bandwidth. It is suitable for applications requiring high data rates.
- **PSK (Pulse-Width Modulation):** Provides a balance between data rate and reliability, with varying performance across different weather conditions.

### 3. Link Distance and Modulation Order Balance
- **Shorter Links:** Provide better performance due to reduced signal propagation delay and attenuation.
- **Longer Links:** Increase link capacity and availability but come with higher latency and reduced data rates.
- **Adaptive Modulation:** Adjusting modulation order based on current conditions can maintain high data rates while mitigating signal degradation
