 Antenna design considerations for reliable PLC integration in high-noise power line environments should focus on minimizing signal attenuation and improving signal-to-noise ratio.

1. Frequency selection: Utilize frequency bands with lower noise levels and less interference from other sources.

3. Antenna placement: Position antennas away from high-noise sources such as electrical motors, power supplies, and fluorescent lights.
4. Directive antennas: Use directional antennas to focus the transmitted signal towards the intended receiver, reducing interference from other power lines and noise sources.
5. Shielding: Implement shielding around the antenna to minimize electromagnetic interference from the power lines and other sources.
6. Adaptive filtering: Implement adaptive filtering techniques to reduce the impact of noise on the received signal.
7. Diversity techniques: Use multiple antennas with different polarizations or locations to improve signal quality and reliability.
8. Power line filtering: Implement power line filters to reduce the noise levels within the power lines, improving the overall signal quality.
9. Antenna impedance matching: Ensure proper impedance matching between the antenna and the power line to minimize signal loss due to mismatching.
10. Regulatory compliance: Adhere to any relevant regulations regarding the transmitted energy and frequencies employed for PLC to prevent interference with other radio signals.
