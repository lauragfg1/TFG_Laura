   - **Signal Processing**: Circular arrays offer uniform sensitivity in all directions, reducing grating lobes and providing more uniform coverage.
   - **Interference**: They are less prone to grating lobes and can handle interference better, making them suitable for environments with multiple sources.
   - **Classification Accuracy**: The uniform coverage and reduced interference can improve the reliability of signal detection and classification, especially in complex heterogeneous environments.
