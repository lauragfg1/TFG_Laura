3. **Substrate and Power Divider Design**: To mitigate the increased insertion loss, the substrate material and power divider design must be carefully optimized. 4. **Beam Steering Mechanism**: The beam steering mechanism itself can also introduce additional losses and complexity. For example, phased array techniques require precise control of the phase shifters, which can add to the overall system complexity and potential for additional insertion loss.

5. **Simulation and Validation**: Precise simulations and experimental validation are crucial to ensure that the increased array size and beam steering mechanisms do not compromise the radiation efficiency and overall performance.
