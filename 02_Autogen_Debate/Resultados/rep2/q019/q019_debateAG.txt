2. **Load Balancing (LB) Scheduling**:
   - **Impact**: LB algorithms distribute the load across the available resources to balance the power consumption. This can be effective in managing both immediate and time-tagged services by ensuring that no single service overloads the storage buffer.
   - **Example**: A satellite with a busy period for immediate services can use LB to allocate power from less busy periods to process time-tagged services, maintaining overall system efficiency.

3. **Perceptive Scheduling**:
   - **Impact**: This algorithm takes into account the current channel conditions and service requirements. It can dynamically adjust power allocation based on real-time data, optimizing both immediate and time-tagged services.
   - **Example**: During periods of high immediate service demand, perceptive scheduling can reduce power to time-tagged services and vice versa, ensuring that critical services are prioritized while still processing stored data.
