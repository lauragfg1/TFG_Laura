Interference cancellation techniques, such as successive interference cancellation (SIC) and dirty paper coding (DPC), can be used to remove the interference from the received signal before decoding.

To address these challenges, techniques such as dynamic spectrum access, cognitive radio networks, and power control algorithms can be employed. Dynamic spectrum access allows for efficient and flexible use of the available spectrum, while cognitive radio networks enable adaptive operation based on the current network conditions. Power control algorithms can help manage the transmit power to maintain a suitable SINR while minimizing power consumption.

In summary, to mitigate interference in overlapping service transmissions across geographically dispersed ground stations, techniques such as analog beamforming, digital beamforming, and interference cancellation can be used.
