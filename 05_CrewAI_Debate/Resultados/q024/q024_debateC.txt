This can reduce the effective gain and bandwidth of the CTS array. Additionally, while AM offers design freedom and cost savings, the inherent limitations in surface finish and mechanical tolerances can lead to higher deployment costs due to the need for post-processing steps like copper plating. Traditional methods, while more precise, are less flexible in terms of complex design integration but offer better control over inductance and capacitance, enhancing the overall performance and scalability of the array. The choice between these methods ultimately depends on the specific requirements of the application, balancing performance, cost, and design complexity.
